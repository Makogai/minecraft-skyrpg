Every [danger region](danger-regions) has a **heart** at its centre: a ruined shrine with an altar. A boss sleeps there. You can wake it with a **Heart Sigil**, or wait for a [Blood Moon](blood-moon) to wake it for you. Defeating one pays out huge coin rewards, a trophy, essences, Blood Shards and a good chance of a Dread Crystal.

## The shrine

The shrine is built when the region's chunks generate (only in newly generated chunks) and reaches 8 blocks from the centre:

- A round platform of the region's own stone, with a checkerboard of lighter blocks at the middle.
- A low, ruined wall around the edge, open at the four ways in.
- Four pillars, five blocks high, each topped with a light.
- An altar in the middle: a 3x3 step, a plinth and the **heart block** on top.

Anything above the platform is cleared, so trees and slopes do not bury it. The boss appears 3.5 blocks east of the altar.

| Region | Floor | Heart block | Light |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Blightlands | polished blackstone bricks | blight crystal | soul lantern |
| The Ashen Wastes | smooth basalt | magma block | lantern |
| Frostgrave | calcite | blue ice | sea lantern |
| The Mire | mud bricks | verdant froglight | lantern |
| The Haunted Moors | stone bricks | crying obsidian | soul lantern |
| The Shattered Highlands | tuff bricks | amethyst block | lantern |

## Waking a boss

There are two ways:

1. **A Heart Sigil.** Stand within **24 blocks** of the altar and use the sigil. It is consumed when the boss wakes.
2. **A Blood Moon.** When one is up, the first player to come within 64 blocks of a heart wakes it. Each heart wakes once per moon.

Each region's sigil only works in its own region. In the wrong place it says "The sigil is cold". Too far from the heart, and it points a waypoint at the altar and tells you how many blocks away it is. If the heart is already awake, it says so. Several regions can have a boss up at once.

### Heart Sigils

Each sigil is crafted at the Artisan Bench in the **region** category. It needs Combat level 10 and costs **32 of the region's essence and 4 Blood Shards**.

## The six bosses

| Boss | Region | Level | Health | Body |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Blight Lord | The Blightlands | 45 | 22,000 | giant zombie in netherite |
| The Ash Colossus | The Ashen Wastes | 50 | 30,000 | giant iron golem |
| The Frost Revenant King | Frostgrave | 50 | 28,000 | giant stray with a crown |
| The Bog Mother | The Mire | 36 | 16,000 | giant witch |
| The Lantern Wraith | The Haunted Moors | 42 | 18,000 | giant vex with a lantern |
| The Storm Griffin | The Shattered Highlands | 55 | 34,000 | a griffin at 2.5 times size |

**Every extra player** near the boss when it wakes (within 32 blocks) adds 50% of its base health. Each boss has a boss bar, two phase changes at 66% and 33% health, and enrages below 33%.

Moves repeat on a timer. Times are in seconds. "Below" means the move only starts once the boss has dropped under that health.

## Rewards

Everyone who fought the boss shares in the coins and Combat XP.

| The Blight Lord | 9,000 to 13,000 | 15,000 coins, 5,000 Combat XP | {{item:skyrpg_world:blighted_heart}} |
| The Ash Colossus | 11,000 to 15,000 | 18,000 coins, 6,000 Combat XP | {{item:skyrpg_world:cinder_core}} |
| The Frost Revenant King | 11,000 to 15,000 | 18,000 coins, 6,000 Combat XP | {{item:skyrpg_world:frozen_crown}} |
| The Bog Mother | 7,000 to 10,000 | 12,000 coins, 4,000 Combat XP | {{item:skyrpg_world:bog_mothers_eye}} |
| The Lantern Wraith | 8,000 to 11,000 | 14,000 coins, 5,000 Combat XP | {{item:skyrpg_world:wraith_lantern}} |
| The Storm Griffin | 14,000 to 19,000 | 22,000 coins, 7,000 Combat XP | {{item:skyrpg_world:stormfeather}} |

Loot from every boss: one trophy, **14 to 24** of the region's essence, **3 to 6** {{item:skyrpg_world:blood_shard}} and a **50%** chance of a {{item:skyrpg_world:dread_crystal}}. Trophies sell for 14,000 to 30,000 coins.

Kills count as region boss kills for the Journal and your SkyRPG Level.

## Staying alive

The boss stays within 28 blocks of the heart and returns if it strays. If nobody is nearby for 30 seconds, the boss vanishes and the heart can be woken again. Because the monsters of the region are still roaming and levelled up, the area is dangerous even before the boss wakes.
