**Brainrots** are fifteen silly creatures that turn up on the surface of the survival world: Tung Tung Tung Sahur with his bat, Tralalero Tralala in his sneakers, Bombardiro Crocodilo circling overhead, and twelve more. Walk up to one and it shouts its own name. **Right-click it to catch it**, and it goes into your **album**, where you can look at it and hear it again whenever you like.

![All fifteen brainrots together](brainrots-lineup.png)

## Finding them

- They appear **on the surface of the survival world**, 20 to 44 blocks from you, never in Skyhaven, caves or other dimensions.
- About **one every two minutes** while you're out and about, but never more than two near you at once. They don't stay long: after **8 minutes** (or once you go far away) a brainrot vanishes in a puff.
- Some like certain places (see the table) and are much more common there: beach brainrots on beaches, desert ones in deserts. **Cappuccino Assassino** mostly comes out at night, and **La Vaca Saturno Saturnita** comes out **only at night**.
- When one is within **10 blocks** it says its name, with music notes over its head, so listen out.

They move in their own ways: most walk, some hop, **Ballerina Cappuccina** spins, **La Vaca Saturno Saturnita** floats just off the ground, and the two fliers (**Bombardiro Crocodilo** and **Bombombini Gusini**) circle high above you. Fliers **swoop down to head height every half a minute or so** (sooner while you're close) and fly slowly for a few seconds: that's when you catch them.

## Catching

**Right-click** a brainrot to catch it. It shouts its name one last time and vanishes in a puff, and you get coins:

| Rarity | First catch | Every catch after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 25 coins | 2 coins |
| Uncommon | 75 coins | 7 coins |
| Rare | 200 coins | 20 coins |
| Epic | 500 coins | 50 coins |
| Legendary | 1,500 coins | 150 coins |

Catching one for the first time shows **NEW BRAINROT!** on your screen. Brainrots can't be hurt (hitting one just makes it jump), and whoever right-clicks first gets it. Catch all fifteen and you're a **Brainrot Master**: the whole server hears about it.

## The album

Press **J** (or open the [K menu](k-menu) and pick **Brainrots**) to open your album.

![The brainrot album](brainrots-album.png)

- A card for each brainrot, coloured by rarity. Ones you've caught show their 3D model (it turns to follow your mouse) and how many times you've caught it. Ones you haven't are a **?** with their rarity.
- **Click a card** to see it up close on the right, with its description, where it's found and when you first caught it. Clicking a caught one plays its voice, and **▶ Hear it** plays it again.
- The bar at the top shows how many of the fifteen you have.

Your album is saved with your character and stays with you when you die. J can be changed in Controls, under **SkyRPG**.
