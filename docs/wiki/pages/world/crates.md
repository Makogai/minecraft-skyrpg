**Crates** drop from monsters in the survival world. Open one with its key and a long reel of prizes spins past a gold marker, ticking as it goes, slows down and stops on what you win, from blue (common) up to the gold ★ prizes. The first crate is the **Hallow's Crate**, for Halloween: it only drops until **November 8**.

## Getting crates

A {{item:skyrpg_crates:hallows_crate}} can drop from any monster you kill in the survival world, the Nether or the End:

| When | Chance per kill |
| --- | --- |
| By day | 0.25% |
| At night, and always in the Nether and the End | 1% |
| During a [Blood Moon](blood-moon) | 3% |
| An elite | 10% |
| A boss (dungeon, world or region) | 25% |

Magic Find makes it more likely. Monsters from spawners never drop crates. A crate drop shows a **RARE DROP!** message.

## Keys

Each crate needs a {{item:skyrpg_crates:hallows_key}} to open. **Odessa Quill** sells them at Quill's Curiosities by the Grand Bazaar for **2,500 coins** each (see [Selling and shops](selling-and-shops#quill-s-curiosities)). Your **first crate comes with a free key**, so everyone gets to spin once.

## Opening a crate

Right-click a crate (or a key) to see everything inside, with the chance of each tier. Press **Open Crate** (or Enter): it uses one crate and one key, and the reel spins for about 7 seconds. You get the prize when the reel stops (even if you close the screen or log off). **Open Another** spins again straight away.

The rarer tiers get a bigger fanfare, and **Cursed** and better prizes are announced to everyone on the server. Gold ★ prizes stay hidden on the reel as **★ Rare Special Item** until one lands.

## What's inside

| Tier | Chance | Prizes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Treat** (blue) | 64% | 8 {{item:skyrpg_crates:candy_corn}}, 4 {{item:skyrpg_crates:toffee_apple}}, a Pumpkin Pouch with 1,000 coins, or (half as likely) one with 2,500 |
| **Trick** (purple) | 25% | One piece of the **Hallowed Harvest** set |
| **Cursed** (pink) | 8% | {{item:skyrpg_items:reapers_scythe}}, or the **Jack o'Lantern** pet (Epic) |
| **Haunted** (red) | 2.5% | The **full Hallowed Harvest set**, all four pieces |
| **★ Hallowed** (gold) | 0.5% | {{item:skyrpg_items:soulreaper}}, or the **Jack o'Lantern** pet (Legendary) |

**Candy** is eaten for a 3-minute boost (a new candy replaces the last one; meals are separate): Candy Corn gives +25 Speed and +20 Farming Fortune, a Toffee Apple +30 Strength and +40 Health. A {{item:skyrpg_crates:pumpkin_pouch}} empties into your purse when you right-click it.

## The Hallowed Harvest set

A Legendary set for **Farming and Combat** at once, only from crates:

| Piece | Stats |
| --- | --- |
| {{item:skyrpg_items:hallowed_helmet\|Jack's Grin}} | 70 Health, 55 Defense, 18 Strength, 15 Crit Damage, 35 Farming Fortune |
| {{item:skyrpg_items:hallowed_chestplate\|Hallowed Harvest Coat}} | 105 Health, 90 Defense, 26 Strength, 50 Farming Fortune |
| {{item:skyrpg_items:hallowed_leggings\|Scarecrow Trousers}} | 90 Health, 75 Defense, 20 Strength, 45 Farming Fortune |
| {{item:skyrpg_items:hallowed_boots\|Gravewalk Boots}} | 55 Health, 45 Defense, 14 Strength, 8 Speed, 30 Farming Fortune |

**Witching Hour** (all four pieces): +40 Farming Fortune and +20 Strength, and rare crop finds are 50% more likely. **After dark in the overworld** it adds +60 Farming Fortune, +40 Strength and +30 Crit Damage more, and **twice that during a Blood Moon**.

## The scythes

Both work as a weapon and as a scythe: they harvest an area of crops (replanting them) when you break one, and their right-click ability swings all the way round you.

- {{item:skyrpg_items:reapers_scythe}} (Epic): 90 Damage, 50 Strength, 6% Crit Chance, 70 Farming Fortune; harvests a 3x3. **Reap** (35 Mana, 8 s): 55 damage to every monster within 4.5 blocks, and harvests the grown crops around you.
- {{item:skyrpg_items:soulreaper}} (Mythic): 200 Damage, 120 Strength, 12% Crit Chance, 50% Crit Damage, 160 Farming Fortune; harvests a 5x5, 15% chance to harvest twice. **Soul Sweep** (70 Mana, 14 s): 130 damage to every monster within 7 blocks, sets them on fire, heals you 3% of your health for each one hit, and harvests the grown crops around you.

## The Jack o'Lantern pet

A floating, grinning pumpkin that levels with **Farming**: +1.4 Farming Fortune and +0.6 Strength per level (at Common; Epic and Legendary give more). At level 25 rare crop finds are 25% more likely; at level 50 it heals you 2% of the melee damage you deal. It has no egg: crates are the only way to get one. See [Pets](pets).
