After the dragon, the outer End still has plenty to offer. Three new biomes change the outer islands, a new ore-like crystal gives **Astral Shards**, and a dungeon, the **Astral Observatory**, hides the End's own boss. Mobs in the End start at **level 40**, the highest base level in the game.

> [!NOTE]
> The new biomes only appear in **newly generated outer-End chunks**. The main island, and any outer islands you have already visited, are not changed.

## The new biomes

Each new biome replaces some of a vanilla biome's area, so you will still see ordinary End highlands, midlands and small islands among them. Find them with `/locate biome skyrpg_world:void_gardens` (or `shattered_isles`, `astral_fields`).

| Biome | Replaces | Share of that area | Monsters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Void Gardens | End Highlands | about 44% | Endermen |
| Shattered Isles | Small outer islands | about 44% | Endermen, endermites |
| Astral Fields | End Midlands (under the highlands and the Void Gardens) | about 47% | Endermen, endermites |

### Void Gardens

Purple **void trees** with a purpur trunk and branches and a canopy of glowing **Void Cap**. Patches of violet {{item:skyrpg_world:void_bloom}} cover the ground (it glows at light 8), alongside vanilla chorus plants. The sky and fog are a deep violet.

### Shattered Isles

Small outer islands as in vanilla, plus **floating rubble islets** of end stone, end stone bricks, purpur and amethyst at heights 40 to 100, with amethyst clusters and loose stepping stones. They are linked by gaps that you must bridge or jump.

### Astral Fields

End stone with stars in the ground. About one surface block in six becomes {{item:skyrpg_world:starstone}}, and about one column in ninety grows an {{item:skyrpg_world:astral_crystal}}, often with a second beside it. The sky and fog are deep blue.

## Starstone and Astral Crystals

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| {{item:skyrpg_world:starstone}} | End stone with glowing specks (light 6). A building block. |
| {{item:skyrpg_world:astral_crystal}} | A glowing crystal cluster (light 11). Break it with a pickaxe for **2 to 4 Astral Shards**. Silk Touch keeps the block. |
| {{item:skyrpg_world:astral_shard}} | A rare material full of cold starlight. It is meant for the Ender Wyrm altar (not in the game yet; see [Ender Wyrm](ender-wyrm)). |
| {{item:skyrpg_world:void_cap}} | The glowing canopy block of void trees. |

> [!TIP]
> Astral Fields give the easiest Astral Shards: wander the fields with a pickaxe and break every crystal you see. The Star Warden also drops 8 to 14 shards on each kill.

## The Astral Observatory

A purpur and glass observatory on stilts, found on the outer islands in all five of the biomes above (the vanilla highlands and midlands too). Inside are star-map halls, archives and telescope rooms. It is a level 64 dungeon in two floors: the Lower Halls, then a 20-block climb up to the Star Deck. See [Astral Observatory](astral-observatory) for the whole dungeon.

| Boss | Health | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Astral Sentinel** (champion, level 62) | 24,000 | Blinks, slams, looses five star-bolts, wraps itself in a ward below 50%, and summons Void Mites. |
| **The Star Warden** (boss, level 64) | 60,000 | Blinks, fires volleys of starlight and slams. Below 66% it pulls players in and summons Acolytes and Void Mites. Below 50% it wards, and below 33% it sends shockwaves and enrages. |

The Star Warden drops 1 to 2 {{item:skyrpg_world:star_warden_core}} (sells for 11,000 coins), an {{item:skyrpg_world:observatory_key}} for the vault behind his dome, and 8 to 14 Astral Shards. It also leaves a Reward Chest.

## Other things to know

- No new monsters live in the End biomes yet: it is vanilla endermen and endermites at higher levels.
- The Void Stalker only exists as a slayer boss, not as a wandering mob.
- The End does not have its own waystone, except at the Observatory entrance. See [Waystones and Travel](waystones-and-travel).

## What comes next

The End's last boss, the **Ender Wyrm**, is already in the game files and can be fought by admins. Its altar is not in the game yet. See [Ender Wyrm](ender-wyrm).
