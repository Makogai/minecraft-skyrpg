The Skyhaven Chronicle is the main questline. It has 8 chapters and 59 quests, and it teaches you the game in order, from saying hello to Hollis in the tavern to facing the Ender Wyrm. Every quest is a conversation with an NPC, so you never need a guide to know what to do next. The **Journal** in the K menu is where you read, track and re-read them.

## How a quest works

1. **Find the NPC.** A quest giver has a floating marker over their head.
2. **Talk.** Right-click them. A conversation opens. Read it, then choose **accept**.
3. **Do the objectives.** The Journal and the Quest Tracker show progress.
4. **Return to the NPC.** Go back to whoever the quest tells you to, talk to them again, and choose the hand-in option. You get your reward then, not before.

### Markers

| Marker | Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **!** | Gold | The NPC has a quest for you to pick up, or someone to talk to |
| **?** | Grey | You are working on one of their quests |
| **?** | Gold | A quest is ready: go and hand it in |

A gold **?** outranks a gold **!**, which outranks a grey **?**.

### Conversations

A conversation is a panel along the bottom of the screen: the speaker's face and name, text that types itself, and answer buttons above it.

- Click, **Space** or **Enter** shows the rest of the text, then goes on.
- Click an answer, or press its number key.
- **Esc** closes it. Nothing is lost. Talk to them again to carry on.
- Stay near the NPC. Walk away and the conversation closes.

Quests that have no giver start by themselves. A "journal entry" pops up on screen, read by the narrator, when you are out of a fight. You can read it, accept it, and the quest begins.

### Objectives

| Objective | What it asks |
| --- | --- |
| Talk | Finish a conversation with a named NPC |
| Visit | Find a place, such as a dungeon entrance, a danger region, a layer, a dimension, or a waystone |
| Collection | Gather an amount of an item after accepting the quest, or reach a collection tier. See [Collections](collections) |
| Skill level | Reach a skill level |
| Reach level | Reach a SkyRPG Level |
| Kill | Defeat a number of a kind of mob |
| Dungeon clear | Clear a dungeon |
| Slayer tier | Start or finish a slayer quest of a tier |
| Craft | Craft an item at the Artisan Bench after accepting the quest |
| Obtain | Have an item, or a pet or an enchanted item, in your inventory |
| Journal | Open the Journal |

### Rewards

Every quest pays coins and **SkyRPG XP**, and many add items. SkyRPG XP counts towards your SkyRPG Level, 100 XP a level. Quest rewards are a large share of it. See [Collections](collections).

## The Journal

Press **K** and open the **Journal** page.

- **Left side:** the chapters, each with how many quests you have finished. Click a chapter to open it, then click a quest to read it.
- **Right side:** the quest's story, who gave it, the "Return to" step, its objectives with progress bars, and its rewards.
- **Track** points your waypoint compass at the next place you need to go, or at the NPC to see.
- **Re-read** shows a quest's conversation again. Finished quests offer **Re-read intro** and **Re-read hand-in**.
- **Abandon** appears on side quests, which can be given up. The Chronicle has none yet, so you will not see it.

A **Quest Tracker** box on the HUD shows the quest you track. It sits on the middle left by default and can be moved or hidden in the HUD editor. See [HUD and minimap](hud-and-minimap).

### Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/quest` or `/quest list` | List your quests |
| `/quest track [id]` | Track a quest, or your tracked one if you give no id |
| `/quest abandon <id>` | Give up an open side quest |

### Chapter 1: Welcome to Skyhaven

A tour of the hub. You talk to Hollis, open the Journal, meet Dorrin, the Banker, and every Master in turn, learn the Artisan Bench with Brannoc and walk through the Wildgate. Rewards are stone tools, a compass, bread and torches. See [First steps](first-steps) for the full table.

### Chapter 2: First Steps

Outside, in the survival world. You attune a waystone and do honest gathering work for each Master: 64 Stone and 24 Coal for Tamsin, 32 Oak for Oswin, 16 Carrots for Mabel, 10 Cod for Marina, and zombies and skeletons for Kestrel. Odessa gives you a pup egg (Combat 3) and asks you to hatch a pet. Brannoc asks for a Delver Pickaxe (Mining 8) and Kestrel for Combat 5.

### Chapter 3: Into the Dark

Find and clear the Forgotten Crypt for Kestrel and craft the Cryptbreaker for Brannoc. Get an enchanted item for Seraphine. Morwen asks you to start and finish a tier I slayer quest. The last quest wants Combat 12 and SkyRPG Level 5.

### Chapter 4: The Red Lands

Enter a danger region, carry 10 region essences for Odessa, craft a piece of region gear, finish a tier II slayer quest, and clear Spider Hollow or the Drowned Temple. It ends with Combat 18, Mining 14 and SkyRPG Level 9.

### Chapter 5: The Deep

Descend into the Glimmer Caverns, the Fungal Depths, the Dwarven Halls and the Magma Rifts. Reach Delving 5 and Mining 18, and craft a Skyiron Pickaxe.

### Chapter 6: Fire Below

Enter the Nether and find the Ember Forest. Gather 64 Hellforged Scrap, fish 8 Magma Fish (Fishing 20), clear the Infernal Citadel and craft Hellforged armour. It ends with Combat 28 and SkyRPG Level 20.

### Chapter 7: Ancient Ruins

Find the Ruined Citadel and clear it, clear the Catacombs, gather 64 Ender Pearls, and finish a tier IV slayer quest.

### Chapter 8: The Void

Enter the End, reach the Shattered Isles, clear the Astral Observatory and defeat the Ender Wyrm. The last quest, "The Chronicle, So Far", asks for SkyRPG Level 35 and pays 30,000 coins and 2,500 XP. More chapters will follow.

> [!TIP]
> Gathering, crafting and kill objectives only count what you do **after you accept the quest**. Stone you mined yesterday does not count towards "Gather 64 Stone"; start mining once the quest is yours. A quest an NPC gives (a gold "Speak to ... to begin" box in the Journal) has not started until you talk to them, and tracking it points at that NPC.

> [!NOTE]
> The Ender Wyrm quest needs you to fight the Wyrm: kill the Ender Dragon, craft a Void Sigil and use it in the End. Everyone who fights the Wyrm gets credit for the kill, not just the player who lands the last hit. See [The Ender Wyrm](ender-wyrm).
