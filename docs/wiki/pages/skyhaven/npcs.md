Skyhaven has fourteen NPCs. Between them they run every shop, the bank, enchanting, slayer quests and the whole questline. This page lists who they are, where to find them and what each one offers. For full price lists see [Selling and shops](selling-and-shops).

## How NPCs work

- **Right-click** an NPC to talk. Most open a screen: a shop, the bank, the enchanting table or the slayer screen. They also greet you with a line.
- If they have a quest for you, or you have one to hand in, right-click opens the **conversation** instead. Many conversations end with a choice to open their shop.
- A gold **!**, a grey **?** or a gold **?** over their head means a quest. See [Quests and the Journal](quests-and-journal).
- NPCs turn to look at you when you are within 8 blocks. They cannot be hurt.
- Every NPC is on both maps with their face, name and role. Hover to read them.
- **Masters** are the five NPCs who each teach a trade: Tamsin (Mining), Mabel (Farming), Oswin (Foraging), Marina (Fishing) and Kestrel (Combat). Their shops are paid partly in coins and partly in the trade's own currency, and they unlock more as your level in that skill grows.

## The town NPCs

### Hollis Barrowmead, Tavern Keeper

Hollis runs the tavern and starts your questline. He also gives or takes in several quests in every chapter. His shop is called **The Lantern & Ladle**. It sells bread, cooked meat, stews, pies and cake for 6 to 80 coins, and a golden apple for 400. He buys raw beef, porkchop, chicken, mutton and rabbit.

{{items:minecraft:cooked_beef, minecraft:mushroom_stew, minecraft:golden_apple}}

### Cassius Vellum, Banker

Cassius keeps the Skyhaven Bank. Deposit and withdraw coins, see your last ten transactions, and read the interest note. See [Coins and the bank](coins-and-bank).

### Brannoc Ashforge, Blacksmith

Brannoc's shop is the **Ashforge Smithy**. It sells the Prospector mining set and the Prospector Pickaxe, iron tools, coal, iron ingots, buckets, an anvil and a smithing table. He buys ores, ingots and stone.

{{items:skyrpg_items:prospector_pickaxe, skyrpg_items:prospector_chestplate, minecraft:anvil}}

He also teaches the Artisan Bench in the Chronicle. See [Crafting](crafting).

### Petra Quarrystone, Builder

Petra's **Quarrystone Yard** sells stone, bricks, planks, glass, concrete, terracotta, ladders, scaffolding, lanterns and torches, mostly by the stack. She does not buy anything.

### Seraphine Vael, Enchanter

Seraphine opens **Arcane Enchanting**. Her library counts as 15 bookshelves, so enchant levels up to V are on offer there. See [Enchanting](enchanting).

**Sneak and talk to her** instead and she sells **spell scrolls** for your [wand](wands): Firebolt and Frost Shard for 500 coins each and Healing Pulse for 700. They are the common spells; the rest are crafted or dropped. See [Spells](spells).

## The Bazaar

The six merchants of the Grand Bazaar stand in pavilions around the bell tower. See [Skyhaven](skyhaven) for the layout.

### Kestrel Vane, Combat Master

**Vane's Arms** sells the Vanguard set and the Vanguard Blade, bows, crossbows, arrows, shields and the Training Dummy. From Combat 10 it sells the Fangguard set and the Pup Egg for Valor Marks. Kestrel buys monster drops: bones, string, gunpowder, ender pearls, blaze rods, Elite Trophies, Dread Fangs and Nightmare Cores. He gives most of the combat and dungeon quests.

{{items:skyrpg_items:vanguard_blade, skyrpg_items:vanguard_chestplate, skyrpg_combat:training_dummy}}

### Odessa Quill, Curio Dealer

**Quill's Curiosities** sells the eight accessories, a few rare weapons and some oddities.

{{items:skyrpg_items:emberstone_pendant, skyrpg_items:starglass_amulet, skyrpg_items:duskwing_cloak, skyrpg_items:riftblade, skyrpg_items:mending_wand}}

She also gives your first pet quests.

### Pip Bramblewick, Florist

**Bramblewick Blooms** sells every vanilla flower for 8 to 16 coins, plus azalea, moss, vines, lily pads and flower pots. Pip does not buy anything.

### Dorrin Saltmarsh, Provisioner

**Saltmarsh Provisions** sells everyday supplies: torches, a compass, a clock, maps, a spyglass, saddles, boats, beds, chests and bundles. It also sells the Satchel and Trail Pack backpacks and the Artisan Bench. He buys fresh fish and ink.

{{items:skyrpg_items:artisan_bench, skyrpg_items:satchel, skyrpg_items:trail_pack, minecraft:compass}}

### Marina Saltwick, Fishing Master

**Saltwick Tackle** sells the Driftwood Rod, Worm Bait and the Angler set for coins. For Tidescales she sells Glowworm Bait, the Reefwalker set, prismarine shards and a Heart of the Sea. At Fishing 10 she sells an Otter egg for Tidescale Plates. She buys fish, shells, pearls and Leviathan Scales.

{{items:skyrpg_items:driftwood_rod, skyrpg_items:angler_chestplate, skyrpg_items:reefwalker_chestplate}}

### Morwen Ashgrave, Slayer Master

Morwen opens the **Slayer** screen, with two tabs: slayer quests and spawners. See [Slayers](slayers).

## The Masters in the north and west

### Tamsin Deepholt, Mining Master

**Deepholt Delving Co.** sells the Quarry Pick, the Delver Pickaxe (Mining 8), the Delver set (Mining 10), and placeable ore nodes. It is paid in **Glimmer Shards** and **Glimmer Crystals** (9 shards), not coins. She buys Skyiron, Duskquartz, Emberite and Lumenite, raw or refined.

{{items:skyrpg_items:quarry_pick, skyrpg_items:delver_pickaxe, skyrpg_items:delver_chestplate}}

| Node | Mining level | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coal | 3 | 12 Glimmer Crystals |
| Iron | 6 | 20 Glimmer Crystals |
| Gold | 10 | 35 Glimmer Crystals |
| Redstone | 14 | 45 Glimmer Crystals |
| Skyiron | 18 | 70 Glimmer Crystals, 16 Raw Skyiron |
| Diamond | 22 | 120 Glimmer Crystals, 4 diamonds |
| Duskquartz | 26 | 100 Glimmer Crystals, 16 Duskquartz Shards |
| Emberite | 30 | 200 Glimmer Crystals, 24 Emberite Chunks |
| Lumenite | 40 | 400 Glimmer Crystals, 12 Lumenite Crystals |

She also sells a Mole egg (Mining 10, 48 Glimmer Crystals).

### Mabel Thornfield, Farming Master

**Thornfield Produce** sells the Harvester set and Harvester Hoe, seeds, sugar cane, bone meal, a composter and hay for coins. From Farming 3 it sells planters, sprinklers, special seeds, compost and the Fieldhand set for **Dewdrops** and **Dew Vials** (9 Dewdrops). She buys crops, seeds and produce, including the special crops. At Farming 10 she sells a Bee egg.

{{items:skyrpg_items:harvester_hoe, skyrpg_items:harvester_chestplate, skyrpg_items:fieldhand_chestplate, skyrpg_farming:copper_sprinkler}}

### Oswin Fernhollow, Foraging Master

**Fernhollow Timber** sells the Woodsman set and Woodsman Axe, vanilla saplings and logs, and from Foraging 8 the Barkguard set. For **Heartwood Splinters** and **Heartwood Bundles** (9 splinters) he sells special saplings and stumps. At Foraging 10 he sells an Owl egg. He buys logs of every kind.

{{items:skyrpg_items:woodsman_axe, skyrpg_items:woodsman_chestplate, skyrpg_items:barkguard_chestplate}}

## A note on the tavern's name

The Chronicle and the shop call Hollis's tavern **The Lantern & Ladle**. The hub build calls the building the Gilded Gull. They are the same place.

> [!TIP]
> The Masters sell a pet egg for their skill. Each needs level 10 in the skill and a cost in that trade's own currency. See [Pets](pets).
