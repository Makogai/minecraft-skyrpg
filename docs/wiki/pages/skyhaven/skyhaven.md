Skyhaven is a floating town above the clouds, and it is where you spawn, shop, bank and meet the people who teach you the game. It is a safe place. This page walks round it by area, with coordinates so you can find things on the map. North is the negative Z direction, and the Skyheart plaza is at 0, 0.

## The rules of the hub

- Skyhaven is its own dimension, separate from the survival world.
- Everyone walks **60% faster** in Skyhaven, so getting from one master to the next is quick. The boost ends the moment you leave the hub.
- Nobody can fight, break blocks, place blocks or take damage. Nobody gets hungry.
- You can use doors, trapdoors, fence gates, buttons, levers, bells and the waystone. Other blocks, such as chests and crafting blocks, cannot be used.
- Fields cannot be trampled.
- If you fall off the island you land back at spawn.
- Block items, buckets and spawn eggs cannot be used here.

`/hub` brings you home from anywhere. `/survival` takes you to the survival world, back to where you were.

## The Skyheart plaza

The plaza is the middle of the town. A golden beacon fountain rises under the floating **Skyheart**, a spinning amethyst crystal ringed with purple shards. Eight runestones with banners stand round the edge, between four avenues, with cherry planters and benches facing the fountain. Hidden lights keep it bright at night.

You spawn on the plaza, a little south of the fountain. The **Skyhaven waystone** stands just to the left of spawn, at -6, 18. Attune any waystone in the survival world and you can travel between them and the hub from here. See [Waystones and travel](waystones-and-travel).

## The north: the Crown Terrace

The avenue north of the plaza climbs to the raised **Crown Terrace**.

- **Skyhaven Bank**, with a copper dome. Cassius Vellum keeps your bank balance and pays you interest. See [Coins and the bank](coins-and-bank).
- **The Emberforge**, where Brannoc Ashforge sells armour, iron tools, coal and anvils, and buys ores and stone. A public [Artisan Bench](crafting) stands in its yard, next to him.
- **The Mason's Guild**, where Petra Quarrystone sells building blocks by the stack.
- **The windmill**, with turning sails, on the north-west edge. The Mining Master, Tamsin Deepholt, stands near it.

## The west: Greenhollow and Mistfall

Greenhollow is a working farm with fields, and Mabel Thornfield sells seeds and planters there. Further west the **Mistfall** waterfall pours from the island onto a small pond islet below.

## The south-west: Timberline

**Timberline Lodge** sits among great trees. Oswin Fernhollow, the Foraging Master, buys logs and sells saplings, stumps and the Woodsman and Barkguard sets.

## The south-east: the tavern

Just off the south avenue, on the lawn in front of the gazebo, stands the **brainrot podium**: statues of the three best brainrot collectors on gold, silver and copper pedestals, with their scores. See [Brainrots](brainrots#the-leaderboard).

The tavern, with a beer garden and a gazebo, is where Hollis Barrowmead gives out the Chronicle's first quest. The build calls the building the Gilded Gull. Hollis's shop is named **The Lantern & Ladle**. A bridge from the beer garden leads to the Arcane Library.

## The east: the Bazaar Promenade and the Grand Bazaar

The east avenue is a tree-lined promenade with benches, flower planters, banner posts and a koi garden with a brook. It ends at the **Bazaar Gate**, a great arch with a hanging "GRAND BAZAAR" sign, and a wide dark-oak bridge leads from it to the **Grand Bazaar** islet.

The Grand Bazaar is a market. A 19 by 19 plaza with a bell tower fountain sits in the middle, and six merchant pavilions stand round it:

| Pavilion | Keeper | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Armoury | Kestrel Vane | Combat Master |
| Curiosities | Odessa Quill | Curio Dealer |
| Petals & Blooms | Pip Bramblewick | Florist |
| The Pantry | Dorrin Saltmarsh | Provisioner |
| The Angler | Marina Saltwick | Fishing Master |
| Slayer Lodge | Morwen Ashgrave | Slayer Master |

There is a pond and dock east of the Angler.

## The Arcane Library

Seraphine Vael's enchanting hall stands on an islet to the south-east. Inside, a giant open book floats under a crystal. Her library counts as 15 bookshelves, so the full range of enchants is on offer. See [Enchanting](enchanting).

## The Wildgate

On its own islet to the south stands the **Wildgate**: a great stone ring with a shimmering purple veil. A sign on it reads "Wildgate, to the Survival World". Walk through and you are in the survival world, back where you left it. If it is your first time, you arrive at the survival spawn.

## Starwatch

Starwatch is a small observatory islet in the north-east, reached from the terrace. Right-click the telescope and you get a short stargazing show: fireworks burst into stars over the observatory, a field of sparkles fills the sky, and you get Night Vision for 15 seconds.

The first time you look through it you earn the **Stargazer** advancement and **500 coins**. Only one person can use the telescope at a time.

## Getting around

- The minimap in the top right shows the town and its NPCs. The map opens zoomed in when you are in the hub.
- Press **M** for the world map. Hover an icon to see who it is.
- Drop a waypoint on a spot you want to return to. See [Waypoints](waypoints).

> [!TIP]
> Not sure where an NPC is? Open the world map and look for their face. Hover to see their role. Quest NPCs also get a gold **!** or **?** above their head.
