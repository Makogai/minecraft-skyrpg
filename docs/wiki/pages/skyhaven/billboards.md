A **Billboard** is a big 16:9 screen in a real cabinet. It shows pictures from web links and cycles through up to four of them as a slideshow. Use one for event posters at spawn, a shop sign or a picture for your base.

## Crafting

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iron Ingot | Glowstone Dust | Iron Ingot |
| Iron Ingot | Painting | Iron Ingot |
| Iron Ingot | | Iron Ingot |

It is also in the creative Functional Blocks tab.

## Placing it

- **On a wall:** right-click the side of a block. The billboard hangs on that wall, its bottom level with the block you clicked and centred on it.
- **Free-standing:** right-click the top of a block. It stands on two posts there, two blocks off the ground, facing you.

It needs room: if something is in the way, you get a message and nothing is placed. The editor opens as soon as it's up.

## Setting it up

Right-click the billboard to open the editor (only the player who placed it, or an op, can):

- **Pictures:** paste up to four links to PNG or JPG pictures (`https://...`). They take turns.
- **Size:** 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 or 16 blocks wide. The height is always 9/16 of the width, and pictures are cropped to fill the screen.
- **Look:** Blackstone, Dark Oak, Quartz, Copper or Iron, which changes the frame, back, truss and post textures and the trim.
- **Each picture shows for:** 3 to 120 seconds.

Press **Save**. Everyone nearby sees the change straight away. If a bigger size doesn't fit where it is, it stays the old size.

## What it looks like

The screen sits in a cabinet with a frame, a gold, copper or iron trim and a planked back with a log truss. Redstone-lamp bulbs chase round the top and bottom bars, and two sea-lantern spotlights on arms light the picture. A free-standing one has posts with a brace. The picture and the lit bulbs glow; the rest takes the world's light like any block. The billboard also lights the ground in front of it.

## Moving or removing it

Hit it. The owner (or an op) breaks it and gets the Billboard back, settings and all reset; place it again somewhere else. Other players can't break or change your billboard.

## Pictures for events

Event art is hosted with the wiki, at `https://makogai.github.io/minecraft-skyrpg/showcase/`. For [Hallow's Eve](hallows-eve) there are `hallows-eve/poster.png`, `teaser.png`, `bloodmoon.png` and `launch.png`. Any public picture link works, but very large pictures (over 8 MB) won't load.
