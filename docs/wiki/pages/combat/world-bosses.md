World bosses are events. Every couple of hours a huge boss rises in the survival world near a random player, the whole server is told where, and everyone who hurts it is paid by how much damage they did. There are two of them, the **Hollow King** and the **Mother of Thorns**. They are the best source of {{item:skyrpg_combat:valor_seal|Valor Seals}} outside the slayer quests, with a real chance at a {{item:skyrpg_combat:nightmare_core}} or {{item:skyrpg_combat:dread_fang}}.

## When and where they appear

- Every **2 to 3 hours** (random), if **two or more players** are online, the game picks a world boss at random. If an op uses `/worldboss start`, it can be sooner.
- It picks a random player in the overworld and chooses a spot **14 to 26 blocks** from them, on dry land.
- A chat message with a warning sound goes to everyone: the boss stirs near that player, with coordinates, and **rises one minute later**. Soul flames show where.
- When it rises, lightning strikes and a second message gives the coordinates again.
- Only one world boss is up at a time.
- If nobody kills it in **15 minutes**, it sinks back into the earth and nobody is paid.

A boss bar shows its health to everyone within 64 blocks.

> [!TIP]
> The warning gives you a minute. Grab your best weapon and armour, a stack of food and arrows, and a friend. The more people hit it, the faster it falls, and everyone who deals damage is paid.

## The bosses

| | The Hollow King | Mother of Thorns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body | giant wither skeleton | giant spider |
| Level | 40 | 35 |
| Health | 5,000 | 4,500 |
| Size | 2.2 times | 3 times |
| Speed | 1.1 times normal | normal |
| Damage | 2.5 times the body's normal attack | 2.5 times the body's normal attack |
| Minions | 4 skeletons (level 25) | 6 cave spiders (level 20) |

Both have full knockback resistance, so you cannot push them around.

## The fight

1. **Ground slam, every 8 seconds.** Everyone within 6 blocks takes damage (10 vanilla damage points, which is 50 Health, before your Defense) and is thrown back. Keep your distance, or stay behind it when you see the particles.
2. **Minions at two thirds health.** The boss calls its helpers in a ring around it and warns you. They are level 25 and level 20 mobs, so kill them quickly or fight away from them.
3. **Minions again at one third health, and the boss enrages.** At one third health the boss gets 30% faster.

Minions disappear when the boss dies or leaves. Creative and spectator players are not hit by the slam.

> [!TIP]
> Ranged weapons are the safest way to damage a world boss. Stay outside the 6 block slam ring, shoot, and move away when it calls minions. Armour with plenty of Defense and Health makes a slam survivable, and a Training Dummy tells you how much damage your build really does. See [Stats](stats).

## Rewards

Everyone who damaged the boss is paid when it falls, whether or not they landed the last hit. The **top damager gets half again** (x1.5) of the coins, seals, Combat XP and rare drop chance.

| | The Hollow King | Mother of Thorns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coins | 800 to 2,000 | 600 to 1,600 |
| Valor Seals | 2 to 5 | 2 to 4 |
| Combat XP | 600 | 500 |
| Rare drop | {{item:skyrpg_combat:nightmare_core}}, 8% | {{item:skyrpg_combat:dread_fang}}, 40% |
| Bonus drop | {{item:skyrpg_pets:golden_pet_egg}}, 6% | {{item:skyrpg_pets:golden_pet_egg}}, 6% |

The top damager's rare drop chance is 12% (Hollow King) or 60% (Mother of Thorns). The rare and bonus drops are rolled separately for each player, and Magic Find does not change these chances. A chat message shows what you earned and where you ranked.

A Nightmare Core sells for 15,000 coins and a Dread Fang for 1,200. A Golden Pet Egg is one of the rarest drops in the game. See [Pets](pets).

## The Headless Horseman

A third world boss, the **Headless Horseman**, only rides during [Hallow's Eve](hallows-eve) (30 October to 1 November 2026). It never comes on the normal timer: it rises at dusk on the event's first night, every third night after that, and on Halloween night with the Blood Moon. It is a level 45 giant vindicator with 5,120 Health, a netherite axe and five pumpkin-headed zombie minions. It pays 1,500 to 3,000 coins, 3 to 6 Valor Seals and 900 Combat XP, with a 45% chance at a Hallow's Key and a 30% chance at a Hallow's Crate. See [Hallow's Eve](hallows-eve#the-headless-horseman).

## Region bosses

Danger regions have bosses of their own. They do not rise on a timer. You wake one by using a Heart Sigil at the region's shrine, or during a Blood Moon, when each heart wakes its boss if someone comes near. They are fought in the same way as a dungeon boss, with their own moves.

| Region | Boss | Level | Health |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Mire | The Bog Mother | 36 | 16,000 |
| The Blightlands | The Blight Lord | 45 | 22,000 |
| The Haunted Moors | The Lantern Wraith | 42 | 18,000 |
| Frostgrave | The Frost Revenant King | 50 | 28,000 |
| The Ashen Wastes | The Ash Colossus | 50 | 30,000 |
| The Shattered Highlands | The Storm Griffin | 55 | 34,000 |

See [Danger regions](danger-regions), [Region hearts](region-hearts) and [Blood Moon](blood-moon).
