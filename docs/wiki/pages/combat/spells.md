Spells are what [wands](wands) cast. Each spell is a **scroll** you install in a wand's slot; the wand's **selected** spell is the one **left click** casts. There are **twelve** in five elements: **Fire**, **Frost**, **Lightning**, **Arcane** and **Nature**. A spell costs **mana** and has a **cooldown** (the time before you can cast that spell again, shown as a sweep on the HUD box and on the spell wheel). Two spells can be **charged**: hold left click and let go.

## How spells deal damage

- Spell damage is the spell's base damage times **(1 + Intelligence / 100)**, so Intelligence from your wand and gear makes every spell stronger. Your wand may add more: the Emberheart Staff adds 25% to fire spells and the Frostspire Staff 25% to frost spells.
- A spell hit is a normal hit as far as everything else goes: **damage numbers** appear over the target, kills give you **Combat XP**, Valor Marks and loot, and they count for **slayer** quests and bosses. **Strength and crits do not apply**; Intelligence does the same job.
- Spells never hurt **you**, your **party** members, your pets, NPCs or villagers. Other players are only hit where player fighting is allowed. Area spells and chains go for hostile mobs; a bolt hits whatever it touches.
- Fireballs and meteors **break no blocks and start no fires**. **Burning** is the spell's own damage over time and counts as your damage too, but water puts it out.
- Mana is 100 + your Intelligence and refills 2% of the maximum a second. See [Stats](stats).

## Charging

Fireball and Meteor can be **charged**. Hold **left click** with the spell selected: a ring fills around your crosshair (about 1.5 seconds) with a rising chime, then pings when it is full. Let go to cast. A quick tap casts the small version. Mana is checked when you start and spent when the spell goes off. Switching the held item cancels a charge, and so does anything that leaves you unable to cast when you let go (no mana, or the spell still recharging).

## Getting scrolls

| Rarity | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Common (Firebolt, Frost Shard, Healing Pulse) | **Seraphine Vael** at the Arcane Library sells them: 500, 500 and 700 coins. Sneak and talk to her. See [NPCs](npcs). |
| Uncommon (Fireball, Blink, Arcane Missiles, Entangle) | The [Artisan Bench](crafting), **Enchanting 8 to 10**: paper, Runic Dust and materials of the element. |
| Rare (Chain Lightning, Blizzard, Mana Shield) | The Artisan Bench, **Enchanting 20 to 22**, and they **drop** from bosses. |
| Epic (Thunderclap) and Legendary (Meteor) | **Drop only**, from the hardest bosses and dungeon rewards (the Ender Wyrm, the Star Warden, the Iron Thane and others), at a low chance. A **Blood Moon** also makes hostile mobs you kill drop a scroll now and then (not a Meteor). |

Scrolls can be sold to the NPCs and traded. Installing one in a wand does not use it up: take it out again and you have it back. Scroll recipes: Fireball (paper, Ember Core, Blaze Powder), Blink (paper, Ender Pearl, Amethyst Shard), Arcane Missiles (paper, Amethyst, Wisp Essence), Entangle (paper, Vine, Dew Vial), Chain Lightning (paper, Stormfeather, Skyiron Ingot), Blizzard (paper, Rime Shard, Frost Essence) and Mana Shield (paper, Glimmer Crystal, Wisp Essence), all with Runic Dust.

## Effects you will notice

- Every spell flashes at the wand when it goes off and has its own **trail**, **impact burst** and **sound**.
- **Meteor**, **Thunderclap**, a charged **Fireball** and the start of a **Blizzard** shake the screen for anyone close.
- **Lightning** is drawn as jagged arcs between targets; a Thunderclap also calls lightning on a few of the enemies it stuns.
- A **Mana Shield** circles you with blue light, flashes when it absorbs a hit and says how much it took.
