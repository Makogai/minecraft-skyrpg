SkyRPG adds its own monsters on top of vanilla's. Each one is a new kind of mob built on a vanilla body, with its own look, attacks and drops. They get levels, nameplates and damage numbers like any other hostile mob, they can be Elites, and several of them count for [slayer quests](slayers). This page is the field guide. For the full item list of any drop, see the [mob list](mob-list).

Health in this page is the base health on the SkyRPG scale (5 health is 1 vanilla point), before the level bonus of +8% per level above 1. See [Combat basics](combat-basics).

## World mobs at a glance

These eight spawn naturally, mostly in the dark. Spawn weight is compared with vanilla's zombie at 95.

| Mob | Where | Weight | Health | Counts for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Glimmer Crawler | caves below y 0 | 40 | 100 | Broodmother |
| Bog Hag | swamps, mangrove swamps | 25 | 200 | none |
| Frost Revenant | snowy biomes | 40 | 150 | Bone Marshal |
| Ember Imp | badlands (30), the Nether (10), Ember Forest (60) | see left | 70 | none |
| Grove Sentinel | forests except dark forests and pale gardens | 30 | 220 | none |
| Tide Lurker | rivers and beaches | 35 | 150 | Grave Tyrant |
| Hollow Wisp | plains, sunflower plains, meadows | 15 | 80 | Void Stalker |
| Gravecaller | dark forests and pale gardens | 35 | 170 | Grave Tyrant |

They follow the usual monster rule: on the ground, in the dark, so surface ones come out at night. Crawlers need to be below y 0. Danger regions, deep layers and Blood Moons add levels on top. See [Danger regions](danger-regions).

### Glimmer Crawler

A quick spider (speed 0.4, a quarter bigger than normal) with glowing crystals on its back. It lives in the caves below y 0 and comes in groups of 1 to 3.

- **Drops:** 0 to 2 string, 1 to 3 {{item:skyrpg_mining:glimmer_shard|Glimmer Shards}}, and a 1% chance of a {{item:skyrpg_mining:prism_fragment}} when a player kills it.

### Bog Hag

A swamp witch. While she fights you she calls bog slimes to her side: **two every 15 seconds, four at most**. The slimes are farmed, so they give nothing.

- **Drops:** a {{item:skyrpg_combat:hags_charm}}, 0 to 2 glass bottles, and a 10% chance of a Duskwillow sapling.

### Frost Revenant

A stray of the snowy lands. Each arrow that hits you adds **6 seconds of freezing**, the same slow as powder snow.

- **Drops:** 0 to 2 bones, 0 to 2 arrows and 1 to 2 {{item:skyrpg_combat:rime_shard|Rime Shards}}.

### Ember Imp

A small, quick blaze (65% size, speed 0.32) that throws fireballs. It is fire immune. Imps also fill the Ember Forest in the Nether and the Magma Tyrant calls them as helpers (see [The Nether](the-nether)).

- **Drops:** an {{item:skyrpg_combat:ember_core}}, 0 to 1 blaze powder and an 8% chance of an Emberwood sapling.

### Grove Sentinel

A slow, tough guardian of the forests, built on a zombie body with 6 armour and 60% knockback resistance. It is made of wood: it does not burn in daylight, has no baby form and calls no reinforcements. Its hits give **Slowness IV for 2 seconds**, which roots you in place. Base damage 25.

- **Drops:** 1 to 3 sticks, 1 to 2 Heartwood Splinters and a 2.5% chance of a Golden Acorn.

> [!TIP]
> Do not stand and trade blows with a Grove Sentinel. Hit it, step back, and let the root wear off before you step in again. It walks slowly, so a bow keeps you out of reach.

### Tide Lurker

A drowned that haunts rivers and beaches at night. Every **6 seconds**, if you are 4 to 12 blocks away and it can see you, it throws a tide hook and **hauls you towards it** (and towards the water). Base damage 20. Tide Lurkers also guard the Drowned Temple.

- **Drops:** 0 to 2 rotten flesh, 1 to 2 Tidescales and a 3% chance of a nautilus shell.

### Hollow Wisp

A lone spirit that drifts through walls, unarmed. It is small and flies, so fight it in the open. Base damage 15.

- **Drops:** a {{item:skyrpg_combat:wisp_essence}} and 0 to 2 glowstone dust.

### Gravecaller

A necromancer of the dark forests. Within 16 blocks of its target it **raises two zombies every 12 seconds, four at most**. The zombies are farmed, so killing them is for safety, not for rewards. Kill the Gravecaller first.

- **Drops:** rotten flesh and bones (0 to 2 each) and a 50% chance of {{item:skyrpg_combat:tyrants_ash}}.

## Mobs of the deep and the Nether

These have no biome spawns in the overworld. The world's layer and biome systems spawn them in their own places, and each has a spawn egg in the creative Spawn Eggs tab.

| Mob | Where | Health | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sporeling | Fungal Depths (y -20 to 0) | 60 | weak, quick, bursts into spores |
| Dwarven Construct | Dwarven Halls layer (y -40 to -20) and the Dwarven Halls dungeon | 300 | armoured, projectiles do half |
| Fire Beetle | Magma Rifts (y -64 to -40) | 200 | charges, sets you alight |
| Bone Giant | the Bone Sea in the Nether | 550 | club and bone quake |

See [The Deep](the-deep) and [The Nether](the-nether) for the places themselves.

### Sporeling

A knee-high mushroom creature, speed 0.32, base damage 15. Sporelings keep to their group and **the whole group joins in when one of them finds you or is hit** (within 12 blocks). When one dies it bursts into a spore cloud (3 blocks wide, about 4 seconds) that gives **Poison for 5 seconds**. Sporelings and their kin are immune to the poison.

- **Drops:** 0 to 1 brown mushroom and a 30% chance of {{item:skyrpg_world:glowcap_spores}}.

> [!TIP]
> Kill Sporelings with a bow or step back as soon as one dies. The cloud only reaches about 3 blocks.

### Dwarven Construct

A stone-and-brass automaton: 10 armour, 4 toughness, slow (speed 0.18), 75% knockback resistance, base damage 40. **Arrows and other projectiles do half damage** (with a spark and a clang). Its punch knocks you back and lifts you off your feet. The Forgeguard champion of the Dwarven Halls is a scaled-up Construct. See [Dwarven Halls](dwarven-halls).

- **Drops:** a copper nugget (3 in 5) or an iron nugget (2 in 5), 1 to 4 or 1 to 3, and a 20% chance of a {{item:skyrpg_world:dwarven_cog}}. In the layer, kills also have their own 25% chance of a Dwarven Cog.

### Fire Beetle

A big six-legged beetle with a magma-cracked shell: 8 armour, 2 toughness, 75% knockback resistance, base damage 30, fire immune. Its bite sets you alight for 4 seconds. Every **7.5 seconds**, if you are 4 to 12 blocks away and in sight, it digs in for one second (the wing cases lift, flames at its feet), then **dashes about 12 blocks in a straight line**. A hit does 1.8 times its normal damage, a big knockback and sets you alight for 5 seconds. A wall or a hit ends the dash early.

- **Drops:** 0 to 2 magma cream and a 25% chance of a {{item:skyrpg_world:rift_ember}}.

> [!TIP]
> When the beetle digs in, sidestep. The charge goes in a straight line, so stepping out of its path is enough.

### Bone Giant

A hunched giant skeleton: 550 health, 8 armour, 2 toughness, 80% knockback resistance, base damage 60, fire immune. It walks over soul sand without sinking.

- **Club swing:** it raises its femur club and brings it down 11 ticks later, about every 1.6 seconds, with heavy knockback and a lift.
- **Bone quake:** about every 10 seconds, with you within 5 blocks, it rears up for a second while a ring of soul fire marks the ground. Then it stomps: everyone within 4 blocks takes 75% of its attack damage, is thrown up a little and is slowed for 3 seconds.
- **Drops:** 2 to 5 bones, a 35% chance of 1 to 2 bone blocks, a 50% chance of 1 to 3 soul soil, and a 12% chance (player kills) of a Hellforged Scrap.

> [!TIP]
> Leave the ring of soul fire before the stomp lands. The quake is only dangerous if you are inside it.

## Named dungeon mobs

Each dungeon names its own packs (Crypt Archers, Broodlings, Drowned Acolytes, Lost Miners and so on). They are vanilla mobs with levels and gear, plus a few of the mobs above. See the dungeon pages for what to expect in each.
