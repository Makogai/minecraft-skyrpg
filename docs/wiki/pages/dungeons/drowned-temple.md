The Drowned Temple is a vast sunken temple of an older sea-faith, built on the ocean floor and flooded to the rafters. It has two floors of prismarine and sandstone halls, drowned guards with tridents, and a goddess of the deep at the bottom. It is the third dungeon, and the first where the environment is part of the fight: you swim, you fight in water, and you hunt for the air pockets. Its boss, the **Tidemother**, drops the Tide Pearls for the Tidecaller Blade and the Tidemother Regalia.

> [!WARNING]
> Almost everything is underwater, doorways included. Bring **Water Breathing** and plenty of light. Air pockets (shrines, ledges, the champion hall and the sanctum) let you breathe, but do not rely on them.

## Finding it

- It generates on the **ocean floor** in oceans of any depth, and in cold and warm oceans, but **not in frozen oceans**. One per 32 by 32 chunks.
- The entrance is an open forecourt of smooth sandstone and sea lanterns on the sea floor, with a **waystone** and coral mounds. Dive and look for the glow.
- Queen Arachne has a 40% chance of dropping a treasure map to the Temple.

## Layout

**Floor 1, the Outer Sanctum** (prismarine bricks and sandstone):

1. The forecourt.
2. Three **spine halls**: colonnades, shrines with air pockets, cross halls, an elite guardian hall (its south gate is barred until the pack is dead) and a grand nave.
3. The **champion hall**, 21 by 12 by 21 blocks: a flooded arena with an air pocket, four corner ledges (two with rare chests), a central dais and ring of pillars. The south portcullis rises when the champion falls.
4. A flooded **descent**, a ramp 10 blocks down that turns the temple a quarter turn.

**Floor 2, the Abyssal Vaults** (darker, with sculk and blackstone):

1. Three more spine halls, including vent galleries and sculk halls.
2. **The Tidemother's sanctum**, a domed round chamber 31 blocks across and 16 tall, with an air pocket. A treasure annex lies behind the Tidemother's door to the west, and a key-locked vault to the east.

Side rooms branch off every hall:

| --- | --- |
| Kelp garden | A drowned grove. Tide Lurkers ambush from the kelp. |
| Shipwreck hold | A whole sunken ship inside the temple. Chests in the hold, and the captain's on the stern. |
| Coral reliquary | A coral altar with a rare chest, guarded in two waves, and a secret behind the coral. |
| Drowned barracks, sentinel barracks | Bunks and a long table. Three waves. |
| Eel tunnels | A serpentine of low tunnels, with arrow slots and spikes at the bends and a rare chest at the end. |

| Vent chamber, vent galleries | Magma vents whose bubble columns pull you down. |

## Puzzles and traps

- **The pressure room.** Three heavy pressure plates around the room. The sign says "Three plates. One door. The tide holds the hoard." Step on all three and the sealed dark-prismarine door sinks, opening two rare chests.
- **The tide gauge.** Three tide pillars show **High, Low, High**. A sign says "Tide gauges: High, low, high. Lever up = high." Set the levers up, down, up. A barred treasury with three rare chests opens.
- **The pearl treasury.** Three levers and a sign "Out. In. In. Lever up = in." Set them down, up, up to open the pearl vault.
- **Arrow slots** in the walls of many rooms (damage 6 and more), **spike plates**, **poison pools** and **vents** that pull you down.
- **Secrets:** caches hidden behind plain sandstone or coral. Break in to find them.

## The Abyssal Herald (champion)

Level 30, 4,000 health, a drowned with a trident and a turtle helmet, scaled 1.6 times. Melee 15.

| Move | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trident volley | every 7.5 s | 3 tridents, damage 9 each. |
| Undertow (pull) | every 15 s | Drags everyone within 12 blocks to it. |
| Tidal wave (shockwave) | every 12 s | 7 block ring, damage 11, pushes you away. |
| Summon | every 24 s | 2 Drowned Acolytes (level 28), up to 4 alive. |

**Pays:** 2,500 to 3,500 coins and 900 Delving XP; first clear 3,000 coins and 1,500 Combat XP. Loot: 4 to 6 rolls of Runic Dust, Tidescale Plates, diamonds, prismarine crystals and more; 50% a Tide Pearl, 35% a Tide Key, 15% a Heart of the Sea, 4% a Tidecaller Blade or Tidemother Pendant. Tomes: Deepcall II (12%), Arcane Weave II, Precision II (8%) and Tideborn I (5%).

## The Tidemother (boss)

Level 34, **14,000 health** (+60% per extra player), a giant drowned in a turtle helmet with a trident, scaled 2.2 times. Melee 20. "You walk the drowned halls of a dead sea-god. Now drown with them." Move damage is in vanilla points (1 point is 5 Health).

| Move | From | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trident volley | start | every 8.5 s | 4 tridents, damage 12. |
| Undertow (pull) | start | every 16 s | Drags everyone within 15 blocks to her. |
| Tidal wave (shockwave) | start | every 13 s | 10 block ring, damage 14, pushes you. |
| Summon | start | every 26 s | 2 Tide Lurkers (level 31) and 3 Drowned Acolytes (level 30), up to 6 alive. |
| Coral armour (ward) | start | every 45 s | Takes 85% less damage for 6 seconds. |
| Crash down (slam) | below 66% | every 15 s | 7 block ring, damage 16, launches you. |
| Poisoned brine (cloud) | below 66% | every 21 s | A Poison II cloud under you, 4 blocks wide, 6 seconds. |
| Conduit mend (heal) | below 50% | every 25 s | Heals 3% of her health. |
| Storm of tridents | below 33% | every 6.5 s | 7 tridents, damage 12, widely spread. |
| Enrage | below 33% | once | 38% faster and 40% more damage. |

At two thirds "the sea itself answers". At one third "the conduit overloads".

> [!TIP]
> Move out of the ring when the warning reads "A tidal wave rises! Get clear!" or "The Tidemother raises her trident to crash down!". Hold your biggest attacks while "Coral armour sheathes the Tidemother", because the ward cuts her damage by 85%. The undertow drags you to her, so be ready for a slam right after it. In the last third, the storm of tridents is the danger, so keep strafing between volleys.

## Loot

**The Tidemother's kill** (each player who hit it):

- coins 10,000 to 14,000 and 4,500 Delving XP,
- 2 to 4 {{item:skyrpg_world:tide_pearl|Tide Pearls}}, a Tide Key, a 12% {{item:skyrpg_items:tidecaller_blade}} and a 12% {{item:skyrpg_items:tidemother_pendant}},
- 6 to 9 rolls of diamonds, nautilus shells, prismarine crystals, gold, scutes, Runic Dust, Prism, Tidescale Plates, Abyssal Pearls and more, plus 2 to 3 rolls of Heartstones, Lumenite, Emberite, Leviathan Scales, Valor Seals, Dread Fangs, Null Cores and Elite Trophies,
- 40% a Heart of the Sea, **25% a Nightmare Core**, 20% a Void Fragment, 12% an otter egg and 6% a Golden Pet Egg,
- tomes: Deepcall III (25%) and V (8%), Arcane Weave III (15%), Precision III (15%) and V (8%), Tideborn I (12%).
- First clear: 20,000 coins and 6,000 Combat XP.

**The Reward Chest** rolls 4 to 6 times for Abyssal Pearls, Leviathan Scales, Tidescale Plates, Prism, Heartstones, Lumenite, Valor Seals and more, plus a 50% Tide Pearl, 30% a Heart of the Sea, 20% a Nightmare Core, 5% a Golden Pet Egg and a 10% Tidecaller Blade or Pendant.

**The vault** (east of the sanctum, one Tide Key) pays about 2,800 coins and 700 Delving XP.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Water Breathing and a good light source before anything else. Use the air pockets to heal and drink. The Tidemother fight is long, so bring food and healing.

- Clear packs, find secrets and solve the three puzzle rooms: the Reward Chest rolls once more for every three packs, every secret and two for every champion.
- Kill a Tidebound Sentinel for the key early.
