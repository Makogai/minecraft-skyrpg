The Forgotten Crypt is the first dungeon most players clear. It is a sealed two-floor burial complex under forests, plains and meadows, full of skeletons, zombies and the dead of a forgotten order. It is common, easy to find and forgiving, and its boss, the **Crypt Warden**, drops the Warden's Sigils you need for the first set of dungeon gear. It is a good place to learn how [dungeons work](dungeons-overview).

## Finding it

- It generates under **any forest, plains, sunflower plains or meadow**, about one per 28 by 28 chunks, so it is the most common dungeon.
- The entrance is a small **stone-brick mausoleum** on the surface, with coffins, skulls and candles, and a stair that spirals down. A waystone stands in the corner. Walking near it discovers the dungeon.
- Wayshrine chests have a 25% chance of a Forgotten Crypt treasure map.

## Layout

**Floor 1, the Upper Crypt** (stone bricks, mossy and cracked):

1. The mausoleum and its stair.
2. Three **spine halls**: long corridors of pillars and coffins, with arrow slits in both long walls and spike plates in the aisle.
3. The **champion arena**, where the Bone Knight holds the way down. The gate stays shut until it falls, and arrow slits along the north wall hit anyone who flees.
4. A **stairwell** that drops 10 blocks and turns east.

**Floor 2, the Ossuary Depths** (deepslate tiles and polished blackstone, soul fire):

1. Three more spine halls.
2. The **Warden's hall**, 31 by 15 by 31 blocks: an aisle up to a dais, a sarcophagus, braziers that fire flame traps around the dais, balconies on both sides, and ossuary niches along the wall.

Every spine hall has side doors east and west leading to side rooms. You will meet:

| Room | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| Lever rooms | A gated treasury or cache, opened by levers (see Puzzles). |
| Guard room | A pack and a chest. |
| Chapel, library, prison, tomb cell | Packs, chests, decor. The prison has a locked cell with a chest inside. |
| Embalming room, bone throne, ossuary, catacomb | Packs in big, bony rooms. |
| Flooded room | A sunken pool with a hidden cache. |
| Collapsed room | A broken hall with a hidden study. |

## Monsters

Packs of 4 to 10, many in two or three waves.

| Mob | Looks like | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crypt Archer | skeleton with a bow | everywhere |
| Bone Knight | armoured skeleton with a sword | floor 1 |
| Restless Dead | zombie with a sword | everywhere |
| Dust Walker | husk | both floors |
| Frostbitten Dead | stray | both floors |
| Ashen Guard | wither skeleton | floor 2 |
| Drowned Dead | drowned with a trident | flooded rooms |
| Gravecaller, Hollow Wisp | our own mobs | some packs. See [Mobs](mobs). |
| Crypt Captain, Wight Captain | **Elite** zombie and wither skeleton in iron armour | on both floors |

Elites carry the {{item:skyrpg_world:crypt_key}}, 35% each.

## Puzzles and traps

- **Lever rooms.** Two rooms hide their loot behind an iron-bar gate and a row of levers. The sign says what to do. "Wake" means pull the lever on.
  - **The cache.** Four levers. "Wake the first and the last. Let the middle two sleep." Pull lever 1 and lever 4 on, and leave 2 and 3 off. It holds a secret cache.
  - **The treasury.** Five levers. "Three kings sat, two slept below. Wake the first, the third, the last." Pull levers 1, 3 and 5 on, and leave 2 and 4 off. It holds a rare chest, and a pack with a Crypt Captain guards it.
- **Arrow slits** in the corridors, and **spike plates** in the aisles of the spine halls.
- **Flame traps** around the Warden's dais. Do not stand on the braziers.

**Secrets** (250 coins and 150 Delving XP each, once): Sealed Burial, Hidden Burial, Sleepers' Cache, Drowned Cache, Hidden Study and the Warden's Vault.

## The Bone Knight (champion)

Level 16, 1,200 health, a skeleton in iron armour, scaled 1.4 times. Melee 10.

| Move | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slam | every 10 s | A 5 block ring, damage 10 after a short wind-up. |
| Summon | every 19 s | 2 Crypt Archers and a Restless Dead (level 15), up to 4 alive. |
| Blink | every 14 s | Teleports behind you. |

**Pays:** 800 to 1,200 coins and 400 Delving XP; first clear 1,500 coins and 500 Combat XP. Loot: 50% a Warden's Sigil (1 to 2), 35% a Crypt Key, 80% Runic Dust, 2 to 5 Valor Marks, iron and bones. Tomes: Hallowed Edge II (12%), Soul Siphon I, Aegis II and Vitality II (8% each).

## The Crypt Warden (boss)

Level 18, **4,500 health** (plus 60% for each extra player), a giant wither skeleton in netherite armour, scaled 1.6 times. Melee 14. It says "Who disturbs the Warden's rest? The dead keep their oaths." Phases are announced at two thirds and one third health. Move damage is in vanilla points (1 point is 5 Health).

| Move | From | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slam | start | every 11 s | A 6 block ring: "the floor cracks". Damage 14 after a 1.2 s wind-up, and a launch. |
| Summon | start | every 21 s | 3 Crypt Archers and 2 Restless Dead (level 17), up to 6 alive. |
| Blink | start | every 15 s | Vanishes and appears behind you. |
| Shockwave | start | every 17 s | A 9 block ring, damage 10, pushes you away. |
| Soulfire volley | below 66% | every 13 s | 3 fireballs, damage 8 each, spread out. |
| Spectral plate (ward) | below 50% | every 35 s | Takes 85% less damage for 7 seconds. |
| Enrage | below 33% | once | 33% faster and 35% more damage. |

> [!TIP]
> Fight it in the aisle with your back to a pillar so the archers cannot flank you, and kill the summons on the spot. When "Spectral plate hardens around the Warden", stop swinging, back off and wait about 7 seconds. In the last third, leave the 6 block slam ring as soon as it flashes.

## Loot

**The Warden's kill** (each player who hit it):

- coins 3,000 to 4,500 and 1,500 Delving XP,
- 2 to 4 {{item:skyrpg_world:warden_sigil|Warden's Sigils}}, a Crypt Key, 6 to 14 emeralds, 4 to 10 gold, 3 to 6 Runic Dust (90%), 3 to 6 Valor Marks,
- a **25% chance of a {{item:skyrpg_combat:nightmare_core}}**,
- 15% each of the Cryptbreaker and the four Warden's Shroud pieces,
- a 40% chance of a **Spider Hollow map**,
- tomes: Hallowed Edge III (30%) and V (10%), Soul Siphon II (15%), Aegis III (15%), Vitality III (12%) and V (8%).
- First clear: 5,000 coins and 1,500 Combat XP.

**The Reward Chest** rolls once plus bonuses from what you cleared: 2 to 3 Warden's Sigils, a Valor Seal, 2 to 4 Prism Fragments, a Dread Fang, 4 to 8 Runic Dust, 1 to 3 golden apples, 10% for each gear piece, a 15% enchanted golden apple and a 1% {{item:skyrpg_pets:golden_pet_egg}}.

**The vault** (key door in the east wall of the Warden's hall, one key): 5 to 8 rolls of emeralds, gold, books, diamonds and materials, plus 2 to 3 Warden's Sigils, a Valor Seal (60%), 1 to 2 Dread Fangs (40%), a Nightmare Core (10%), an Elite Trophy (20%) and a 20% chance of a Cryptbreaker or Shroud piece. It pays about 1,200 coins and 300 Delving XP.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Clear every pack on both floors and find the secrets before you fight the Warden. Each three packs, each secret and each champion adds a bonus roll to the Reward Chest. The Crypt is short enough to do in one sitting.

- Bring a shield or sprint through the corridors with arrow slits.
- Kill Elites for the key early so the vault is ready.
- A bow or crossbow works well against the Warden's summons. Keep melee for the Warden itself.
- The Warden drops a map to [Spider Hollow](spider-hollow), the next dungeon.
