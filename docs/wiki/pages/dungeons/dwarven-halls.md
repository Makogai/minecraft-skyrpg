The Dwarven Halls are a ruined dwarven city, built into the rock under mountains, hills and forests. You enter through a broken stone gate on a hillside and walk down a very long spiral stair into forge halls, barracks, rail stations and vaults lit by glowing runes. Lost Miners and clockwork Constructs still guard it. It is the fourth dungeon, and its boss, **the Iron Thane**, drops the runes for the Thane's Hammer and the Runeforged set.

## Finding it

- It generates under **mountains, hills, forests, windswept hills and forests, and stony peaks**, about one per 40 by 40 chunks, and never within 8 chunks of a Spider Hollow.
- The entrance is the ruined **Dwarven Gate**: a carved deepslate doorway in a hillside with braziers, a collapsed statue and a waystone. Walking near it discovers the dungeon.
- Abandoned Mine Camps (small camps in caves, guarded by Lost Miners) have a 15% chance of a Dwarven Halls treasure map in their chests.
- The same dwarven stone shows up in the **Dwarven Halls layer** of the caves (y -40 to -20), but the dungeon itself is a separate structure. See [The Deep](the-deep).

## Layout

The halls run deep, so budget for a long walk and a long climb out. Use the waystone at the gate to come back.

**The surface and the stairwell.** From the gate, a square-spiral stair of about 76 blocks goes down, with landings that hold packs, chests and arrow traps. It leaves through an east door at the bottom.

**Floor 1, the Upper Halls** (deepslate bricks, lanterns):

1. Three **spine halls**: great halls, barracks, a rail station and forge halls.
2. **The Forgeguard's arena**, where the champion waits behind a barred plug.
3. A switchback **descent** that drops 20 blocks and turns right.

Side rooms: a Duskquartz vault, a library with a hidden alcove, a collapsed tunnel, an armoury, a mess hall, a forge room, a cart depot, a rune shrine and a plate-puzzle vault.

**Floor 2, the Deep Forge** (polished blackstone and oxidised copper, soul lanterns):

1. Three more spine halls: forge halls, Duskquartz galleries, great halls, a rail station and barracks.
2. **The Deep Forge**, 31 by 15 by 31 blocks, where the Iron Thane stands. A key-door vault and a hoard nook open here.

Side rooms: a deep vault, forge works, a lava cistern, the king's tomb, a foundry, an ore gallery, a rail junction and the rune archive. Ore walls of Duskquartz can be mined: {{item:skyrpg_mining:duskquartz_shard|Duskquartz}} for the halls' gear and other recipes.

## Monsters

| Mob | Looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Lost Miner | zombie with an iron pickaxe and a helmet. Some are **Elites** and carry the {{item:skyrpg_world:thane_key}}. |
| Deepdelve Archer | skeleton with a bow and an iron helmet |
| Dwarven Construct | our brass-and-stone automaton: heavy armour, projectiles do half damage. See [Mobs](mobs). |

A standard pack on floor 1 is 3 Lost Miners, 2 Archers and 1 Construct. On floor 2 it is 2 Miners, 2 Archers and 2 Constructs.

> [!TIP]
> Constructs shrug off arrows (half damage). Fight them in melee or with an ability, and use bows on Miners and Archers. Their punch lifts you off your feet, so do not stand with your back to a drop.

## Puzzles and traps

- **The plate vault.** Three pressure plates. The sign says "Three plates. One door. The ancestors keep the hoard." Step on all three to open the door.
- **The rune archive** (floor 2). Three levers with a sign: "Down. Up. Down. The runes obey the old order." Set the levers down, up, down to open the door.
- **The vault.** A sign reads "The vault is sealed behind iron and rune. Bring the Thane's Key." The key opens it.
- **Arrow traps** on the stair landings and in side rooms, and a lava cistern room on floor 2. The Deep Forge's floor turns to magma in the Thane's last phase.
- The **Forgeguard's** gate stays shut until it falls.

## The Forgeguard (champion)

Level 38, 6,500 health, a Dwarven Construct scaled 1.7 times. Melee 21. "The Forgeguard grinds awake. Its rune-core flares: no trespasser passes the Thane's forge."

| Move | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hammer-fist slam | every 11.5 s | A 5.5 block ring, damage 18, launches you. |
| Rune-core shockwave | every 16.5 s | 8 block ring, damage 14, pushes you away. |
| Summon | every 26 s | 2 Dwarven Constructs (level 36), up to 4 alive. |
| Brass plates (ward) | below 50%, every 45 s | Takes 75% less damage for 5 seconds. |

**Pays:** 3,800 to 5,000 coins and 1,300 Delving XP; first clear 4,500 coins and 2,200 Combat XP. It **always drops a Thane's Key**, 2 to 4 {{item:skyrpg_world:dwarven_cog|Dwarven Cogs}}, 45% a Rift Ember and 35% an Emberite Ingot, plus Duskquartz, Skyiron, diamonds and books. 7% a Nightmare Core. Tomes: Rich Vein II (12%), Veinseeker II (8%), Steadfast II (8%), Forgefire I (4%).

## The Iron Thane (boss)

Level 42, **24,000 health** (+60% per extra player), a hulking dwarven king on his own body, melee 28. "You walk the Thane's halls with living feet. Kneel, or be forged anew in my fire." Move damage is in vanilla points (1 point is 5 Health).

| Move | From | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hammer slam | start | every 10.5 s | 7 block ring, damage 24, launch. |
| Vault (leap) | start | every 15 s | Jumps up to 20 blocks, damage 22, 4 block ring. |
| Anvil shockwave | start | every 17 s | 10 block ring, damage 18, pushes you. |
| Rune plate (ward) | start | every 45 s | Takes 80% less damage for 6 seconds. |
| Call the Forgeguard | below 66% | every 23.5 s | 2 Forgeguards (level 40) and 2 Lost Miners (level 38), up to 6 alive. |
| Anvil pull | below 66% | every 19 s | Drags everyone within 14 blocks to him. |
| Forge-fire mend (heal) | below 50% | every 26 s | Heals 3% of his health. |
| Molten floor | below 33% | every 12 s | 12 spots (4.5 blocks) glow, then burn for 6 seconds, 22 damage. |
| Slag volley | below 33% | every 9.5 s | 5 fireballs, damage 12 each. |
| Erupting slam | below 33% | every 8.5 s | A faster slam: 8 block ring, damage 26. |
| Enrage | below 33% | once | 36% faster and 40% more damage. |

At two thirds he stamps his hammer and the Forgeguard answers. At one third "his crown ignites" and the floor runs molten.

> [!TIP]
> Phase 1 is a tank-and-spank. Stand on his flank and step out of each slam ring. In phase 2, kill the Forgeguard summons before they box you in, and use a ranged attack on the Miners. In phase 3 the floor is the danger: when "the floor glows red-hot", move. Leave the glowing spots before the burn starts, and never stand still in the Deep Forge.

## Loot

**The Thane's kill** (each player who hit it):

- coins 16,000 to 22,000 and 7,000 Delving XP,
- a {{item:skyrpg_world:thanes_rune|Thane's Rune}} (and a 25% chance of a second), a Thane's Key, 4 to 8 Dwarven Cogs, 10 to 20 Duskquartz Shards and 4 to 8 Emberite Ingots,
- 5 to 7 rolls of diamonds, emeralds, Runic Dust, Skyiron, Glimmer Crystals, Prism Fragments, Glimmer Geodes, Rift Embers and more,
- 50% a Valor Seal, 60% an Elite Trophy, **25% a Nightmare Core**, 3% a Golden Pet Egg,
- tomes: Rich Vein III (25%) and IV (8%), Veinseeker III (15%), Gemcutter III (12%), Steadfast III (15%), Titanbane III (8%), Forgefire I (12%).
- First clear: 30,000 coins and 9,000 Combat XP.

**The Reward Chest** rolls 5 to 7 times for the same family of materials, with a 20% Nightmare Core, a 35% chance of 1 to 2 Dread Fangs and a 4% Golden Pet Egg.

**The vault** (a key door in the Deep Forge, one Thane's Key) pays about 3,400 coins and 850 Delving XP. The **hoard nook** opens when the Thane dies.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Bring ore-mining gear: the halls have Duskquartz walls you can mine as you go. Kill an Elite Lost Miner or the Forgeguard for the Thane's Key, then open the vault before the fight so you know what you are fighting for.

- Fight the Constructs in melee, not with arrows.
- The Thane's pull and shockwave move you around the arena. Keep out of the lava-red spots and do not stand still in the last phase.
