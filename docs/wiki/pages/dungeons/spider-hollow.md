Spider Hollow is a spider nest dug under dark forests and pale gardens: web-choked tunnels, glowing grottoes, silk bridges over underground lakes, and a queen at the bottom. It is the second dungeon, a step up from the [Forgotten Crypt](forgotten-crypt), and it asks you to deal with webs, poison and swarms. Its boss, **Queen Arachne**, drops Arachne's Eyes for a poison sword, a dodge cloak and the Carapace set.

## Finding it

- It generates under **dark forests and pale gardens**, about one per 18 by 18 chunks, kept 10 chunks from Forgotten Crypts.
- The entrance is a **sinkhole** in the forest floor with a waystone beside it.
- The Crypt Warden has a 40% chance of dropping a treasure map to Spider Hollow.

## Layout

The tunnels are carved into rock, with moss, tuff and calcite on floor 1 and deepslate, basalt and sculk on floor 2.

**Floor 1, the Web Tunnels:**

1. The sinkhole and a shaft down.
2. Three **spine halls** of webbed tunnels, 21 blocks deep each.
3. The **champion nest**, where the Silk Matron waits behind a door plug.
4. A spiral **shaft** that drops 14 blocks.

**Floor 2, the Brood Depths**, running at a right angle under floor 1:

1. Three more spine halls.
2. **Queen Arachne's Lair**, 31 by 16 by 31 blocks, with the vault in it.

Side doors off every hall lead to themed chambers: egg chambers, brood nests, cocoon galleries, glimmer and mushroom grottoes, an underground lake, silk bridges, venom pools, collapsed mines, bone pits, a web maze and the plate puzzle room. Most hold a pack and a chest. Silk bridges have spike traps, venom pools have poison clouds, and the web maze has spike traps and a chest at its heart.

## Puzzles and traps

- **The plate puzzle.** Three stone pressure plates lie in dark corners of the room, each half-buried in webs. The sign reads "Three plates sleep in the webs. Wake them all." Step on every plate. They light up, and the mossy gate grinds open on a **Sleeping Cache** secret.
- **Arrow slits** (4 damage on floor 1, 6 on floor 2) in the walls and **spike traps** (3, then 5) around bridges and the maze.
- **Poison pools** around the venom pools.
- **Webs** slow you down. Cut them with a sword or burn them out.

**Secrets** (250 coins and 150 Delving XP each) are hidden in side chambers. The Sleeping Cache is one of them.

## The Silk Matron (champion)

Level 23, 2,400 health, a giant spider scaled 1.7 times. Melee 12.

| Move | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Webs | every 10 s | 8 cobwebs around each nearby player for 5 seconds. |
| Leap | every 12.5 s | Pounces from up to 16 blocks. Damage 12, 3.2 block ring. |
| Summon | every 19 s | 3 Broodlings (level 21), up to 6 alive. |

**Pays:** 1,500 to 2,200 coins and 650 Delving XP. Loot: 3 to 6 Brood Silk, 80% 1 to 2 Venom Glands, a 35% Elite Trophy, 15% a Dread Fang, 6% a Nightmare Core, and gold, emeralds, diamonds and Runic Dust. Tomes: Webcutter II (12%), Venom Coat I (10%), Evasion II (8%) and Ambush II (8%).

## Queen Arachne (boss)

Level 26, **8,000 health** (+60% per extra player), a giant spider scaled 2.4 times. Melee 18. "You dare tread upon my silk? Feed, my children!" Move damage is in vanilla points (1 point is 5 Health).

| Move | From | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Webs | start | every 10 s | 8 cobwebs around each nearby player. |
| Web volley | start | every 8.5 s | 4 web shots (damage 8). Where they land, they leave cobweb. |
| Leap | start | every 13 s | Damage 15, 3.5 block ring, range 18. |
| Summon | start | every 21 s | 4 Broodlings (level 23) and a Hollow Weaver (level 24), up to 10 alive. |
| Venom pool | start | every 15 s | A Poison II cloud under you for 6 seconds. |
| Web drag (pull) | below 66% | every 18 s | Drags everyone within 14 blocks to her. |
| Shockwave | below 33% | every 15 s | 8 block ring, damage 12, knocks you away. |
| Enrage | below 33% | once | 42% faster and 40% more damage. |

At two thirds she screeches and her silk-sisters answer. At one third "her eyes blaze".

> [!TIP]
> Do not stand still while webs bind your feet: cut them with your sword or move before the next leap. Kill Broodlings as they come, because a swarm of ten drains you. When a venom pool forms under you, step out of it. In the last third, stay at range and keep your back clear for the shockwave.

## Loot

**The Queen's kill** (each player who hit it):

- coins 6,000 to 8,000 and 2,800 Delving XP,
- 2 to 4 {{item:skyrpg_world:arachne_eye|Arachne's Eyes}}, a Hollow Key, 15% an {{item:skyrpg_items:arachne_fang}}, 15% a {{item:skyrpg_items:widowsilk_cloak}},
- 5 to 7 rolls of gold, emeralds, diamonds, Runic Dust, Brood Silk, Venom Glands, Prism Fragments and more,
- 50% a Valor Seal, 60% an Elite Trophy, **25% a Nightmare Core**, 3% a Golden Pet Egg,
- a 40% chance of a **Drowned Temple map**,
- tomes: Webcutter III (25%) and V (10%), Venom Coat II (20%), Evasion III (15%), Ambush III (12%) and V (8%).
- First clear: 9,000 coins and 3,000 Combat XP.

**The Reward Chest** rolls 5 to 7 times for materials, with a 20% Nightmare Core, 35% 1 to 2 Dread Fangs, a 10% owl or bee egg, a 6% Arachne's Fang or Widowsilk Cloak and a 4% Golden Pet Egg.

**The vault** (a key door in the Queen's lair, one {{item:skyrpg_world:hollow_key}}): 6 to 8 rolls of diamonds, emeralds, gold, Brood Silk, Prism Fragments, Heartstones, Lumenite, Valor Seals and Elite Trophies, plus 2 to 3 Arachne's Eyes, 2 to 4 Venom Glands, a 12% Nightmare Core and a 20% chance of the Fang or Cloak. About 2,200 coins and 550 Delving XP.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Webs and swarms are the dungeon. Keep a sword that clears webs quickly, fight in tunnels you have already cleared, and keep moving so webs and poison pools do not pin you. Arachne's Fang also does extra damage to the Broodmother slayer boss.

- Clear the packs before the Queen. Each three packs, each secret and each champion adds a bonus roll to the Reward Chest.
- The Queen drops a map to the [Drowned Temple](drowned-temple).
