The Astral Observatory is the last dungeon: a purpur and glass observatory on the outer End islands, with star-map murals, telescopes, glass roofs and end rods. You climb through the Lower Halls past the Astral Sentinel, up to the Star Deck, and into the Dome where the **Star Warden** keeps the sky. It is the hardest fight in the game short of the Ender Wyrm, and its boss drops the Star Warden's Core and the Astral Shards that go into the Void Sigils that summon the Wyrm.

## Finding it

- It generates on the **outer End islands**: the End highlands, midlands and barrens, and the new Void Gardens, Shattered Isles and Astral Fields biomes. One per 26 by 26 chunks, and never within 6 chunks of an End city. The main island does not have one. See [The End](the-end).
- Track "The Astral Observatory" in your journal and a waypoint points at the nearest one. See [Quests and journal](quests-and-journal).
- The first floor stands on purpur stilts, on the island or over the void, at about y 68. The second floats on a thin hull above it.
- The entrance is the **Courtyard**: a terrace on stilts with a waystone, a telescope and a gate 7 blocks wide, leading to an atrium under a glass roof.

> [!WARNING]
> The first floor stands on stilts, sometimes over the void, and the End has no safe ground below it. Bring a way to stop a fall (blocks, slow falling) and be careful at the edges.

## Layout

**Floor 1, the Lower Halls** (end stone bricks, purpur and glass):

1. The Courtyard.
2. Three spine stages of halls 17 blocks deep: halls of charts (carpets, star-map murals, cartography tables), archives, glass galleries and sentinel quarters.
3. **The Orrery Hall**, where the Astral Sentinel waits behind a plug of bars, under an orrery of end rods and glass above the dais.
4. **The Ascent**: a tower with two switchback flights of purpur stairs, 20 blocks up. It leaves by its east wall.

Side rooms: a telescope room, a map room, a lens workshop and the astronomer's quarters (with a cache). Small rooms: a closet, a stargazer's nook (a cache) and a guard post.

**Floor 2, the Star Deck**, 20 blocks higher and a quarter turn from floor 1:

1. Three more spine stages with the same four kinds of hall.
2. **The Star Warden's Dome**, 35 by 28 by 35 blocks: a glass roof and windows, a star chart inlaid in the floor, and the great telescope in a back alcove. A key-door vault and a hoard nook (which opens when the Warden dies) are here.

There are **no lever or plate puzzles**. The doors are the champion's bar plug, the vault's key door and the hoard door.

> [!TIP]
> Shulker bullets levitate you, so deal with Star Sentinels early. Do not look at an Astral Acolyte until you are ready to fight it: like any enderman, it attacks when you look it in the eyes.

## The Astral Sentinel (champion)

Level 62, 24,000 health, an enderman scaled 1.8 times with 85% knockback resistance. Melee 40. "The sky is closed to the unlettered."

| Move | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flicker (blink) | every 8 s | Vanishes and appears behind you. |
| Long arm (slam) | every 11.5 s | 6 block ring, damage 38, launches you. |
| Star-bolts (volley) | every 13 s | 5 arrows, damage 18. |
| Shell of light (ward) | below 50%, every 40 s | Takes 75% less damage for 5 seconds. |
| Summon | every 24 s | 3 Void Mites (level 62), up to 4 alive. |

**Pays:** 14,000 to 18,000 coins and 6,000 Delving XP; first clear 16,000 coins and 7,000 Combat XP. It **always drops an Observatory Key** and 3 to 6 {{item:skyrpg_world:astral_shard|Astral Shards}}. Also ender pearls, ender eyes, shulker shells, 25% a Dread Crystal, 35% an Elite Trophy, 8% a Nightmare Core. Tomes: Cleave III and Bloodthirst III (8% each), Steadfast III (6%), Vitality IV (8%).

## The Star Warden (boss)

Level 64, **60,000 health** (+60% per extra player), an enderman scaled 2.5 times. Melee 56. "The great lens swings round and the dome goes dark. 'I keep the sky.'" Move damage is in vanilla points (1 point is 5 Health).

| Move | From | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Step between stars (blink) | start | every 7 s | Appears behind you. |
| Starlight volley | start | every 10.5 s | 6 arrows, damage 22. |
| Collapsed-star fist (slam) | start | every 11.5 s | 8 block ring, damage 52, launches you. |
| Black hole (pull) | below 66% | every 18 s | Drags everyone within 18 blocks into his palm, hard. |
| Summon | below 66% | every 23 s | 2 Astral Acolytes and 3 Void Mites (level 62), up to 6 alive. |
| Shell of starlight (ward) | below 50% | every 45 s | Takes 80% less damage for 6 seconds. |
| Starlight ring (shockwave) | below 33% | every 15 s | 12 block ring, damage 40, a huge push. |
| Enrage | below 33% | once | 38% faster and 40% more damage. |

At two thirds "the lenses align" and the Warden calls his acolytes. At one third "the dome cracks and starlight pours in".

> [!TIP]
> Expect the Warden at your back every 7 seconds. The black hole drags you towards him, so be ready to leave the 8 block slam ring straight after. Spend your burst when the ward drops, and kill the Mites quickly.

## Loot

**The Star Warden's kill** (each player who hit it):

- coins 60,000 to 80,000 and 28,000 Delving XP,
- a {{item:skyrpg_world:star_warden_core|Star Warden's Core}} (and a 25% chance of a second), an Observatory Key and 8 to 14 Astral Shards,
- 5 to 7 rolls of diamonds, ender eyes, shulker shells, echo shards, Runic Dust, Dread Crystals and more,
- 50% a Valor Seal, 60% an Elite Trophy, **25% a Nightmare Core**, 3.5% a Golden Pet Egg, 10% a Wolf Pup or Mole egg,
- tomes: Reaper's Mark IV (12%) and V (5%), Bloodthirst IV (12%) and V (5%), Cleave V (15%), Vitality VI (15%) and Soulrend III (4%).
- First clear: 110,000 coins and 36,000 Combat XP.

**The Reward Chest** rolls 5 to 7 times for Astral Shards, ender eyes, shulker shells, echo shards, Dread Crystals, Heartstones and more, with a 22% Nightmare Core, a 35% chance of 1 to 2 Dread Fangs, a 4% Golden Pet Egg and a 12% pup or mole egg.

**The vault** (in the Dome, one Observatory Key) pays about 6,400 coins and 1,600 Delving XP. The vault also has a 2% chance of an elytra.

The Star Warden's Core sells for 11,000 coins. {{item:skyrpg_world:astral_shard|Astral Shards}} are also dropped by Astral Crystals, which grow in the Astral Fields. They go into the Void Sigil that summons the Ender Wyrm. See [Ender Wyrm](ender-wyrm).

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Treat it as an end-game raid: your best gear, food and healing, and a stack of blocks for the void. The Warden's health rises by 60% for each extra player in the room, so bring people who add real damage.

- Do not skip the Sentinel. It always drops the Observatory Key that opens the vault.
- Clear packs and find secrets for the Reward Chest bonus.
