The Ruined Citadel is a castle sunk into the mountain peaks: a broken gatehouse on the slope, then three floors of sunken courtyards, libraries, chapels and a throne room under the rock. Armoured zombies, skeleton knights and castle guards still hold it. It is the sixth dungeon, and the first with three floors. Its boss, **the Fallen Knight**, drops the Fallen Crest, a trophy for the Ender Wyrm altar, plus top-tier materials and a Reward Chest.

## Finding it

- It generates in the **mountains**: frozen peaks, jagged peaks, stony peaks, snowy slopes, groves and windswept hills (including gravelly hills). One per 56 by 56 chunks, and never within 10 chunks of the Dwarven Halls.
- The entrance is a ruined **gatehouse** built into the slope: two broken towers, a gate arch, a plaza with banners on poles, a fallen statue and a **waystone**. Walking near it discovers the dungeon.
- The questline asks you to find it. See [Quests and journal](quests-and-journal).

## Layout

From the gatehouse a tunnel leads into a **stairwell** with two landings (each with a pack, chests and an arrow trap), and the castle runs under the mountain.

**Floor 1, the Outer Ward:**

1. Two spine stages of halls 19 blocks deep: sunken courtyards, barracks, stables with idle skeleton horses, and mess halls.
2. **The Knight-Captain's Hall**, behind a plug of bars.
3. A descent 16 blocks down, carved in rock.

Side rooms: an armoury behind three pressure plates, a kitchen, a storeroom with a cache, a guardroom and a cellar well, plus leaf rooms.

**Floor 2, the Keep:**

1. Two spine stages: libraries, great halls, chapels and a statue gallery.
2. A second descent, 16 blocks down.

Side rooms: a scriptorium, a treasury behind three levers, a solar, cells, a shrine and an armoury.

**Floor 3, the Throne Room:**

1. **The Hall of Banners**, a portcullis shut until three levers are set right, with a pack waiting on each side.
2. **The Throne Room**, 31 by 21 by 31 blocks, a plain blackstone floor, a key-door vault with two vault chests, and a hoard nook behind a door that opens when the Fallen Knight dies.

## Puzzles and traps

- **The treasury levers** (floor 2). Three levers with the text "Up, down, up. Like the banners." Set them up, down, up to open the iron-bar gate.
- **The Hall of Banners** (floor 3). "Raised, lowered, raised. As the banners hang." The banners on the walls are red, white, red. Set the levers the same way (up, down, up) to raise the portcullis.
- **The armoury** (floor 1). Three pressure plates open the door.
- **The vault.** "The vault is sealed behind a crest. Bring the Crest Key."
- **Arrow slits** (11 damage on floor 1, 13 on floor 2, 15 on floor 3) and **spike plates** (8, 10 and 12) throughout, and a poison trap in one room.

## The Knight-Captain (champion)

Level 57, 16,000 health, a zombie in diamond armour scaled 1.8 times. Melee 36. "The Citadel does not fall. It only waits."

| Move | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blade slam | every 10 s | 6 block ring, damage 30, launches you. |
| Vault (leap) | every 14 s | Jumps up to 18 blocks, damage 28, 4 block ring. |
| Archers' volley | every 13 s | 6 arrows, damage 12 each, spread out. |
| Tower shield (ward) | below 50%, every 40 s | Takes 75% less damage for 5 seconds. |
| Summon | every 24 s | 2 Citadel Knights (level 55), up to 4 alive. |

**Pays:** 10,000 to 13,500 coins and 3,600 Delving XP; first clear 11,000 coins and 5,400 Combat XP. It **always drops a Crest Key**. Also Duskquartz, Lumenite Crystals, diamonds and emeralds, 45% Prism Fragments, 30% a Hellforged Ingot, 25% a Dread Crystal, 35% an Elite Trophy, 8% a Nightmare Core. Tomes: Titanbane III (12%), Final Stand III (10%), Cleave IV (10%), Steadfast III (8%), Aegis V and Vitality V (8% each).

## The Fallen Knight (boss)

Level 58, **48,000 health** (+60% per extra player), a giant wither skeleton in black netherite plate with a black banner on his arm, scaled 2.6 times. Melee 54. "I held this hall when kings were dust. Kneel, or break." Move damage is in vanilla points (1 point is 5 Health).

| Move | From | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Black blade (slam) | start | every 10 s | 8 block ring, damage 42, launches you. |
| Launch (leap) | start | every 15 s | Jumps up to 22 blocks, damage 38, 5 block ring. |
| Shadow-fire volley | start | every 12 s | 5 fireballs, damage 18. |
| Call the dead (summon) | below 66% | every 23 s | 2 Fallen Guards and 3 Skeleton Knights (level 56), up to 6 alive. |
| Vanish (blink) | below 66% | every 18 s | Teleports behind you. |
| Wither cloud | below 66% | every 17 s | A Wither II cloud under you, 4 blocks wide. |
| Banner feast (heal) | below 50% | every 28 s | Heals 3% of his health. |
| Black plate (ward) | below 50% | every 45 s | Takes 70% less damage for 5 seconds. |
| Drag (pull) | below 33% | every 20 s | Drags everyone within 16 blocks towards him. |
| Rotting floor (molten) | below 33% | every 12.5 s | 14 spots (5 blocks) glow, then burn for 6 seconds, 34 damage. |
| Shockwave | below 33% | every 15 s | 11 block ring, damage 30, a big push. |
| Collapse (slam) | below 33% | every 8.5 s | A faster slam: 9 block ring, damage 48. |
| Enrage | below 33% | once | 37% faster and 40% more damage. |

At two thirds he rips his banner free and the dead knights of the Citadel rise. At one third his visor burns violet.

> [!TIP]
> Treat the summons as a clock: kill the Skeleton Knights and Guards each time they appear, or they box you in against the wither cloud. When he blinks behind you, turn and keep your distance. In the last third, three things come at once (the drag, the rotting floor and the fast slam), so move early and keep your health high.

## Loot

**The Fallen Knight's kill** (each player who hit it):

- coins 44,000 to 58,000 and 21,000 Delving XP,
- a {{item:skyrpg_world:fallen_crest|Fallen Crest}} (and a 25% chance of a second), a Crest Key, 2 to 4 Hellforged Ingots, 2 to 4 Prism Fragments, 4 to 8 Lumenite Crystals and 6 to 12 Duskquartz Shards,
- 5 to 7 rolls of diamonds, emeralds, gold, Runic Dust, Dread Crystals, Attuned Lumenite and more,
- 30% a Prism, 35% Heartstones, 50% a Valor Seal, 60% an Elite Trophy, **25% a Nightmare Core**, 4% a Golden Pet Egg, 10% a Wolf Pup or Mole egg,
- tomes: Titanbane IV (16%) and V (6%), Final Stand IV (14%) and V (5%), Cleave VI (12%), Bloodthirst V (10%), Reaper's Mark V (8%), Vitality VII (12%), Hallowed Edge VII (10%), Aegis VII (10%), Deflection IV (10%) and Soulrend V (5%).
- First clear: 90,000 coins and 26,000 Combat XP.

**The Reward Chest** rolls 5 to 7 times for the same materials, with a 25% Prism, 22% Nightmare Core, 4.5% Golden Pet Egg and a 12% pup or mole egg.

**The vault** (one Crest Key; it holds two vault chests) pays about 5,600 coins and 1,400 Delving XP for each vault chest you open.

The Fallen Crest and other late trophies sell for thousands of coins, but you will want to keep them. They are for the Ender Wyrm altar. See [Ender Wyrm](ender-wyrm).

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> This is a fight for geared players: bring Defense, a strong healing source, and fire and wither protection if you have it. The vault and the Reward Chest are worth a full clear. Remember that three packs, every secret and every champion add bonus rolls to the Reward Chest.

- Kill Elites for the Crest Key early so the vault is ready.
- Do not skip the banner and treasury puzzles. They are quick, and the treasury has good chests.
- Hold your big attacks while the Knight-Captain's tower shield is up, and use the gap when it drops.
