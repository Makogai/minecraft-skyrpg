The Catacombs are a labyrinth of crypts, buried in the deep rock of the overworld with no door on the surface. You find the sealed stair, dig your way in, and hunt through mazes, ossuaries and lever halls for three **Ossuary Keys**. Each is held by a Bone Warden, a giant skeleton. The keys open the three bone doors of the inner tomb, where **the Bonelord** waits. It is the seventh dungeon, and it asks for a bit of everything: puzzles, traps, a key hunt and one big boss.

## Finding it

- It generates **anywhere in the overworld**, very rarely (one per 64 by 64 chunks), and the whole structure lies between about y -59 and y -36, in the Magma Rifts layer. See [The Deep](the-deep).
- There is **no surface part** and no treasure map. The easiest way to find it is the questline: track "The Catacombs" in your journal and a waypoint points at the nearest one within 100 chunks. See [Quests and journal](quests-and-journal).
- The entrance is a **sealed stair**: solid rock around a square-spiral stair of 18 blocks. Its top is a small sealed bone chamber at about y -41, with a sign reading "SEALED. The dead keep their own counsel.", candles, skulls, a chest, and a cracked wall. Dig down to it and break through the cracked wall. Unlike the other dungeons, there is no waystone here.

> [!WARNING]
> Digging into the Magma Rifts is dangerous on its own: lava pools and Fire Beetles. Bring fire protection and a way to escape. See [Mobs](mobs).

## Layout

The Catacombs are one labyrinth with no floors. Everything is a stage on the way to the tomb.

1. **The entrance stair**, with two landings (packs and an arrow trap), then out to the south.
2. **Stage 1 and stage 2** of the spine, each 21 blocks deep. Each stage is either a **maze** (6 by 5 cells with loops, dead-end chests and packs, traps) or a **hall**: a crypt hall, an ossuary hall, a **lever hall** or a **plate hall**. The lever and plate halls shut the spine with a bone grate until the puzzle is solved. Every spine piece has a west and an east door.
3. **The tomb gate**: a hall of three bone doors in a row.
4. **The Bonelord's Crypt**, 31 by 18 by 31 blocks, with bone arches, a throne and a hoard alcove that opens when he dies.

Off the spine:

- **Bone Warden chambers.** The west and east doors of stage 1, and the west door of stage 2, lead to a chamber with a Bone Warden. Each drops exactly one Ossuary Key.
- **The side rooms** off the east door of stage 2: an ossuary, a bone pit, sarcophagi, a trap gallery, a shrine, a lever vault, a plate vault and a charnel house.
- **Lanes**: crossroads, tees and straight tunnels cut from solid stone, ending in leaf rooms (urns, a cache, a grave).

## Keys

The tomb needs three Ossuary Keys, one per bone door. A door uses up one key per click, so the gate is three doors in a row, labelled "SEAL 1", "SEAL 2" and "SEAL 3": "Press an Ossuary Key to the bone."

- Each of the **three Bone Wardens** drops one.
- **Elites** in packs drop an Ossuary Key 35% of the time.
- A rare chest has a 5% chance of a spare key, so a lost warden cannot lock you out.

## Puzzles and traps

- **The lever hall.** Four levers and the text "Four levers: pull the three that are bright. Leave the dark one." Set three on and one off.
- **The plate hall.** "Three stones in the floor. Wake them all." Step on all three stone plates.
- **The lever vault** (side room). "The first one sleeps. Wake the others." Leave lever 1 off and set 2 and 3 on to raise the grate on a vault with two vault chests and a rare chest.
- **The plate vault** (side room). Plates open a vault.
- **The trap gallery**, and traps in the mazes: arrow slits (14 to 17 damage), spike plates (11 to 14) and poison pools (10 to 13).

## The Bone Warden (champion)

Three of them, level 60, 15,000 health each, a Bone Giant scaled 1.3 times. Melee 40. "A Bone Warden unfolds from its alcove, a key of grey bone hanging from its ribs."

| Move | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bone fist (slam) | every 9.5 s | 6 block ring, damage 34, launches you. |
| Lurch (leap) | every 14.5 s | Jumps up to 16 blocks, damage 30, 4 block ring. |
| Grave-cloud | every 16.5 s | A Wither II cloud under you, 3.5 blocks wide. |
| Bone plates (ward) | below 50%, every 40 s | Takes 75% less damage for 5 seconds. |
| Summon | every 24 s | 2 Bone Soldiers (level 59), up to 4 alive. |

**Pays:** 11,000 to 14,500 coins and 3,800 Delving XP; first clear 12,000 coins and 5,800 Combat XP. Always drops an Ossuary Key and 5 to 10 Marrow Shards. Also 45% Prism Fragments, 30% a Hellforged Ingot, 25% a Dread Crystal, 35% an Elite Trophy, 8% a Nightmare Core. Tomes: Soulrend III and Soul Siphon III (10% each), Hallowed Edge V, Aegis V, Vitality V and Cleave IV (8% each).

## The Bonelord (boss)

Level 62, **60,000 health** (+60% per extra player), a Bone Giant scaled 2 times with a golden crown. Melee 70. "Three keys, and still you come. The Catacombs have waited a long time for fresh bones." Move damage is in vanilla points (1 point is 5 Health).

| Move | From | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fist the size of a cart (slam) | start | every 10 s | 8 block ring, damage 48, launches you. |
| Rib volley | start | every 11.5 s | 8 arrows, damage 20 each, spread out. |
| Leap | start | every 16 s | Jumps up to 22 blocks, damage 42, 5 block ring. |
| Grave-cloud | start | every 16 s | A Wither II cloud under you, 4 blocks wide, 6 seconds. |
| Call the dead (summon) | below 66% | every 23 s | 2 Tomb Wraiths and 3 Crypt Archers (level 60), up to 6 alive. |
| Throne pull | below 66% | every 20 s | Drags everyone within 16 blocks to him. |
| Phylactery drain (heal) | below 50% | every 27 s | Heals 3% of his health. |
| Rib cage (ward) | below 50% | every 45 s | Takes 70% less damage for 5 seconds. |
| Rotting floor (molten) | below 33% | every 12 s | 14 spots (5 blocks) rot green, then burn for 6 seconds, 36 damage. |
| Grave-cold shockwave | below 33% | every 15 s | 11 block ring, damage 32, a big push. |
| Cracking slam | below 33% | every 8.5 s | A faster slam: 9 block ring, damage 54. |
| Enrage | below 33% | once | 35% faster and 40% more damage. |

At two thirds the walls tear and the dead march. At one third "the crown burns green" and the floor rots.

> [!TIP]
> Never stand in a wither cloud: step out as soon as it forms. Do not fight in the middle of the room in the last third, because the rotting floor, the pull and the fast slam all hit there. Hold your burst for the moments the ward is down.

## Loot

**The Bonelord's kill** (each player who hit it):

- coins 48,000 to 62,000 and 24,000 Delving XP,
- a {{item:skyrpg_world:bonelord_phylactery|Bonelord's Phylactery}} (and a 25% chance of a second), 3 to 5 Hellforged Ingots, 3 to 5 Prism Fragments, 4 to 8 Lumenite Crystals, 6 to 12 Duskquartz Shards and 8 to 16 Marrow Shards,
- 5 to 7 rolls of diamonds, gold, Runic Dust, Dread Crystals, Attuned Lumenite, Dread Fangs and more,
- 35% a Prism, 35% Heartstones, 50% a Valor Seal, 60% an Elite Trophy, **25% a Nightmare Core**, 4.5% a Golden Pet Egg, 10% a Wolf Pup or Mole egg,
- tomes: Soulrend IV (15%) and V (6%), Soul Siphon IV (14%), Reaper's Mark V (10%), Bloodthirst V (10%), Cleave VI (12%), Vitality VII (12%), Hallowed Edge VII (10%), Aegis VII (10%), Rift Echo V (8%), Final Stand V (6%).
- First clear: 110,000 coins and 30,000 Combat XP.

**The Reward Chest** rolls 5 to 7 times for the same materials, with a 28% Prism, 22% Nightmare Core, 5% Golden Pet Egg and a 12% pup or mole egg.

**The vaults** pay about 6,200 coins and 1,550 Delving XP per vault chest.

The Bonelord's Phylactery sells for 13,000 coins. It is a trophy for the Ender Wyrm altar. See [Ender Wyrm](ender-wyrm).

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Kill the Bone Wardens first. They are champions with a lot of health, and each counts as a champion for bonus Reward Chest rolls (two per champion). Do the puzzle rooms on your way: the vault rooms are the best chests before the Bonelord.

- Plan an exit: it is a long dig down and a long way back, and there is no waystone.
- Dungeon Elites drop Ossuary Keys too, so you rarely run short.
- The tomb gate needs all three keys before you can reach the Bonelord.
