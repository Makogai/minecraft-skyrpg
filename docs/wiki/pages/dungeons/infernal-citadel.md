The Infernal Citadel is a blackstone fortress in the Nether, on stilts over the lava sea or built into a cliff. Its halls are lined with polished blackstone bricks, gilded blackstone, nether bricks and soul-fire braziers, with lava falls behind iron bars. It is the fifth dungeon and the first one outside the overworld. Its boss, **the Magma Tyrant**, drops the Tyrant's Core and the Hellforged scrap you need for the fire-proof Hellforged armour.

> [!WARNING]
> Lava is everywhere. Fire resistance, or fire immunity from the Hellforged armour, will save your life. Check your gear before you go through the Nether portal. See [The Nether](the-nether).

## Finding it

- It generates in the **Nether wastes, basalt deltas and soul sand valleys**, and in the new Nether biomes (Ember Forest, Bone Sea and Obsidian Spires). One per 44 by 44 chunks, so it is rare, and never within 8 chunks of a vanilla fortress or bastion.
- The first floor is at about y 65, the second at about y 45, above the lava sea (y 31). Look for a blackstone fortress with banner towers.
- The **gatehouse** is the entrance: a forecourt on stilts with a waystone, and a portcullis gate between two banner towers. Walking near it discovers the dungeon.

## Layout

**The gatehouse.** A forecourt on stilts, then the gate hall: a causeway between two lava moats, columns, lava falls and arrow slits. The spine leaves by its north wall.

**Floor 1, the Burning Bastion:**

1. Three spine stages of halls: muster halls, barracks, war forges, lava bridges, a prison block and the chapel of flame.
2. **The Knight's Hall**, where the Infernal Knight waits behind bars that stay closed until he falls.
3. A switchback **descent** carved in basalt and blackstone, 20 blocks down.

Side rooms: an armoury vault with a plate door, a cell block, a flame shrine, a war room with a hidden strongbox, a mess hall, a guard post, a lava overlook and a forge annex, plus small leaf rooms (a closet, a smuggler's nook with a cache, a guard post, a vent).

**Floor 2, the Molten Throne**, 20 blocks lower:

1. Three spine stages: lava bridges, magma foundries, obsidian colonnades, imp warrens and an ember chapel.
2. **The Molten Throne**, 35 by 23 by 35 blocks: a plain solid floor, a stepped throne, a magma-heart lava fall behind it, lava falls down both walls, a key-door vault and a hoard nook that opens when the Tyrant dies.

Side rooms: an armoury vault, an imp den, a magma cistern, an obsidian crypt with a cache, a war room, a flame shrine, a forge annex, a soul well and a lava overlook.

## Puzzles and traps

- **The armoury vault** (floor 1). A room with three pressure plates and the sign "Hold all three. The war-store opens for no one." Step on all three plates to open the door.
- **The cell block.** A sign reads "PRISONERS OF THE CITADEL, no bail."
- **The vault.** "The vault is sealed in obsidian and fire. Bring the Citadel Key."
- **Lava falls** behind iron bars (lava cannot flow through bars), **arrow slits** (9 damage on floor 1, 11 on floor 2) and **flame bursts** (7, then 9) in the halls.
- The Knight's Hall bars open when the champion dies.

## The Infernal Knight (champion)

Level 50, 14,000 health, a wither skeleton in blackened netherite plate scaled 2.2 times. Melee 32. "No one breaches the Burning Bastion."

| Move | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Burning blade (slam) | every 10.5 s | 6 block ring, damage 26, launches you. |
| Vault (leap) | every 14.5 s | Jumps up to 18 blocks, damage 24, 4 block ring. |
| Fire volley | every 13.5 s | 4 fireballs, damage 14. |
| Black plate (ward) | below 50%, every 40 s | Takes 75% less damage for 5 seconds. |
| Summon | every 24 s | 2 Citadel Guards (level 48), up to 4 alive. |

**Pays:** 8,500 to 11,000 coins and 3,000 Delving XP; first clear 9,500 coins and 4,800 Combat XP. It **always drops a Citadel Key** and 3 to 6 Hellforged Scrap. Also 35% a Hellforged Ingot, 30% Netherite Scrap, 25% a Dread Crystal, 35% an Elite Trophy, 8% a Nightmare Core. Tomes: Emberbrand II (12%), Cleave III, Bloodthirst III (8%), Steadfast III (6%), Vitality IV (8%).

## The Magma Tyrant (boss)

Level 52, **36,000 health** (+60% per extra player), a huge molten-rock giant on its own body, melee 44. "The throne cracks open and the molten heart beats. 'Little cinders. I am the fire under the world, and I am hungry.'" Move damage is in vanilla points (1 point is 5 Health).

| Move | From | How often | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Molten fist (slam) | start | every 10.5 s | 8 block ring, damage 36, launches you. |
| Fireball volley | start | every 10 s | 5 fireballs, damage 16. |
| Leap | start | every 15.5 s | Jumps up to 22 blocks, damage 32, 5 block ring. |
| Call the heart (summon) | below 66% | every 23.5 s | 2 Magma Cubes and 3 Ember Imps (level 50), up to 6 alive. |
| Heart pull | below 66% | every 19 s | Drags everyone within 16 blocks towards the heart. |
| Heart-fire mend (heal) | below 50% | every 27 s | Heals 3% of his health. |
| Molten floor | below 33% | every 12 s | 14 spots (5 blocks) glow white-hot, then burn for 6 seconds, 30 damage. |
| Fire shockwave | below 33% | every 15 s | 11 block ring, damage 26, a big push. |
| Erupting slam | below 33% | every 8.5 s | A faster slam: 9 block ring, damage 40. |
| Enrage | below 33% | once | 35% faster and 40% more damage. |

At two thirds magma cubes and Ember Imps pour out. At one third "the crown ignites" and the floor runs molten.

> [!TIP]
> Fire immunity (the Hellforged set, or potions) makes the fireballs and molten floor much less dangerous. Without it, keep moving in the last phase, and leave marked spots before the burn starts. Kill the Ember Imps first, because they add fire damage.

## Loot

**The Tyrant's kill** (each player who hit it):

- coins 36,000 to 48,000 and 16,000 Delving XP,
- a {{item:skyrpg_world:tyrants_core|Tyrant's Core}} (and a 25% chance of a second), a Citadel Key, 8 to 14 {{item:skyrpg_mining:hellforged_scrap|Hellforged Scrap}}, 2 to 4 Hellforged Ingots, 2 to 4 Netherite Scrap and 5 to 9 Emberite Ingots,
- 5 to 7 rolls of diamonds, gold, blaze rods, magma cream, Runic Dust, Rift Embers, Dread Crystals and more,
- 50% a Valor Seal, 60% an Elite Trophy, **25% a Nightmare Core**, 3.5% a Golden Pet Egg, 10% a Wolf Pup or Mole egg,
- tomes: Emberbrand III (20%), Ember Ward I (12%), Reaper's Mark IV (12%) and V (5%), Bloodthirst IV (12%) and V (5%), Cleave V (15%), Vitality VI (15%).
- First clear: 70,000 coins and 20,000 Combat XP.

**The Reward Chest** rolls 5 to 7 times for the same materials plus Heartstones, with a 22% Nightmare Core, a 35% chance of 1 to 2 Dread Fangs, a 4% Golden Pet Egg and a 12% pup or mole egg.

**The vault** (in the Molten Throne, one Citadel Key) pays about 4,800 coins and 1,200 Delving XP.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Kill the Infernal Knight for the key and Hellforged Scrap, then clear both floors before the Tyrant. The armour needs Hellforged Ingots, which the bosses and chests here drop. Hellforged Scrap drops from the bosses and from Bone Giants. See [Mining](mining).

- Bring a lot of fire protection, food and a bow for the blazes.
- Dread Crystals in the chests are used for region gear. See [Danger regions](danger-regions).
- The Tyrant's Core is a trophy for the Ender Wyrm altar. See [Ender Wyrm](ender-wyrm).
