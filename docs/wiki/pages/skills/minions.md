A **Minion** works one block for you: every 30 seconds it mines its ore, chops its log or harvests its crop, and keeps the drops and **half the skill XP** until you collect them. It keeps working while you are away or offline (the server only has to be running) and holds up to **2 hours** of work (240 actions). After that it stops and puffs angry clouds until you empty it.

## Getting one

Craft it at the [Artisan Bench](crafting) (Minions tab): 2 Iron Blocks, 32 Redstone, 16 Copper Ingots and 8 Amethyst Shards. No skill level is needed.

You can have **one minion, plus one more per 10 levels of your best skill**, so six at level 50.

## Using it

1. Place it anywhere in the survival world.
2. Right-click it with the block it should work: an ore (Iron Ore, Diamond Ore...), a log, or seeds for a crop (Wheat Seeds, a Carrot, a Potato...). Anything that gives skill XP when you break it works. It shows little puffs of that block while it works.
3. Right-click it with an empty hand (or anything else) to collect: the items go into your inventory (or drop at your feet if it's full) and you get the XP. Chat shows what it made.
4. Right-click it with a different block to change its work; it hands over what it made first.

Only the owner can collect from a minion or break it. Breaking it collects everything first and gives the Minion back.

## Numbers

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Speed | one action every 30 seconds (120 an hour) |
| Storage | 240 actions (2 hours) |
| XP | half of what breaking the block yourself gives; Insight does not boost it |
| Drops | the block's normal drops, without Fortune |

An Iron Ore minion makes about 120 Raw Iron and 300 Mining XP an hour.
