Fishing is the skill of catching things. Every catch pays Fishing XP and may bring a **Tidescale** (the Fishing Master's currency) or a rare find, and sometimes the bite is a **sea creature** that you have to fight. A special rod even lets you fish in the Nether's lava. Level 50 is the cap (see [Skills overview](skills-overview)).

## How catching works

Fishing uses vanilla fishing, so the vanilla Lure and Luck of the Sea enchants still work on the rod. On top of the vanilla catch, SkyRPG adds:

- **Fishing XP:** 8 for every catch (14 in lava). The Fishing Insight stat adds 1% more XP per point.
- **Tidescales:** a 1 in 10 chance on every catch, multiplied by 1 + Fishing Fortune / 100.
- **Rare finds:** a {{item:skyrpg_fishing:abyssal_pearl}} with a 1 in 300 chance and a {{item:skyrpg_fishing:leviathan_scale}} with a 1 in 3,000 chance.
- **Double Catch:** gear, baits and talents give a chance that a catch comes up twice.
- **Sea creatures:** your Sea Creature Chance is the chance that a bite is a creature instead of a fish.

Each Fishing level gives +1 Fishing Speed and +1 Health. You never meet sea creatures in the hub.

## Fishing stats

| Stat | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Fishing Speed | Fish bite sooner. Every 25 points is one more level of Lure, up to four levels (100 points). |
| Sea Creature Chance | The chance that your line pulls up a sea creature instead of a fish. You start with 5%. |
| Fishing Fortune | More Tidescales from your catches. Every 100 is one more. |
| Fishing Insight | Each point is 1% more Fishing XP. |

## Tidescales

**{{item:skyrpg_fishing:tidescale|Tidescales}}** come from catches and from killing sea creatures. Nine make a **{{item:skyrpg_fishing:tidescale_plate}}** at a crafting table, and back again. **Marina Saltwick** (Saltwick Tackle, by the pond east of the hub square) takes them for gear and eggs.

## Sea creatures

When the bite is a creature, it is pulled out of the water at you and attacks. Killing it pays Fishing XP and Tidescales, and rolls the rare finds. It leaves after two minutes. Which ones can come up depends on your Fishing level, and the odds are by weight. Health and damage are multiples of the body's normal values.

| Creature | Fishing level | Weight | Health, damage | Fishing XP | Tidescales |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Drowned Sailor | 0 | 10 | x1.5, x1.0 | 25 | 1 |
| Brine Slime | 4 | 6 | x2.0, x1.0 | 35 | 2 |
| Reef Guardian | 10 | 6 | x1.5, x1.2 | 70 | 3 |
| Riptide Raider | 15 | 5 | x3.0, x1.6 | 110 | 4 |
| Night Skimmer | 20 | 3 | x3.0, x1.5 | 160 | 5 |
| Abyss Warden | 30 | 1 | x1.5, x1.2 | 900 | 20 |

The Abyss Warden is an Elder Guardian body and the rarest catch. The lava creatures are in the lava fishing section below.

> [!TIP]
> Your level only decides which creatures can come up. Sea Creature Chance decides how often one does, so rods, baits and the Deep Lure talent are how you meet more of them.

## Marina's shop

Marina Saltwick sells:

- The {{item:skyrpg_items:driftwood_rod}} (900 coins) and Worm Bait.
- The Angler set (700, 1,100, 950 and 650 coins), and the Reefwalker set for Tidescales (Fishing 10).
- Glowworm Bait (Fishing 10), 8 prismarine shards for 4 Tidescales (Fishing 5), a heart of the sea for 8 Tidescale Plates (Fishing 25).
- An {{item:skyrpg_pets:otter_egg}} for 6 Tidescale Plates (Fishing 10, see [Pets](pets)).

She buys fish, ink sacs, nautilus shells, prismarine and pearls. An Abyssal Pearl sells for 1,800 coins and a Leviathan Scale for 18,000.

## Lava fishing in the Nether

The {{item:skyrpg_items:magmaline_rod}} fishes in the Nether's lava. Hold it in your main or off hand, in the Nether, and cast into lava.

- Its bobber floats on lava like on water, cannot burn, and bites with the usual timing and your Fishing Speed.
- A lava catch goes straight into your inventory, because an item flung out of lava would burn.
- Lava catches give 14 Fishing XP instead of 8. Tidescales, Double Catch and your stats work the same.
- The only rare find in lava is a {{item:skyrpg_fishing:molten_pearl}} (1 in 250, sells for 2,500 coins).
- Lava sea creatures come up instead of water ones.

## Events and contracts

**Fishing Frenzy** is a five-minute event. Every catch scores 1 point and a sea creature scores 3. Fishing also has daily contracts (see [Contracts](contracts) and [Events](events)).
