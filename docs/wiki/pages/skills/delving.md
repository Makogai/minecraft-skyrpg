Delving is the skill of exploring dungeons. You earn Delving XP from opening dungeon chests, finding secrets, slaying champions and bosses, and discovering dungeons. Its levels give Health and Magic Find, and unlock the dungeon recipes at the [Artisan Bench](crafting). Level 50 is the cap, on the same curve as the other skills (see [Skills overview](skills-overview)).

## What each level gives

Every Delving level gives:

- +2 Health and +0.25 Magic Find.
- One talent point for the Delving talents.
- Every 5th level pays coins (level x 100).
- Unlocks for bench recipes (see below).

**Delving Insight** adds 1% Delving XP per point. It comes from the Delver set and Delver Pickaxe and the Scholar's Focus helmet enchant.

## Where Delving XP comes from

| Source | Delving XP |
| --- | --- |
| Discovering a dungeon (walking up to its entrance, once) | 100 plus 5 per dungeon level |
| Finding a secret (once each) | 150 |
| Opening a dungeon chest | Chest rate times the dungeon level (table below) |
| Slaying a champion | 400 to 6,000, by dungeon |
| Slaying a boss | 1,500 to 28,000, by dungeon |

Every chest is per player: each of you rolls your own loot and gets your own coins and XP, and the chest refills when the dungeon resets. The XP of a chest is the dungeon's level times its rate:

| Chest | XP per dungeon level | Coins per dungeon level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shrine chest | 2 | 6 |
| Common | 3.5 | 15 |
| Rare | 7 | 30 |
| Secret cache | 10 | 45 |
| Vault | 25 | 100 |

Coins vary by 20% either way. A vault in the Forgotten Crypt (dungeon level 12) pays about 1,200 coins and 300 XP.

### Bosses and champions

Every boss and champion pays Delving XP to everyone who took part in the kill, on every kill.

| Dungeon | Level | Champion (XP) | Boss (XP) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Forgotten Crypt](forgotten-crypt) | 12 | Bone Knight (400) | Crypt Warden (1,500) |
| [Spider Hollow](spider-hollow) | 22 | Silk Matron (650) | Queen Arachne (2,800) |
| [Drowned Temple](drowned-temple) | 28 | Abyssal Herald (900) | Tidemother (4,500) |
| [Dwarven Halls](dwarven-halls) | 34 | Forgeguard (1,300) | Iron Thane (7,000) |
| [Infernal Citadel](infernal-citadel) | 48 | Infernal Knight (3,000) | Magma Tyrant (16,000) |
| [Ruined Citadel](ruined-citadel) | 56 | Knight-Captain (3,600) | Fallen Knight (21,000) |
| [Catacombs](catacombs) | 62 | Bone Warden (3,800) | Bonelord (24,000) |
| [Astral Observatory](astral-observatory) | 64 | Astral Sentinel (6,000) | Star Warden (28,000) |

The boss's Reward Chest also rolls extra loot for what your party did on the run: packs cleared, secrets found and champions slain. Running straight to the boss skips that bonus. See [Dungeons overview](dungeons-overview).

> [!TIP]
> Dungeon discovery and secrets are one-time XP, but chests and bosses refill every reset. Clear everything on a run for the Reward Chest bonus, and the Delving XP for the chests is a bonus on top of the boss.

## Unlocks

Dungeon gear is made at the Artisan Bench from boss tokens, with no luck needed. Each recipe needs a Delving level.

| Delving | Recipes unlocked |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | {{item:skyrpg_items:cryptbreaker}} and the Warden's Shroud set |
| 12 | {{item:skyrpg_items:arachne_fang}}, {{item:skyrpg_items:widowsilk_cloak}} and the Carapace of Arachne set |
| 20 | {{item:skyrpg_items:thanes_hammer}}, the Runeforged set, {{item:skyrpg_items:tidecaller_blade}}, {{item:skyrpg_items:tidemother_pendant}} and the Tidemother Regalia set |
| 30 | The Hellforged set (from the Infernal Citadel's boss, using Hellforged Ingots from the Nether) |

The tokens come from the dungeon bosses: Warden Sigils from the Crypt Warden, Arachne Eyes from Queen Arachne, Tide Pearls from the Tidemother, Thane's Runes and Dwarven Cogs from the Dwarven Halls and the deep layers, and the {{item:skyrpg_world:tyrants_core}} from the Magma Tyrant. See [Weapons](weapons), [Armour sets](armour-sets) and [Accessories](accessories) for what each makes.

## Talents

You get one talent point per Delving level (K, Talents). A reset is free once a day, then costs 2,000 coins.

| Talent | Ranks | Needs | Per rank |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Delver's Vigor | 5 | Delving 0 | +5 Health |
| Delver's Luck | 5 | Delving 5 | +1 Magic Find |
| Crypt Hardened | 5 | Delving 10 | +3 Defense |
