Skills are how your character grows. You gain XP in a skill by doing what it is about (mining, chopping, fighting...), and each level gives permanent stats, a talent point and new things to craft and buy. There are seven skills, each capped at level 50. Open the SkyRPG menu with K for the Skills page, or type `/skills`.

## The seven skills

| Skill | You gain XP from | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farming | Harvesting fully grown crops, melons, pumpkins, sugar cane and more | [Farming](farming) |
| Mining | Breaking stone and ores (rarer ores give more) | [Mining](mining) |
| Foraging | Chopping logs and stumps | [Foraging](foraging) |
| Combat | Killing monsters (tougher ones give more) | [Combat basics](combat-basics) |
| Fishing | Catching fish and killing sea creatures | [Fishing](fishing) |
| Enchanting | Binding enchants, applying and combining tomes, filling the Codex | [Enchanting](enchanting) |
| Delving | Dungeon chests, secrets, champions, bosses and discoveries | [Delving](delving) |

**Slayer levels** are separate: each slayer boss (Grave Tyrant, Bone Marshal, Broodmother, Void Stalker) has its own level that you raise by killing that boss. See [Slayers](slayers).

## How XP and levels work

All seven skills use the same curve. The table shows the XP needed to reach each level in total.

| Level | Total XP | Level | Total XP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 50 | 30 | 252,700 |
| 5 | 775 | 35 | 456,200 |
| 10 | 4,000 | 40 | 758,700 |
| 15 | 15,500 | 45 | 1,217,700 |
| 20 | 50,200 | 50 | 2,017,700 |
| 25 | 121,700 | | |

Reaching level 10 takes 1,000 more XP than level 9, and the last step (49 to 50) takes 195,000. The curve is steep on purpose. Level 50 is the cap, and the action bar shows "MAX" once you get there.

- **Seeing it:** every XP gain shows above the hotbar, for example "+4 Farming (54/125)", and a level up shows a notice in chat with your rewards, talent point and new recipes.
- **Insight stats:** a skill's Insight stat gives that many percent more XP, so 20 Farming Insight is +20% Farming XP. Combat, Mining, Farming, Foraging and Fishing each have one. They come from gear (the Harvester set, the Woodsman set, the Moonlit Reaper...), talents, meals and the Scholar's Focus helmet enchant (+6 in every skill at the top level).
- **Pets:** your active pet earns 25% of the XP you earn in its skill, and 5% from the other skills (see [Pets](pets)).
- **No wasted XP:** blocks you placed yourself give no XP (see [Crafting](crafting)).

## What every level gives

Every level of every skill gives all of these:

1. **Stats**, listed per skill below. They are permanent and stack with gear.
2. **A talent point** for that skill (see below).
3. **New recipes and shop offers**: the Masters' shops and the Artisan Bench unlock things at set levels, and the level-up box lists the new recipes.
4. **SkyRPG XP:** each skill level adds 5 SkyRPG XP towards your SkyRPG Level (see [Collections](collections)).
5. **Coins every 5th level** (see below).

| Skill | Stats per level | Stats at level 50 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farming | +4 Farming Fortune, +2 Health | +200 Farming Fortune, +100 Health |
| Mining | +4 Mining Fortune, +1 Defense | +200 Mining Fortune, +50 Defense |
| Foraging | +4 Foraging Fortune, +1 Strength | +200 Foraging Fortune, +50 Strength |
| Combat | +0.5% Crit Chance, +1 Health | +25% Crit Chance, +50 Health |
| Fishing | +1 Fishing Speed, +1 Health | +50 Fishing Speed, +50 Health |
| Enchanting | +1 Intelligence, +0.2 Magic Find | +50 Intelligence, +10 Magic Find |
| Delving | +2 Health, +0.25 Magic Find | +100 Health, +12.5 Magic Find |

### Milestone coins

Every 5th level pays coins equal to the level times 100: 500 coins at level 5, 1,000 at level 10, up to 5,000 at level 50. That is 27,500 coins per skill over its ten milestones.

## Talents

Every skill level gives one **talent point** for that skill, so a maxed skill has 50 points. Spend them in the SkyRPG menu (K, Talents) on that skill's perks. Most talents have several ranks and cost one point per rank, and the stronger ones unlock at a skill level. Talents give stats and the same gear effects as items do, so they stack with gear. You can have a talent point left over, and there is no deadline to spend it.

A reset gives all of a skill's points back. It is free once a day, then costs 2,000 coins.

| Skill | Talents | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farming | 8 | More Dewdrops, faster planters, no trampling, replanting with any tool, double harvests |
| Mining | 8 | More Glimmer, faster nodes, better rare finds, Vein Echo, double ore, Night Vision deep down |
| Foraging | 10 | More Heartwood Splinters, faster stumps, double logs, safer falls, rare-find luck |
| Combat | 6 | Strength, Crit Chance and Damage, Defense, Health, and Second Wind (survive a killing blow once every 5 minutes) |
| Fishing | 7 | Faster bites, more sea creatures, more Tidescales, Double Hook |
| Enchanting | 3 | Intelligence, more tomes, Magic Find |
| Delving | 3 | Health, Magic Find, Defense |

The full trees are on each skill's page: [Farming](farming), [Mining](mining), [Foraging](foraging), [Fishing](fishing), [Enchanting](enchanting) and [Delving](delving).

> [!TIP]
> Put your first points in the talents with no level requirement (the +Fortune and +Speed ones), then pick up the rare-find and double-drop talents as they unlock. The big ones appear at levels 20 to 30.

## Combat XP

Combat XP comes from kills, and tougher mobs pay more: 6 for a zombie, skeleton or husk, 5 for a spider, 8 for a creeper, 10 for a witch or blaze, 12 for an enderman, 15 for a wither skeleton or ghast, 40 for a ravager, 100 for an Elder Guardian, 500 for a Warden, 1,000 for the Wither and 2,000 for the Ender Dragon. Farm animals give 1. See [Combat basics](combat-basics) and [Mobs](mobs).

## Gear and levels

The Masters in the hub (Tamsin, Mabel, Oswin, Marina and Kestrel) sell gear and eggs at set levels, and the [Artisan Bench](crafting) has recipes at levels 8, 18, 26, 35 and 45 for each skill. [Armour sets](armour-sets), [Weapons](weapons) and [Tools](tools) list what each level unlocks.
