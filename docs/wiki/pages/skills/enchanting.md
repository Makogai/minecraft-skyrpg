SkyRPG replaces vanilla's enchanting table with its own enchants: 71 of them, from simple stat boosts to effects like lightning and cheating death. You bind them at **Arcane Enchanting**, find them as **tomes**, and every bind levels the **Enchanting** skill. This page covers how it all works. The full enchant list is on [Enchant list](enchant-list). Vanilla enchanted books from loot and the anvil still work as before.

## Where to enchant

- **Seraphine Vael**, the Enchanter in the tower in the hub. Her library counts as 15 bookshelves, and you can stay within 12 blocks of her.
- **Any enchanting table.** Right-click it to open the same screen. Stay within 8 blocks of the table.

### Table power

A table's **power** is the number of bookshelves around it (vanilla's rule: two blocks out, with air between), up to 15. Higher enchant levels need power:

| Enchant level | I | II | III | IV | V |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bookshelves needed | 0 | 4 | 8 | 12 | 15 |

A table only offers up to level V. Higher levels (some enchants go to VI or VII) only come from tomes.

## The Arcane Enchanting screen

A violet window with an altar in the middle: two rune rings turn against each other and light bursts when something is bound. It has three tabs.

- **Enchant:** your enchantable gear on the left (worn armour first, then the held item, hotbar, bag and offhand), the item on the altar with what it already holds, and the enchants the table offers for it on the right. Pick an enchant and a level, then **Bind**. Locked levels say why (bookshelves, or your Enchanting level).
- **Tomes:** your tomes on the left. For the chosen tome you can **Combine** it with an identical one, **Salvage** it into Runic Dust, or **Apply** it to any item you carry that takes it. Each row shows the change, for example "II to Keen III", and the cost.
- **Codex:** every enchant there is, filtered by weapons, bows, armour, tools, fishing and ultimates, with what each does at its highest level and where it comes from. Hover for every level and the conflicts. Tome-only enchants you have not seen yet show as runes.

Replacing something (a conflicting enchant, or another ultimate) needs a second click. The footer shows your coins, XP levels, Lapis, Runic Dust and Enchanting level.

## What can be enchanted

Each enchant goes on a kind of item: swords, bows and crossbows, armour (any piece or one slot), pickaxes, axes, hoes, any tool, fishing rods or wands. Only single items can be enchanted, never a stack. Armour enchants work in their own slot, and everything else works while held in your main hand. An item with enchants glints.

- **Conflicts:** Keen, Hallowed Edge and Webcutter exclude each other.
- **Ultimates:** an item holds only one ultimate.

## Binding costs

An enchant costs coins, XP levels, Lapis and, for the rarer ones, Runic Dust and Prism Fragments. Each level is paid in full, not as a difference. Materials are taken from your backpacks too.

| Rarity | Coins, level I to V | XP levels, I to V | Enchanting level needed, I to V | Other materials |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 250, 800, 2,000, 4,500, 9,000 | 2, 4, 7, 10, 14 | 0, 0, 2, 4, 6 | Lapis 2 to 16 |
| Uncommon | 500, 1,600, 4,000, 9,000, 18,000 | 3, 6, 9, 13, 18 | 1, 3, 5, 8, 11 | Lapis 4 to 24 |
| Rare | 1,500, 4,500, 10,000, 22,000, 40,000 | 5, 9, 13, 18, 24 | 4, 7, 10, 13, 16 | Lapis 8 to 32, Runic Dust 2 to 8 from III |
| Epic | 5,000, 15,000, 35,000, 70,000, 120,000 | 8, 12, 17, 23, 30 | 10, 13, 16, 19, 22 | Lapis 12 to 48, Runic Dust 2 to 32 |
| Legendary | 15,000, 40,000, 90,000, 160,000, 300,000 | 12, 17, 23, 30, 40 | 16, 20, 24, 28, 32 | Lapis 16 to 64, Runic Dust 4 to 64, Prism Fragments 1, 2 and 4 from III |

Reseeding has its own price (4,000 coins, 8 XP levels, Enchanting 5, 12 Lapis and 16 Dewdrops).

## Tomes

A tome is an item that holds one enchant at one level. Apply it at the screen to put that enchant on an item.

- **Applying** gives the tome's level if it is higher than the item's. A tome of the same level as the item's raises it by one, up to the enchant's maximum. It costs coins (300 for Common up to 25,000 for an Ultimate, times the level it ends at) and 3 XP levels per level.
- **Combining** two identical tomes (same enchant, same level) makes one a level higher. This is the only way past the table's level V. It costs coins (150 to 10,000 times the new level squared) and, from Uncommon up, Runic Dust.
- **Salvaging** a tome gives {{item:skyrpg_enchants:runic_dust}}: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or 40 by rarity (Common to Ultimate), doubled for every level above I. Runic Dust sells for 80 coins, but the best binds and merges ask for it.

### Where tomes come from

- **Hostile mobs:** about 0.5% per kill, a little more for tougher mobs (up to 2%), times Magic Find and the Tome Hunter talent. They are combat enchants of levels I to III, mostly Common. Spawner mobs never drop them.
- **Gathering:** while you gain XP in a skill, there is a small chance of a tome for that skill's gear: mining 0.06%, foraging 0.06%, farming 0.04%, fishing 1% per catch. The skill's ultimate is much rarer (mining and foraging 0.003%, farming 0.002%, fishing 0.06%).
- **Slayer bosses and the world boss:** each drops its own enchants, with a higher tier giving better odds.
- **Dungeon bosses and champions:** each dungeon boss and champion has its own tome drops.

| Boss | Tomes it can drop |
| --- | --- |
| Grave Tyrant | Hallowed Edge, Soul Siphon, **Soulrend** |
| Broodmother | Webcutter, Venom Coat, **Final Stand** |
| Bone Marshal | Deadeye, Splitshot, **Starshot** |
| Void Stalker | Keen, Stormcaller, **Rift Echo** |
| World boss | Stormcaller, Momentum, Titanbane, **Phoenix Ember** |

Ultimates (in bold) only drop at slayer tier III and above. See [Slayers](slayers) and [World bosses](world-bosses). Epic and better finds are announced to everyone.

## Ultimates

An ultimate is the top enchant for its slot, and an item holds only one. They come as tomes only.

| Ultimate | Goes on | Effect at level V |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soulrend | Swords | Each kill binds a soul for 20 seconds (up to 10), and every soul adds 3% damage |
| Rift Echo | Swords | Every fifth hit tears the air, and a moment later the target is struck again for 80% of it |
| Starshot | Bows | Every fourth arrow becomes a falling star bursting for 150% of its damage within 4 blocks |
| Final Stand | Armour | Below 40% health, take 12% less damage (all pieces add up, to 60% at most) |
| Phoenix Ember | Chestplates | Once every 180 seconds, a killing blow sets you alight instead and you rise with 40% health |
| Motherlode | Pickaxes | 0.8% chance per ore of +200 Mining Fortune for 15 seconds (see [Mining](mining)) |
| Heartwood Surge | Axes | 0.8% chance per log of +200 Foraging Fortune for 15 seconds (see [Foraging](foraging)) |
| Bountiful | Hoes | 0.7% chance per crop of +200 Farming Fortune for 15 seconds (see [Farming](farming)) |
| Leviathan's Favor | Rods | 8% chance per catch of +20% Sea Creature Chance for 30 seconds (see [Fishing](fishing)) |

The four gathering ultimates are very rare finds while you mine, forage, farm or fish. The gathering boosts fire at most once every 30 seconds.

## The Enchanting skill

Enchanting is a skill with 50 levels on the same curve as the others. Each level gives +1 Intelligence and +0.2 Magic Find. It also gates the higher enchant levels (see the cost table).

| Action | Enchanting XP |
| --- | --- |
| Each XP level spent | 20 |
| Each 1,000 coins spent at the table | 8 |
| Each level of a tome applied | 60 |
| Each level of a tome combined | 80 |
| Each level of a tome salvaged | 10 |
| First discovery of an enchant (Common to Ultimate) | 150, 300, 600, 1,200, 2,500, 5,000 |

| Talent | Ranks | Needs | Per rank |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arcane Mind | 5 | Enchanting 0 | +4 Intelligence |
| Tome Hunter | 5 | Enchanting 3 | Tomes 8% more likely |
| Seeker's Eye | 5 | Enchanting 8 | +1 Magic Find |

## The Codex

The Codex records every enchant you have had on an item in your bag or as a tome. The first time you find one you get a chat line, a "CODEX DISCOVERY" banner and the XP above. Your bags are checked every five seconds.

> [!TIP]
> Chase first discoveries early. An Ultimate tome in your bag is worth 5,000 Enchanting XP the moment the Codex notices it, even before you use it.
