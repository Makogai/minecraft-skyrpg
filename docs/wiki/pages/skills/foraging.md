Foraging is the skill of chopping trees. SkyRPG adds **five hard woods** that need better axes and only grow where their conditions hold, **stumps** you can place for a steady trickle of XP, **Heartwood Splinters** as a currency, and a ladder of axes whose **Treefeller** ability fells a whole tree in one chop. Level 50 is the cap (see [Skills overview](skills-overview)).

## Where Foraging XP comes from

Every natural log you chop gives Foraging XP. Logs you placed yourself give nothing extra, and Treefeller never fells placed logs, so your log cabin is safe.

| Log or stump | Foraging XP |
| --- | --- |
| Any vanilla log | 6 |
| Ironbark log | 10 |
| Mistpine log | 14 |
| Duskwillow log | 18 |
| Emberwood log | 24 |
| Starwood log | 40 |
| Oak, Birch or Spruce stump | 1.5 |
| Ironbark stump | 3 |
| Mistpine stump | 4 |
| Duskwillow stump | 6 |
| Emberwood stump | 8 |

The **Foraging Insight** stat adds 1% more Foraging XP per point. Each Foraging level gives +4 Foraging Fortune and +1 Strength.

**Foraging Fortune** drops a log's normal drops again: one extra copy for every full 100 Fortune, plus a chance of one more from the remainder.

## The five woods

Each wood has logs, planks (they work in every vanilla wood recipe), leaves and a sapling that only grows where its condition holds. Bone meal does not work on them. Hard logs need an axe of at least the listed tier, the same way ores need pickaxes (see the tier list below).

| Wood | Axe tier | Sapling grows | Sapling from | Log sells for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:ironbark_log\|Ironbark}} | III | Anywhere with enough light | Oak logs (1 in 200), Oswin | 4 |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:mistpine_log\|Mistpine}} | IV | In a cold biome, or above y 120 | Spruce logs in mountains (1 in 125) or taigas (1 in 250), Oswin | 6 |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:duskwillow_log\|Duskwillow}} | V | Within 4 blocks of water, and only at night | Mangrove logs (1 in 167), oak logs in swamps (1 in 125), Oswin | 8 |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:emberwood_log\|Emberwood}} | VI | Within 6 blocks of lava, or in the Nether | Crimson stems (1 in 250), a ripe Emberpepper (1 in 250), Oswin | 11 |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:starwood_log\|Starwood}} | VII | Under open sky, and only at night | The bench: 2 Golden Acorns and 4 Heartwood Bundles (Foraging 45) | 18 |

Saplings: {{item:skyrpg_foraging:ironbark_sapling}}, {{item:skyrpg_foraging:mistpine_sapling}}, {{item:skyrpg_foraging:duskwillow_sapling}}, {{item:skyrpg_foraging:emberwood_sapling}}, {{item:skyrpg_foraging:starwood_sapling}}. Sapling drops come from chopping logs, not stumps, and the Lucky Bough talent makes them likelier. Emberwood is fireproof and its leaves glow, and Starwood leaves shine.

### Wild trees

All five also grow naturally, in chunks generated after they were added:

- **Ironbark** in forests.
- **Mistpine** in snowy taigas, groves, snowy slopes, snowy plains, windswept forests and windswept hills.
- **Duskwillow** in swamps and mangrove swamps.
- **Emberwood** in badlands, savanna plateaus and windswept savannas, and in the Nether's crimson forests and wastes.
- **Starwood**, the rarest, in cherry groves, meadows and flower forests.

### Axe tiers

An axe has a tier, shown in its tooltip. A log that needs a higher tier does not chop at all, and the action bar says what you need. Vanilla axes: wooden and golden I, stone and copper II, iron III, diamond IV, netherite V. A Woodsman Axe is only tier II, so you need an iron axe or better for your first Ironbark.

## Heartwood

**{{item:skyrpg_foraging:heartwood_splinter|Heartwood Splinters}}** are the Foraging Master's currency. Chopping gives a splinter 10% of the time (50% from a stump), multiplied by 1 + Foraging Fortune / 100. Nine splinters make a **{{item:skyrpg_foraging:heartwood_bundle}}** at a crafting table, and back again.

## Stumps

Stumps are placeable foraging nodes you buy from Oswin. Chop one for its log, a little XP and a splinter chance, then it regrows. Sneak and hit it with an empty hand to pick it up. The harder stumps need the same axe tier as their logs.

| Stump | Needs Foraging | Cost at Oswin | Regrows in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:oak_stump\|Oak}} | 3 | 24 Heartwood Splinters | 30 s |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:birch_stump\|Birch}} | 6 | 32 Heartwood Splinters | 30 s |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:spruce_stump\|Spruce}} | 6 | 32 Heartwood Splinters | 30 s |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:ironbark_stump\|Ironbark}} | 14 | 6 Bundles, 16 Ironbark Logs | 60 s |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:mistpine_stump\|Mistpine}} | 22 | 10 Bundles, 16 Mistpine Logs | 90 s |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:duskwillow_stump\|Duskwillow}} | 30 | 16 Bundles, 16 Duskwillow Logs | 120 s |
| {{item:skyrpg_foraging:emberwood_stump\|Emberwood}} | 38 | 24 Bundles, 16 Emberwood Logs | 180 s |

The Stump Whisperer talent shortens regrowth by 6% per rank (never by more than half). Stumps are a steady trickle of splinters, not the way to level.

## Rare finds

Every log you chop rolls for two rare finds, announced with "RARE FIND!":

- A {{item:skyrpg_foraging:golden_acorn}} with a 1 in 500 chance (sells for 2,500 coins).
- {{item:skyrpg_foraging:elder_heartwood}} with a 1 in 5,000 chance (sells for 25,000 coins).

Stumps are three times rarer. Gear, talents and enchants that say "rare finds 10% more likely" raise the odds, and +100% means twice as likely. The best axes and armour need them.

## The Foraging Master

**Oswin Fernhollow** runs Fernhollow Timber in the hub.

- The Woodsman set (600, 950, 850 and 550 coins) and the {{item:skyrpg_items:woodsman_axe}} (1,300 coins).
- The Barkguard set from Foraging 8 (1,600, 2,600, 2,300 and 1,300 coins).
- Ironbark Saplings (Foraging 8, 12 splinters), Mistpine (18, 2 bundles), Duskwillow (26, 4 bundles) and Emberwood (35, 8 bundles).
- Every stump in the table above.
- Vanilla saplings and logs, an {{item:skyrpg_pets:owl_egg}} for 6 Heartwood Bundles (Foraging 10, see [Pets](pets)).
- He buys logs and stems of every kind, apples and Golden Acorns.

## Axes and Treefeller

Axes are made at the Artisan Bench from the one before, and the new axe keeps the old one's name and enchantments. The first two steps also need Collection of their wood (Ironbark Collection II for the Hatchet, Mistpine Collection III for the Splitter). See [Tools](tools) for the full stats.

| Axe | Tier | Needs | Ingredients besides the previous axe | Treefeller cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| {{item:skyrpg_items:woodsman_axe}} | II | Crafting table | Logs and sticks | None |
| {{item:skyrpg_items:ironbark_hatchet}} | IV | Foraging 8 | 32 Ironbark Logs, 16 Splinters | 60 s |
| {{item:skyrpg_items:mistpine_splitter}} | V | Foraging 18 | 48 Mistpine Logs, 4 Bundles | 45 s |
| {{item:skyrpg_items:willowcleaver}} | VI | Foraging 26 | 48 Duskwillow Logs, 8 Bundles | 30 s |
| {{item:skyrpg_items:emberbrand_axe}} | VII | Foraging 35 | 48 Emberwood Logs, 12 Bundles, 8 Emberite Ingots | 20 s |
| {{item:skyrpg_items:starwood_greataxe}} | VII | Foraging 45 | 32 Starwood Logs, 16 Bundles, 2 Golden Acorns, 1 Elder Heartwood | 12 s |

**Treefeller** is the right-click ability of every axe above the Woodsman Axe. For 10 seconds, the next natural log you chop fells the whole tree (up to 200 logs, each counting as chopped for XP, splinters, rare finds and contracts). Better axes add more:

- Mistpine Splitter and up: the leaves drop at once, saplings and all.
- Willowcleaver and up: the sapling is replanted where the tree stood.
- Emberbrand: 15% chance a log drops twice. Starwood Greataxe: 25%.

> [!TIP]
> Use Treefeller on a Starwood tree for 40 XP a log, and let the double-log chance and Foraging Fortune stack on every log of the tree.

## Events and contracts

**Timber Rush** is a five-minute event where every log you chop is a point. Foraging also has daily contracts (see [Contracts](contracts) and [Events](events)).
