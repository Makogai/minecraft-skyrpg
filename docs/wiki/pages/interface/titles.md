A **title** is a small tag, an icon and a few letters in a colour, that shows before your name in chat and next to it in the Tab list: `[* Deep Miner] Name: hello`. You earn titles by doing things in the game, from reaching a skill level to defeating the Ender Wyrm. Once earned a title is yours for good. You wear one at a time.

## Earning titles

Titles unlock by themselves. The game checks your progress every couple of seconds, so a title you already qualify for is awarded the first time you log in after the update: nothing you did earlier is lost. When you earn one you hear a chime and get a `TITLE UNLOCKED` message in chat with a **[Equip]** button. Your very first title is worn automatically.

A title's colour shows how hard it is: grey is common, green uncommon, blue rare, purple epic, gold legendary and pink mythic. A few have their own colour.

Two titles are **hidden**: they show as `???` on the Titles page until you earn them.

## Choosing your title

- **K menu, Titles page.** Every title is listed. Click an earned one to wear it, click the one you are wearing to take it off, or use **Take off title**. The tabs filter All, Earned and Locked. Locked titles are dimmed and say how to earn them, with a progress bar where the goal is a number (for example `4 / 25`). Hover a row for the full text.
- **Commands.** `/title` lists your earned titles with an **[Equip]** button, `/title locked` lists the rest, `/title set <id>` wears one (press Tab to complete the id, or click the button) and `/title clear` takes it off.

## Where it shows

- **Chat:** between the level badge and your name, as a small pill: `(* 42) [* Deep Miner] Name`. Your name stays white.
- **Tab list:** a pill after your name, in the title's colour. A long title is cut with "...".
- **Name card:** hover a name in chat to see the title on the card.

An administrator can also give a player a **staff title** with `/chat title` (see [Chat](chat)). A staff title **replaces** the earned one while it is set, and the earned titles stay saved underneath.
