Press **K** and one window opens with everything the pack adds: your character, skills, quests, pets, collections and settings. It has a sidebar of pages on the left and a dark custom look instead of vanilla screens. This page lists every page, in the order they appear.

## Using the menu

| Input | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **K** | Open the menu, on the page you last used. Press again to close. |
| **Esc** | Step back inside a page, for example clear a search. Closes the menu when there is nowhere left to go back. |
| Up and Down arrows | Previous or next page |
| **1** to **9** | Jump to one of the first nine pages |
| Click a page in the sidebar | Go there |
| Scroll wheel | Scroll the page |

A page with a text field, such as Pay, Party or Settings, takes digits and letters first.

The window fits itself to your screen. **Settings, Interface, Menu size** lets you pick Auto, Compact or Large. See [Settings](settings).

## The pages

| Page | Order | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile | 1 | Your character and a tile for each stat |
| Stats | 2 | Every stat, grouped, with where each bonus comes from |
| Skills | 3 | A card for each skill |
| Talents | 4 | Spend talent points |
| Slayers | 5 | Your slayer lines |
| Contracts | 6 | Today's three shared contracts |
| Journal | 7 | The Skyhaven Chronicle |
| Pets | 8 | Your pet and your pet storage |
| Mounts | 9 | Your griffins and your stable |
| Party | 10 | Your party: members, health, where they are, invites |
| Collections | 11 | Gathered items, tiers and the SkyRPG Level |
| Titles | 12 | Earn titles and wear one before your name |
| Brainrots | 13 | Your brainrot album (also on **J**) |
| Pay | 14 | Send coins to another player |
| Settings | last | Every option |

The order shown is the order in the sidebar. Slayers and Contracts sit very close together and may appear the other way round.

### Profile

The first page. Your character is on the left. On the right is a tile for each thing the mods register:

- **Purse** and **Bank**, the coins you carry and the coins you keep at the bank.
- **SkyRPG Level**, shown in aqua, which is also the badge in chat.
- **Skill Average**.
- One tile for every stat: Health, Defense, Strength, Crit Chance and so on.

Tiles fill one to three columns, depending on the window, and scroll when there are more than fit. A number too long for its tile is shortened, such as `1.23B`. Hover to see it in full. See [Collections](collections) and [Coins and the bank](coins-and-bank).

### Stats

Every stat as a tile, in four groups:

| Group | Stats |
| --- | --- |
| Combat | Health, Defense, Strength, Crit Chance, Crit Damage, Speed, Intelligence |
| Gathering | Mining Speed, Mining Fortune, Farming Fortune, Foraging Fortune, Fishing Speed, Sea Creature Chance, Fishing Fortune |
| Wisdom | Insight for all seven skills |
| Other | Magic Find |

Hover a tile to see what the stat does and where your bonuses come from: skills, gear, set bonuses, accessories, pets, collections and talents. See [Stats](stats).

### Skills

A card for every skill with your level, an animated progress bar and your XP. Hover for details. See [Skills overview](skills-overview).

### Talents

A tab for each skill. Every skill level gives one **talent point**. Spend points on that skill's talents by clicking a card. Each card shows its ranks, what each rank gives and what it needs.

**Reset** gives the skill's points back. The first reset each day is free. After that it costs 2,000 coins. See [Skills overview](skills-overview).

### Slayers

The overview has a card for each slayer line with your level, an XP bar, your boss kills per tier and any quest you are running. Click a line to see its level track (what each level gives and unlocks), the four tiers (the boss, cost, rewards, attacks and defences) and your own stats. See [Slayers](slayers).

### Contracts

Today's three contracts with progress bars and rewards. Items to hand in have a hand-in button, and untouched contracts have a **Reroll** button. See [Contracts](contracts).

### Journal

The Skyhaven Chronicle. Chapters on the left, the open quest on the right, with **Track** and **Re-read** buttons. See [Quests and the Journal](quests-and-journal).

### Pets

A live 3D preview of the selected pet, with its rarity, name, level and XP bar. On the right are its skill, stats and perks, then your **pet storage**, shown as `12 / 30`. Click a pet to select it, then:

- **Equip** puts it out. The old one goes to storage.
- **Unequip** puts it away.
- **Take out** moves it into your inventory as an item, so you can trade it.

See [Pets](pets).

### Mounts

A live 3D preview of the selected griffin, with its colour, name, Speed and Flight Stamina. Below is **Your stable**, shown as `4 / 20`. Select a mount, then **Call**, **Put away** or **Take out**. See [Mounts](mounts).

### Party

Your party's members, each on a card with their face, a crown for the leader, online or offline, health and where they are. Click a member to set a waypoint to them. The leader can **Promote** and **Kick**. There are buttons for **Party chat**, **Leave** and **Disband**, a name field to **Invite** a player, and your pending invites with **Accept** and **Deny**. See [Party](party).

### Collections

Tabs for Mining, Farming, Foraging, Fishing and Combat. Each row shows an item's icon, your count, your tier, a bar to the next tier and what that tier gives. Hover a row for all nine tiers. See [Collections](collections).

### Titles

Every title in the game. Earned ones are bright: click one to wear it, click it again to take it off. Locked ones are dimmed, say how to earn them and show a bar where the goal is a number. Tabs filter All, Earned and Locked. See [Titles](titles).

### Brainrots

Your brainrot album: a card for each of the fifteen, with the 3D model of the ones you've caught and a **?** for the rest. Click a card to see it up close and hear it say its name. **J** opens this page straight away. See [Brainrots](brainrots).

### Pay

Pick an online player, type an amount or tap a preset, and send. Big payments take a second click. See [Coins and the bank](coins-and-bank).

### Settings

Every option, grouped by category, with a search box. See [Settings](settings).

## Other screens

These open from NPCs and blocks rather than the K menu, but use the same look:

- **Shops** and the **bank**, by right-clicking a keeper. See [Selling and shops](selling-and-shops).
- **Arcane Enchanting**, by Seraphine or an enchanting table. See [Enchanting](enchanting).
- **The Artisan Bench**, by right-clicking one. See [Crafting](crafting).
- **Conversations**, by right-clicking an NPC with a quest. See [Quests and the Journal](quests-and-journal).

## The Wiki

This wiki is also inside the game. Look for **Wiki** in the menu, or type `/wiki`. Hover an item in any inventory and press **H** to jump to its entry. See [Controls and keys](controls-and-keys).

> [!TIP]
> The menu remembers the last page you used. Press K twice to go back to where you were.
