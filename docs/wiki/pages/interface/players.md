The **Players** page of the [K menu](k-menu) shows everyone who has ever played on the server and their statistics. Use it to check your own numbers, look up a friend, or see who has mined the most.

## The player list

The list on the left has everyone who has played on the server. You are at the top, then everyone online, then everyone else by playtime. Each row shows the player's face, their name and their playtime. A green dot means they are online now. Offline faces are a little dimmer.

Click a player to open their page. **Up** and **Down** move through the list. In a narrow window the list shows faces only. Hover a face to see the name.

## A player's page

At the top is the player's face and name, whether they are online or when they were last seen, and when they were first seen.

Under that are five headline tiles: **Playtime**, **Distance travelled**, **Blocks mined**, **Mobs killed** and **Deaths**. When more than one player has played, each tile has a rank chip such as **#1**. It is gold when the player is first.

Below the tiles, every statistic is listed by section:

| Section | Statistics |
| --- | --- |
| SkyRPG | SkyRPG Level, Skill Average, Purse, Bank |
| Skills | Your level in each skill |
| Time & Life | Playtime, sessions, time since the last death, nights slept, fish caught, animals bred, villager trades, items enchanted, chests and barrels opened, cake slices eaten |
| Travel | Total distance, walked, sprinted, crouched, swum, climbed, fallen, glided with an elytra, flown, by boat, riding (horses, pigs, striders, happy ghasts, nautiluses), by minecart, jumps |
| Mining & Building | Blocks mined, blocks placed, items crafted, tools worn out, items picked up, items dropped |
| Combat | Mobs killed, players killed, deaths, damage dealt, damage taken, damage blocked with a shield, raids won |

At the bottom are rows of item slots: **Most mined**, **Most crafted**, **Most killed** and **Killed by**. Each row has up to 12 entries with a count in the corner. Hover a slot for the name and the exact count.

## Comparing players

Hover any number, on a tile or in a section, to see a ranking of every player on that statistic. The player whose page you are on is highlighted. Use it to find out who has the most playtime, who has walked the furthest or who dies the most.

## Where the numbers come from

Most numbers are Minecraft's own statistics, the same ones as in the vanilla Statistics screen, added up. Distance travelled adds together every way of moving. Blocks placed counts every block you placed. The SkyRPG and Skills sections come from the mods.

The page refreshes every few seconds while it is open, so online players' numbers keep counting up. For a player who is offline, the page shows their numbers from when they last left. Numbers are also saved every five minutes while a player is online, so a server crash loses at most five minutes.

A player who last played before this page was added shows only their Minecraft statistics. Their SkyRPG numbers and skin appear after they next join.
