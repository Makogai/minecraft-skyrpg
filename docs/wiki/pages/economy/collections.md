Collections count how much of each item you have gathered from the world. Every collection has nine tiers, and each tier pays coins and SkyRPG XP. Some tiers also give a small permanent stat, and some unlock Artisan Bench recipes. The **SkyRPG Level** adds up everything you do in the game, and it is the number other players see next to your name in chat.

Open the page with **K**, then **Collections**.

## How collections count

A collection counts items you **gain from the world**:

- Normal pickups from the ground, as long as no player threw or dropped them. Items you drop on death and pick up again do not count twice.
- Blocks you mine with auto-pickup, and fishing and lava-fishing catches.
- Harvests, logs and catches handled by the farming, foraging and fishing mods.

Items you buy, craft or take out of a chest do **not** count, and neither do items another player drops or throws to you.

There are **63 collections** in five categories: Mining, Farming, Foraging, Fishing and Combat.

## Tiers and rewards

Every collection has nine tiers, I to IX. Reaching a tier gives:

- **Coins.** The amount depends on the collection. Rarer items pay more.
- **4 SkyRPG XP.**
- **A stat** at tier V (+1) and tier IX (+2 more), in the category's fortune stat for gathering collections. See the tables below.
- **Recipes**, for some collections. A locked recipe at the Artisan Bench shows "Requires Coal Collection III".

You get a notice and a sound when you reach a tier. Hover a row on the page to see all nine tiers and what each gives.

### How big are the tiers?

Common items need a lot. Rare items need a little. As an example, the Wheat collection looks like this.

| Tier | Wheat gathered | Coins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 100 | 100 |
| II | 200 | 250 |
| III | 500 | 500 |
| IV | 2,000 | 1,000 |
| V | 5,000 | 2,000 |
| VI | 10,000 | 4,000 |
| VII | 20,000 | 8,000 |
| VIII | 50,000 | 15,000 |
| IX | 100,000 | 30,000 |

Each collection follows the same shape. Stone is the cheapest (50 coins at tier I, 30,425 in all) and Leviathan Scale the dearest (400 coins at tier I, 243,400 in all).

### Stats from collections

| Category | Stat at tier V, then tier IX |
| --- | --- |
| Mining | +1, then +2 Mining Fortune |
| Farming | +1, then +2 Farming Fortune |
| Foraging | +1, then +2 Foraging Fortune |
| Fishing | +1, then +2 Fishing Fortune |
| Combat | +1, then +2 of the stat in the table below |

The two steps add up, so a finished collection gives +3 of its stat.

## The SkyRPG Level

The SkyRPG Level is your overall progress. It has **no cap**, and it is **100 SkyRPG XP for every level**.

### Where SkyRPG XP comes from

| Source | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Each collection tier | 4 |
| Each skill level | 5 |
| Each slayer level | 10 |
| First clear of a dungeon (its champion's first kill) | 20 to 60, by how tough the dungeon is |
| First kill of any other boss | 15 |
| Quest rewards | Varies by quest |

The dungeon bonus is 20 for a champion below level 25, 30 below 40, 40 below 52, 50 below 62 and 60 from there. It is paid once for each boss, per player.

The most SkyRPG Level a player can reach, and how much of it each source gives, is on [SkyRPG Level: the maximum](skyrpg-level-max).

### Level rewards

- Every level shows "SkyRPG Level N" above your hotbar.
- **Every 10th level pays coins**: 500 coins times the level. Level 10 pays 5,000, level 20 pays 10,000, level 50 pays 25,000. You also get a notice and the level-up effect.

### Where it shows

- The header of your Profile page.
- The **level badge** before your name in chat and in the Tab list, in a colour that changes every 40 levels: grey (0 to 39), white, yellow, green, dark green, aqua, dark aqua, blue, pink, purple, gold, red, and dark red from level 480. Hover a badge to see what the next colour is.
- Other players can see it, since it is shared with everyone.

Several Chronicle quests ask for a SkyRPG Level: 2, 5, 9, 20 and 35. See [Quests and the Journal](quests-and-journal).

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Early collections go fast, and they feed your first Artisan Bench recipes. Start with what you gather anyway: stone, coal, wood and crops.

> [!TIP]
> Rare materials have small thresholds. A Lumenite or Hellforged Scrap collection reaches tier I at only 15 items.

> [!NOTE]
> Collections count what **you** gather. A friend who gives you materials does not move your collection.
