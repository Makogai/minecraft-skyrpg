Every day the server posts three **contracts**: shared jobs such as "mine 300 stone" or "deliver 24 Skyiron Ingots". Everyone's progress adds up to the same goal, and when it is done everyone who helped gets the full reward. Contracts pay far better than selling the same goods, so check the board every day. Open it with **K**, then **Contracts**.

## How the board works

- **Three contracts a day** for the whole server. A new set is posted when the date changes, with a chat line: "New contracts on the board! (K -> Contracts)".
- They come from different trades where possible. You will usually see three different ones, such as one mining, one farming and one fishing.
- **Level gates.** A contract that needs a skill level is only picked if at least one player who is online when it is posted has that level. Everyone else can still join in.
- **Shared progress.** Everything every player does counts towards one bar.
- **Everyone who helped is paid.** If you added even one point, you get the full reward when it completes, however much the others did.
- **Offline players still get paid.** If you helped and logged off before it finished, you get your reward the next time you join, with a "Contracts finished while you were away" message.
- **Done contracts stay done** until the next day.

`/contracts` lists today's contracts in chat. A HUD box named **Contracts** shows the progress of the ones that are not finished yet. You can move it in the HUD editor. See [HUD and minimap](hud-and-minimap).

### Kinds of goals

| Kind | How it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Break blocks** | Blocks you break count, such as ore, logs or crops. Blocks that players placed do not count. Some contracts only count fully grown crops. |
| **Kill mobs** | Kills of the named mobs count. |
| **Kill Elites** | Killing an Elite counts. |
| **Catch** | Any catch with a rod counts. Some only count sea creatures. |
| **Deliver** | Items do not count when you gather them. Open the contract on the board and press the hand-in button. It takes the items from your inventory, up to what is still needed. |

Blocks broken in creative mode do not count.

## Rewards

Rewards are given as coins, skill XP, and for some contracts items such as Glimmer Crystals, Dew or Elite Trophies.

- **The amount varies.** Each contract has a range, such as 300 to 600 stone. The board rounds it to a tidy number when it is posted.
- **Coins and XP scale with the amount.** The tables below show the reward at the lowest amount. A contract that asks for double that pays double coins and double XP. Items do not scale.
- Coins go to your purse and the skill XP is added at once.

## Rerolling

You can swap an **untouched** contract for a different one for **500 coins**.

- It must have no progress and not be finished.
- Each contract can be rerolled once. The replacement cannot be rerolled again.
- The new one is never the same contract, and it prefers a trade that is not already on the board.
- The 500 coins come from your purse, and are refunded if no other contract can be offered.

## Tips

> [!TIP]
> Look at the board first, then plan your session. If a mining contract is up, a Mining session gets the contract and the XP for the same blocks.

> [!TIP]
> Cheap contracts like Wheat Harvest and Timber Order are worth doing with friends. Everyone who helps gets the full 500 coins.

> [!TIP]
> Deliver contracts are the easiest for a group. One player can gather and hand in for all of you, and everyone who handed in is paid.

> [!NOTE]
> Only players who added progress are paid. Standing nearby does nothing.
