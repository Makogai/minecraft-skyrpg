Skyhaven's shopkeepers buy what you gather and sell what you need to keep going. This page explains how selling and buying work, what each keeper takes, and what is on every shelf. Prices are in coins unless a column says otherwise.

## Selling

There are three ways to sell. They all pay the **same price per item**.

### At a shopkeeper

Right-click a keeper who buys things. The shop screen has a **sell panel** on the right that lists what you carry that this keeper wants.

- Click an item to sell **all** of it.
- Click **Sell all** to sell everything this keeper wants.
- Each keeper only buys their own trade. Mabel will not buy ore, and Brannoc will not buy wheat.
- After you sell, a **Buy back** button appears. It returns your last sale at that keeper for the same coins, as long as your inventory has room and you can pay.

### With commands

These work anywhere, with no keeper, at the same prices.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/sell` or `/sell hand` | Sells the stack in your hand |
| `/sell all` | Sells every stack of the item in your hand |
| `/sell inventory` | Sells everything in your inventory and hotbar that has a price |
| `/worth` | Shows the price of the item in your hand, and of the whole stack |

Armour you wear and your offhand are never sold. Backpack contents are not touched either.

> [!WARNING]
> `/sell inventory` sells **everything** with a price, including rare finds. A Heartstone sells for 15,000 and a Prism Fragment for 1,500, but both are worth more as crafting ingredients. A shopkeeper's **Sell all** button skips them, because no keeper lists them. Use `/worth` first if you are unsure.

### What cannot be sold

If an item has no price, NPCs will not buy it. Most SkyRPG weapons, armour, tools and accessories have none, so there is no way to turn them back into coins.

## Buying

Click an offer to buy one. **Shift-click** buys a full stack, and **right-click** lets you pick a number: type it, use the arrow keys, or choose 1, 8, 16, 32 or 64, then press **Enter**. You can buy at most a full inventory's worth in one go.

- Offers you cannot afford show how many coins you are short.
- Offers that need a skill level you do not have show "Needs Mining 10" in red.
- Some offers cost **items** instead of coins, such as Glimmer Crystals at Tamsin or Dew Vials at Mabel. The cost shows as icons with counts, red where you are short. The items are taken from your inventory.
- If your inventory is nearly full, you buy as many as fit.
- Bought SkyRPG items come with their proper name, rarity and stats.

Stay within 10 blocks of the keeper. If you walk further away the shop refuses the trade.
