The wardrobe keeps your armour sets so you don't have to carry them. Mining set, farming set, combat set: put each in a wardrobe slot and switch with one click.

## Opening it

Press **O** (you can rebind it under SkyRPG in Controls), or type `/wardrobe` or `/wd`.

## Storing sets

The wardrobe has **18 slots** over two pages of nine. Each slot is a column with a helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots space.

- Drag armour into a column, or **shift-click** armour in your inventory to put it in the first free space of the right kind.
- Each space only takes its kind of piece. A set doesn't need all four pieces.
- Take a piece out the same way you'd take it out of a chest.

## Wearing a set

Under each column is a dye:

| Dye | Means | Click it to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grey | Empty slot | (nothing to wear yet) |
| Pink | A stored set | Put it on |
| Lime | The set you're wearing | Take it off (it goes back to its slot) |

When you put a set on, the set you were wearing goes back to **its own wardrobe slot**. If what you were wearing didn't come from the wardrobe, it goes into your inventory (you need free slots for it, or put it in an empty wardrobe slot first).

While you wear a set, its column shows what you have on and is locked; your stats and set bonuses work as normal.

## Dying

Sets stored in the wardrobe are **kept when you die**, like your backpacks. The set you're wearing is on you, so it drops like any armour would.
