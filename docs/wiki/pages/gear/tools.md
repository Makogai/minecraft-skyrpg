Tools are how you gather. SkyRPG has a ladder of pickaxes, axes, hoes and scythes, and fishing rods, each made at the [Artisan Bench](crafting) from the one before it. Tools carry gathering stats (Mining Speed, Fortune, Fishing Speed...) that apply while you hold them, and the better ones have a right-click ability. SkyRPG tools do not break.

## What tools give you

- **Stats while held.** A pickaxe's Mining Speed and Mining Fortune only count while it is in your hand. Gathering stats from armour, talents and enchants add on top.
- **Tiers.** Pickaxes and axes have a tier from I to VII. SkyRPG's harder ores and woods need a minimum tier, and a weaker tool does not crack them at all (see [Crafting](crafting)).
- **Abilities.** The better tools have a right-click ability with a cooldown. They do not cost mana.
- **Upgrades keep your enchants.** The bench keeps a tool's name and enchantments when you upgrade it.
- **Enchants.** Pickaxes, axes, hoes (scythes count as hoes), any tool and fishing rods each take their own kind of enchant (see [Enchanting](enchanting)).

### Tier needs for blocks

| Block | Tier needed |
| --- | --- |
| Skyiron ore and node | II |
| Ironbark logs and stumps | III |
| Duskquartz ore and node, Mistpine logs and stumps | IV |
| Emberite and Hellforged ore, Emberite node, Duskwillow logs and stumps | V |
| Lumenite ore and node, Emberwood logs and stumps | VI |
| Starwood logs | VII |

Vanilla tools have tiers too: wooden and golden I, stone and copper II, iron III, diamond IV, netherite V.
