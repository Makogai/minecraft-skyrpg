Wands are the weapons of a **mage build**. A wand gives you **Intelligence** (more mana) while you hold it, has **spell slots** for [spell scrolls](spells), and turns the mouse into a spellcasting tool: **left click** casts the selected spell, **hold right click** opens the **spell wheel**. There are **seven**, from the Apprentice Wand to the Starweaver Staff, each crafted at the [Artisan Bench](crafting) from the one before it, with the **Enchanting** skill.

Each upgrade keeps the old wand's **enchants and the scrolls inside it**. The Skyiron Rod is the fork in the road: both epic staves and the Tempest Scepter are made from it; the Starweaver Staff then comes from the Tempest Scepter. Intelligence counts only while you **hold** the wand (like any held item), so your mana is 100 + Intelligence from the wand + your other gear. See [Stats](stats).

## Controls

With a wand in your **main hand** the mouse is for spells:

| Input | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Left click** | Casts the selected spell. You do not swing and cannot break blocks. |
| **Hold left click** | Charges a chargeable spell (Fireball, Meteor) for up to 1.5 seconds. A ring fills around your crosshair and a chime rises; let go to cast. A full charge pings. |
| **Hold right click** | After a quarter of a second the **spell wheel** opens. Move the mouse towards a spell and **release** to select it. |
| **Sneak + right click** | Opens the wand's **spell slots**, where you put scrolls in and take them out. |
| **Sneak + scroll wheel** | Selects the next or previous spell. |

Right click still does what it does on things that have a use of their own: chests, doors, levers, animals and NPCs. The wheel opens when you aim at the air, a plain block or an enemy. A shield in your off hand still raises. Two keys can be changed under **Controls > SkyRPG**: *Spell wheel (hold)*, right click by default, and *Spell wheel (toggle)*, **G** by default, which opens a wheel that stays open until you click a spell. See [Controls and keys](controls-and-keys).

### The spell wheel

Your wand's spells sit round a circle in the middle of the screen, each with its icon, name, **mana cost** and, while recharging, the time left. The one under the mouse lights up in its element's colour and the middle says what it does. It pops open and shrinks away with a chime. A spell you cannot afford shows its cost in red.

### The spell box

While you hold a wand, a box on the HUD (bottom right; move it in the HUD editor) shows the selected spell: the icon, with the **cooldown sweeping** down over it, its name, its mana cost, a charge bar for chargeable spells and a pip for each slot. Without enough mana the cost flashes red and says so; casting a recharging spell tells you how long is left.

## Spell slots

Sneak and right click with the wand open its slots: a row where the wand's slots sit in the middle and the rest are locked. Drop a **spell scroll** into a slot to install it (each spell only once per wand), shift-click to move it in, and take it out again to get the scroll back. The wand cannot be moved while its slots are open.

## Mages

A mage build is a wand, Intelligence and mana: Intelligence makes every spell hit harder (**each point adds 1% damage**) and gives more mana for more casts. Mana returns at 2% of your maximum per second, so a bigger pool also refills faster. Spells **do not use Strength or crits**. They do count for combat XP, loot, kills and [slayers](slayers) like any hit of yours, and they **never hurt your party** members. See [Spells](spells) for the twelve spells and how to get the scrolls.

> [!TIP]
> Put a cheap fast spell (Firebolt or Frost Shard) in the first slot for everyday fights, and keep a Blink or a Mana Shield ready on the wheel for when things go wrong. The Astral Aegis in your off hand adds 25 Intelligence.
