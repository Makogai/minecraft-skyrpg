A long mining trip fills your inventory in minutes. Three things fix that: **enchanted materials**, the **Personal Compactor** that makes them for you, and the **Personal Deletor** that never lets junk in.

## Enchanted materials

Every bulk material has an enchanted form: **160 of it packed into one** glinting item, such as {{item:skyrpg_items:enchanted_cobblestone}} or {{item:skyrpg_items:enchanted_diamond}}. It looks like the material, with the enchantment glint and a green name.

- Craft one at the **Artisan Bench** (Enchanted tab): 160 of the material makes one.
- Or let a Personal Compactor make them as you gather.
- NPCs pay **160 times** the material's price, so nothing is lost by compacting.
- They stack to 64, so one slot holds 10,240 of a material.
- An enchanted material can't be unpacked again.

## Personal Compactor

{{item:skyrpg_items:personal_compactor_4000}} Keep it **anywhere in your inventory or in a backpack you carry**. Right-click it to choose its materials. From then on, every time you have 160 of one of them, they become one enchanted item: as you mine, chop, farm, fish or pick things up. If your inventory is full, it packs first to make room.

## Personal Deletor

{{item:skyrpg_items:personal_deletor_4000}} Also works from your inventory or a carried backpack. Items set on it are **thrown away as you gather them**, before they take a slot: dirt, gravel, rotten flesh, whatever you don't want. They still count for your collections.

- It only takes stackable items, so gear can never be deleted.
- Items you or someone else dropped are left alone; only fresh drops are deleted.
- Named or otherwise changed stacks are never touched, by either tool.

## Setting one up

Right-click the compactor or deletor. A small chest-style menu opens with its slots:

- **Click an item in your inventory** to add it. For a compactor you can click the material or its enchanted form.
- **Click a filled slot** to clear it.
- Nothing moves out of your inventory; the menu only reads what you click.

The tooltip lists what it's set to. Carrying several tools adds their lists together.

## Tiers

Both tools come in four tiers. A higher tier holds more materials. Upgrading at the Artisan Bench keeps the materials you set.

| 5000 | 5 | Rare | Compactor 4000, 8 Enchanted Iron Ingot, 4 Enchanted Redstone Dust | Deletor 4000, 2 Enchanted Obsidian, 8 Enchanted Coal |
| 6000 | 7 | Epic | Compactor 5000, 16 Enchanted Gold Ingot, 4 Enchanted Diamond | Deletor 5000, 4 Enchanted Blaze Rod, 8 Enchanted Gold Ingot |
| 7000 | 12 | Legendary | Compactor 6000, 16 Enchanted Diamond, 2 Prism Fragments | Deletor 6000, 12 Enchanted Diamond, 1 Prism Fragment |

All are made at the Artisan Bench (Compactors tab).
