SkyRPG armour comes in **sets of four pieces** (helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots). Each piece gives its own stats, and wearing all four adds a **full-set bonus**: extra stats plus an effect that does something you can feel, such as Night Vision underground or lightning-fast replanting. There are 42 sets, from starter gear you craft at a table to legendary sets from bosses.

## How sets work

- **Wear all four pieces** of the same set for the bonus. Fewer pieces give the piece stats only.
- Piece stats and the set's bonus stats are added to your stats, so they show up on the Stats page (see [Stats](stats)).
- SkyRPG armour does not break.
- Most sets are made at the [Artisan Bench](crafting) from materials, not from the previous set. Armour enchants count in their slot (see [Enchanting](enchanting)).
- **Names in the tables:** "Four pieces together" adds up the stats of the helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots. The bonus stats are on top of that. Rarity is the rarity of the chestplate (the slayer sets have Epic helmets, leggings and boots with a Legendary chestplate).
- The cards under each table show a chestplate from each set. The other three pieces are in [Items: armour](items-armour).

> [!TIP]
> Pieces of different sets still add their own stats. If you cannot afford a full set yet, mix pieces for the stats you need, then complete one set later for the bonus.

## Skill sets

Each gathering skill has a ladder of sets. The first is a starter you can buy or craft from vanilla materials. The rest unlock at Skill levels 8 to 45, and are made from the previous tier's materials. Each full-set bonus is tied to its skill.

## 3D looks

Worn SkyRPG armour is not a flat skin. Each set has raised 3D detail that matches its icon, drawn over re-shaded worn layers, so brims, crests, pauldrons, belts and cuffs stand out from the body. It shows on players and mannequins, and it is drawn by the game client, so you need the pack's client mod to see it.
