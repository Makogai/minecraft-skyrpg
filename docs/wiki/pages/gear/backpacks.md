Backpacks give you more room to carry things. You carry up to **three** at once, each in its own slot, and you open them on the move without putting anything down. Mining and farming fill your bag fast, so a backpack is one of the first upgrades worth getting.

## The slots

Three backpack slots sit in a strip to the right of the inventory in the E screen (and on the creative inventory's Survival Inventory tab). Like the accessory strip, it hides while the recipe book is open.

- Put a backpack in a slot: click it in, or shift-click it from your inventory.
- Right-click the slot to open that backpack. Press **B** to open the first one (you can rebind the key under SkyRPG in Controls).
- Backpacks cannot go inside backpacks.
- A pack's contents are stored on the pack item, so it keeps them wherever it goes. Backpacks you carry are stored with you, so you keep them on death. The tooltip shows how full a pack is.

### Crafting and upgrading

The Satchel is made at a crafting table: string on the top corners, leather around it and an iron ingot in the middle. Dorrin sells the Satchel and the Trail Pack at Saltmarsh Provisions in the hub.

Each bigger pack is an upgrade made at the [Artisan Bench](crafting). The new pack **keeps everything inside the old one** (even while the old one is worn in a backpack slot). None of these recipes needs a skill level.

| Upgrade | Ingredients besides the old pack |
| --- | --- |
| Satchel to Trail Pack | 12 leather, 8 string, 2 Glimmer Crystals |
| Trail Pack to Ranger's Pack | 16 leather, 12 string, 8 Raw Skyiron |
| Ranger's Pack to Voyager's Pack | 8 white wool, 16 gold ingots, 4 Emberite Chunks |
| Voyager's Pack to Colossal Pack | 32 leather, 4 Prism Fragments, 1 Heartstone |

## Using them with crafting

Packs are not only storage:

- The [Artisan Bench](crafting) takes its ingredients from your inventory **and** from inside the backpacks you carry.
- Enchanting costs (Lapis, Runic Dust, Prism Fragments) are taken from your backpacks too (see [Enchanting](enchanting)).

> [!TIP]
> Keep your bench materials (Glimmer Crystals, ingots, logs, Dew Vials) in a backpack and your inventory free for loot. You never have to take them out to craft.
