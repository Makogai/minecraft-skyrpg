Shields are the first way to block damage in SkyRPG instead of just out-healing it. There are **seven**, one for each stage of the game, and each is bigger and more ornate than the last. They are crafted at the [Artisan Bench](crafting), each one from the shield before it, and from the third on they come with an effect of their own.

## How blocking works

- **Hold right-click** with a shield in your off hand (or your main hand) to raise it. You block damage that comes from the front, within a quarter turn either side of where you are looking. A short moment passes after raising it before it works.
- Each shield blocks a **share of the damage**: 50% for the Oak Buckler, up to 90% for the Astral Aegis. Your [Defense](stats) works on what is left, so the two stack.
- Fire, starving, magic and a few other kinds of damage get past any shield.
- Axes knock a shield out of your hands for a few seconds, like in vanilla.
- A shield is **gear**: its Defense and Health count while it is in your off hand (or your main hand), even when you are not blocking. Its effect works whenever it is held, and only blocking triggers it.
- Shields never break.

The Thunderguard also gives 3% Crit Chance and the Astral Aegis 25 Intelligence. The first shield needs only Combat 3; the ladder follows the same combat levels as the swords and armour (10, 18, 26, 35, 45).

## Effects in detail

### Bash

A blocked **melee** hit shoves the attacker away from you, which gives you room to swing or run. The Skyiron Bulwark's shove is a good one, about a block and a half.

### Burning and freezing

The Emberforged Aegis sets the **attacker** on fire when you block their hit, and the Frostward Barrier slows them heavily and frosts them for three seconds. Both only trigger on melee attackers that you actually block: hits from behind, and arrows, do not.

### Storm charges (Thunderguard)

Every hit you block (melee or an arrow) stores a **charge**, up to five. A message on the action bar counts them. **Sneak and right-click** to release them as a shockwave around you: every hostile mob within 3 blocks plus 1 per charge takes lightning and **40 damage per charge**, before your Strength and a crit roll. With five charges that is 200 damage to everything within 8 blocks, and the charges are gone. You cannot raise the shield while you are releasing.

> [!TIP]
> Stand in a crowd with the Thunderguard up, take hits until the action bar says 5/5, then sneak and right-click. It is the best crowd-clearing move in the game before the Starfall Blade.

### Reflecting projectiles (Astral Aegis)

An arrow, a fireball or a trident that hits the raised Astral Aegis from the front is sent back at whoever shot it, a little faster. A reflected arrow becomes yours: it deals SkyRPG damage and counts for your kills. Projectiles from behind still hit you.

## Looks

Every shield is a small 3D model: you see its thickness and raised rims in your hand, in the inventory and when you block. The Emberforged Aegis' cracks and the Thunderguard's veins glow in the dark, the Astral Aegis' stars twinkle, and the stronger ones shed particles (embers, snowflakes, sparks, motes of light).
