SkyRPG bows are real ranged weapons. They draw and shoot like vanilla bows and use **ordinary arrows** as ammo, but the arrows deal **SkyRPG damage**: the bow's **Damage** stat plus your **Strength**, **Crit Chance** and **Crit Damage**, the same way a sword does. There are **seven**, from the Hunter's Bow to the Starfall Bow. Each is crafted at the [Artisan Bench](crafting) from the bow before it, with logs from [Foraging](foraging) and the usual combat materials.

## How bow damage works

- **Damage per arrow** is the bow's Damage stat times how far you drew it. A full draw (one second) is full damage; a half draw is about half. A shot weaker than a tenth of a draw does not fire.
- Then your **Strength** adds 1% per point and a **Crit Chance** roll can multiply the hit by 1 + Crit Damage / 100, exactly like melee. The Strength and crit stats on your armour, talents and on the bow itself all count. See [Stats](stats).
- The hit is a normal hit: **damage numbers** show above the target (crits in gold), a kill gives you the **Combat XP**, Valor Marks and loot, and it counts for **slayer** quests and bosses.
- A bow's own stats (Strength, crits) only count while you **hold** it. Hitting something with the bow itself does almost nothing.
- Bow **enchants** such as Deadeye (+damage), Splitshot, Longshot, Quiversong and Starshot work on all SkyRPG bows (see [Enchanting](enchanting)).
- Arrows are not used up by the extra arrows of the Starfall Bow, and its stars cannot be picked up again.

Each bow has its own **arrow trail** while the arrow flies: sparks for the Hunter's Bow, green motes for the Recurve, a thin smoke line for the Longbow, flames for the Ember Bow, snowflakes for the Frostbite Bow, electric sparks for the Stormcaller and stardust for the Starfall Bow.

## Effects in detail

- **Pierce:** the Longbow's arrow flies through its first target and hits the next one, each for full damage.
- **Ember burst:** a fire arrow sets what it hits alight, and bursts, hurting every hostile mob within 2.5 blocks for 60% of the hit. The burst never destroys blocks or starts fires on the ground.
- **Frost:** the Frostbite Bow's hit slows the target to a crawl and frosts it for 3 seconds, which also makes it easy to hit again.
- **Lightning:** the Stormcaller Bow counts your **full-draw** shots. The third one, then the sixth, and so on, calls a bolt of lightning on the target for 80% of the hit on top of it. Half-drawn shots do not count and do not reset the count.
- **Star arrows:** the Starfall Bow's three arrows fan out a few degrees. Each deals 55% of the bow's Damage (a full hit with all three is 165%), and they bend towards enemies in front of them, so close shots on a single target often land all three.

> [!TIP]
> The Ember Bow's burst makes it the bow for groups, the Longbow for lines of mobs in corridors, the Frostbite Bow for fast or dangerous melee mobs and bosses you want to keep at range.

## Making bows better

Bow enchants (Deadeye, Longshot, Quiversong, Splitshot, Starshot, Cinder Arrows) can all be put on SkyRPG bows. Strength and crit stats from armour, accessories and talents apply to arrows too, so a melee build is a good ranged build as well.
