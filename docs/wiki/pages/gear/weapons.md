Weapons are what you hit monsters with. SkyRPG's weapons are not drops you hope for: almost all of them are crafted at the [Artisan Bench](crafting) from monster drops, boss tokens and the previous weapon in their line. Each line has a clear path, and the better weapons carry abilities and special effects.

## How weapon damage works

A weapon's **Damage** is its base damage before anything else. Your stats then change it:

- **Strength** adds 1% to your melee damage per point. A weapon with 20 Strength is 20% stronger, and the Strength on your armour and talents stacks with it.
- **Crit Chance** is the chance for a hit to be a critical hit (you start with 30%). A crit multiplies the hit by 1 + Crit Damage / 100 (you start with 50% Crit Damage).
- **Defense** on a target cuts damage the same way, so 100 Defense halves it.

Weapons also carry set effects and perks. "Heals 3% of the damage you deal" is life steal, "+40% damage to spiders" is a bane effect that only counts against that kind of mob, and "poison" or "sets on fire" apply to what you hit. See [Stats](stats) and [Combat basics](combat-basics) for the full picture.

### Abilities

Some weapons have an ability you use with right-click. There are two kinds:

- **Cooldown abilities** are free but have a cooldown (Stormedge's Thunderclap, Starfall Blade's Starfall).
- **Mana abilities** cost mana. Your mana is 100 plus 1 per point of Intelligence (Riftblade's Riftstep costs 50, the Mending Wand's Mend costs 60).

## The combat line

The main sword ladder is paid in **Valor Marks** (dropped by levelled mobs, more often the higher their level, nine make a Valor Seal) and rare drops from monsters. Each sword is made from the one before it and keeps its name and enchantments. Combat gear unlocks at Combat 10, 18, 26, 35 and 45.

## The Mending Wand

The {{item:skyrpg_items:mending_wand}} is a wand, not a sword. It has 50 Intelligence and the ability **Mend** (right-click): it costs 60 mana and heals you for 40 health, with a 5 second cooldown. Odessa Quill sells it at Quill's Curiosities for 18,000 coins. The Spellweave enchant (+55 Intelligence while held) goes on wands.

## Making weapons better

Enchants are the main way to improve a weapon beyond its base numbers (see [Enchanting](enchanting)). Sword enchants include Keen (+40% damage at the top level), Hallowed Edge and Webcutter (more damage to the undead and to spiders), Bloodthirst (life steal), Frostbite, Venom Coat, Cleave (hits nearby enemies), Reaper's Mark, Titanbane and the ultimates Soulrend and Rift Echo. Bow enchants such as Deadeye, Splitshot and Starshot work with the vanilla bows and crossbows that Kestrel Vane sells.

## Weapon looks

The strongest weapons and tools are drawn with larger, animated art (sheens, lightning, embers, stars, runes) and the biggest blades are held larger. Turn this on or off in Settings, Interface, Detailed weapon art. Some weapons also show held particles around the hand (Settings, Gameplay, Weapon particles).
