Accessories are extra pieces of gear that sit outside your armour: an **amulet**, a **cloak**, a **belt** and a pair of **bracers**. They give stats like any other gear, they are kept when you die, and everyone nearby can see them on your character. There are 19 accessories, and you can wear one in each of the four slots at once.

## The four slots

A strip of four slots sits to the left of your armour in the inventory screen (press E), and on the creative inventory's Survival Inventory tab. It hides while the recipe book is open, because the book uses the same space.

| Slot | Worn | Hint when empty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amulet | A cord round the neck with a pendant on the chest | Pendants and charms, worn at the neck |
| Cloak | A mantle on the shoulders and a cape down the back | Mantles and capes, worn on the back |
| Belt | A band round the waist with a buckle and a pouch | Sashes and belts, worn at the waist |
| Bracers | Cuffs on both forearms with raised plates | Bracers and vambraces, worn on the forearms |

### Wearing and removing

- Right-click an accessory in your hand to put it on. Like armour, it swaps with whatever is in the slot.
- Or shift-click it in your inventory, or drop it onto its slot. The slot lights up gold while you carry something that fits.
- Each accessory only fits its own slot.
- Accessories are stored with you, not in your inventory, so you **keep them on death** and `/clear` does not touch them.

### Stats

A worn accessory adds its stats under the **Accessories** source on your Stats page (see [Stats](stats)). Holding one in your hand gives nothing. Some accessories also have an effect, such as life steal or a dodge chance.

## On your character

Accessories are 3D models that everyone nearby sees. The cape swings as you move (it replaces your own cape and hides under an elytra), the belt has a buckle and pouch, and the cuffs also show on your hands in first person. Over chest armour each one switches to a wider fit that sits on top of the armour. They are hidden while you are invisible.

## Where to get them

- **Odessa Quill's shop (Quill's Curiosities) in the hub** sells the eight starter accessories, from 800 to 24,000 coins.
- **Slayer accessories** are made at the [Artisan Bench](crafting) at slayer level 3 of their boss, from that boss's tokens and Valor Seals. See [Slayers](slayers).
- **Region accessories** are made at the bench at Combat 12 to 20 from a danger region's essence. See [Danger regions](danger-regions).
- **The Widowsilk Cloak and Tidemother Pendant** are dungeon crafts that need Delving 12 and 20 (see [Delving](delving)).

> [!TIP]
> Early on, the cheap shop accessories (the Emberstone Pendant for 900 coins, the Tinker's Toolbelt or Sunwoven Sash for 850 and 800) give you stats for a tiny price. Swap them for slayer and region pieces as you get them.
