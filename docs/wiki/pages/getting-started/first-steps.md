You spawn in Skyhaven with nothing to do and a lot of friendly strangers. This page is a route through your first hour: which quests to take, who to meet, what to buy and where the first coins come from. Follow it and you will know every system you need for the survival world.

## Minute 0: look around

You appear on the Skyheart plaza, a little south of the golden fountain at the centre of the town. Nobody can hurt you here and nobody gets hungry. Take a minute to find your bearings with the minimap in the top right corner. Press **M** for the world map.

Press **K** to open the SkyRPG menu. The pages down the left are your Profile, Stats, Skills, Talents, Journal and more. See the [K menu](k-menu) for all of them.

## The Skyhaven Chronicle

The questline is called the Skyhaven Chronicle. Every quest is a conversation with an NPC.

- A gold **!** over an NPC means they have a quest for you.
- A grey **?** means you are working on one of theirs.
- A gold **?** means it is ready: go back and talk to them to hand it in.

Right-click the NPC to talk. Click a choice, press Space to skip the typing, or press the number on a choice. Stay close to the NPC while you talk. Esc closes the conversation, and you can pick it up again later. Details are on [Quests and the Journal](quests-and-journal).

### Chapter 1: Welcome to Skyhaven

Chapter 1 has 11 quests and pays 600 coins and 165 SkyRPG XP in total. It is a tour of the town in this order.

| Quest | From, then to | What you do | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A Warm Welcome | Journal, then Hollis | Talk to Hollis Barrowmead in the tavern | 40 coins, 8 {{item:minecraft:bread|bread}} |
| Your Journal | Hollis | Open the Journal in the K menu | 30 coins |
| Find Your Way | Hollis, then Dorrin Saltmarsh | Visit the Provisioner at the Grand Bazaar | 40 coins, a {{item:minecraft:compass|compass}} |
| Safe and Sound | Dorrin, then Cassius Vellum | Visit the Banker at the Skyhaven Bank | 60 coins |
| Meet the Masters | Hollis, then Tamsin Deepholt | Meet the Mining Master | 50 coins, a stone pickaxe |
| Timber Talk | Tamsin, then Oswin Fernhollow | Meet the Foraging Master | 50 coins, a stone axe |
| Fields of Gold | Oswin, then Mabel Thornfield | Meet the Farmer | 50 coins, a stone hoe, 8 carrots |
| Patience, Love | Mabel, then Marina Saltwick | Meet the Fishing Master | 50 coins, a fishing rod |
| Steel and Nerve | Marina, then Kestrel Vane | Meet the Combat Master | 50 coins, a stone sword |
| The Artisan Bench | Kestrel, then Brannoc Ashforge | Meet the Blacksmith | 80 coins, 4 iron ingots |
| Through the Wildgate | Hollis | Step through the Wildgate | 100 coins, 32 torches |

Where each NPC stands is on [NPCs](npcs). The Master quests hand you a tool each, so you finish chapter 1 with a stone pickaxe, axe, hoe and sword and a fishing rod.

### Chapter 2: First Steps

Chapter 2 starts outside, in the survival world. It has 12 quests and pays 2,290 coins and 365 SkyRPG XP. It asks you to:

- Attune a waystone (right-click a standing stone, for example by a village bell).
- Gather 64 Stone and 24 Coal for Tamsin, 32 Oak for Oswin, and 16 Carrots for Mabel.
- Reach Fishing level 3 and gather 10 Cod for Marina.
- Defeat 5 zombies and 5 skeletons for Kestrel.
- Reach Mining, Foraging and Combat level 2 for Hollis.
- Reach Mining level 8 and craft a Delver Pickaxe at the Artisan Bench.
- Get a pet (Odessa Quill gives you a pup egg) and bring it along.

You do not have to buy anything. The stone tools get you through chapter 1 and 2, and you can save for the upgrades at the Artisan Bench later. See [Crafting](crafting).

## Your first coins

Coins sit in your **purse**, shown in the top left. They are kept when you die. Early on they come from:

1. **Quests.** Chapter 1 pays 600 coins and chapter 2 another 2,290.
2. **Contracts.** Three shared contracts a day, in the K menu. Mine 300 stone, chop 128 logs or harvest 128 grown wheat and you get 500 coins and skill XP. See [Contracts](contracts).
3. **Selling.** Each Master buys the goods of their trade. A whole melon sells for 18, a pumpkin for 10, a mushroom for 10, a tropical fish for 20. Stone and logs are worth 1 and 2. See [Selling and shops](selling-and-shops).
4. **Elites.** Gold-named monsters in the survival world drop 50 to 200 coins and an Elite Trophy worth 250.
5. **Events.** About every 60 to 90 minutes, when two or more players are on. See [Events](events).

> [!TIP]
> Contracts pay far better than selling the same materials. 300 stone sells for 300 coins, but the Stone Quota contract pays 500 coins and 300 Mining XP for the same job.

Put coins you do not need into the bank at the Skyhaven Bank. They earn 1% interest a day (up to 5,000). See [Coins and the bank](coins-and-bank).

## A good first hour

1. Press K and do chapter 1 (allow 15 to 20 minutes). Talk to everyone.
2. Open the Settings page once and glance at the HUD and Map options. See [Settings](settings).
3. Walk through the Wildgate with your stone tools and torches.
4. Do the chapter 2 gathering quests near the gate. Every one of them also levels a skill.
5. Come back by `/hub` or a waystone, hand the quests in and sell what you do not need.
6. Check the day's contracts in the K menu and take the ones you can finish.
7. Spend your first coins on the tool for the trade you like most.

> [!WARNING]
> Night in the survival world is dangerous. Mobs are levelled by distance from spawn, and monsters further out hit much harder. Stay close to the Wildgate until you have levelled a few skills. See [Combat basics](combat-basics).
