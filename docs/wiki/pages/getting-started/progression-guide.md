SkyRPG has a long ladder and no single route up it. This page lays out the path in eight stages, matched to the eight chapters of the Skyhaven Chronicle. For each stage it gives the skill levels to aim for, the gear that unlocks, and the places to go. The numbers are the levels the game itself uses: skill levels needed to craft or buy gear, the levels of dungeons and mobs, and the checks the questline makes.

Levels here are rough guides. You do not need to be exactly there. Read the gear tables as "you can start using this at about this level".

## How the ladder works

Every skill follows the same loop: **gather, refine, craft, wear better gear, gather deeper.**

- Gathering gives skill XP, and every skill level gives stats and a talent point.
- Materials are refined and crafted into gear at the **Artisan Bench**. Many recipes need the previous tier of the same item, and the new one keeps its name and enchantments.
- Recipes unlock by skill level, and many mid-tier ones also need a collection tier. See [Crafting](crafting) and [Collections](collections).
- Better tools reach better materials. Pickaxes and axes have tiers (I to VII), and a hard ore will not break with a weaker tool.
- Tougher gear lets you survive further from spawn, where mobs have higher levels. That is where the rare drops are.

### Mob levels by place

| Place | Mob level |
| --- | --- |
| Near world spawn | 1 |
| Surface, further out | +1 per 120 blocks from spawn, up to 30 |
| Underground | +5 below y 0, +10 below y -32 |
| Danger regions | +12 to +20 |
| The Nether | starts at 20 |
| The End | starts at 40 |
| Highest ordinary level | 50 |

Each level above 1 adds 8% health and 5% damage. Elites are three levels higher, with four times the health. A Blood Moon adds 10 more levels. See [Combat basics](combat-basics) and [Danger regions](danger-regions).

## Stage 1: Skyhaven

**Goal:** meet everyone, learn the menu, earn your first 600 coins.

Do chapter 1 of the Chronicle. You finish with stone tools, a compass and 32 torches. See [First steps](first-steps).

## Stage 2: First steps

**Goal:** level each trade past level 2, find your first waystone, get a pet.

- Gather with your stone tools near the Wildgate: stone, coal, oak, carrots, cod.
- Buy the trade's starter set from its Master when you can afford it.
- Take the day's [contracts](contracts).
- At Mining 8 craft the {{item:skyrpg_items:delver_pickaxe}} at the Artisan Bench.

| Trade | First gear | Master |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mining | {{item:skyrpg_items:prospector_pickaxe}}, Prospector set | Brannoc (set), Tamsin (Quarry Pick) |
| Farming | {{item:skyrpg_items:harvester_hoe}}, Harvester set | Mabel |
| Foraging | {{item:skyrpg_items:woodsman_axe}}, Woodsman set | Oswin |
| Fishing | {{item:skyrpg_items:driftwood_rod}}, Angler set | Marina |
| Combat | {{item:skyrpg_items:vanguard_blade}}, Vanguard set | Kestrel |

## Stage 3: Into the dark

**Goal:** clear your first dungeon, start slaying, enchant something.

- Reach about Combat 10 to 12 and SkyRPG Level 5.
- Find the **Forgotten Crypt** (forests and plains, mob levels 12 to 19). Its champion is the Bone Knight and its boss is the Crypt Warden.
- Talk to Morwen Ashgrave and start a tier I slayer quest. Tier I costs 1,000 coins and needs slayer level 0. See [Slayers](slayers).
- Talk to Seraphine Vael and enchant a piece of gear. See [Enchanting](enchanting).

| Skill level | Unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| Mining 8 | {{item:skyrpg_items:delver_pickaxe}}, then the Delver set at Mining 10 |
| Combat 10 | {{item:skyrpg_items:fangsteel_sabre}} (life steal), and the Fangguard set from Kestrel for Valor Marks |
| Fishing 10 | The Reefwalker set from Marina for Tidescales |
| Farming 8 | Fieldhand set and Sunwheat from Mabel, paid in Dew |
| Foraging 8 | The Barkguard set, and the {{item:skyrpg_items:ironbark_hatchet}} (also needs Ironbark collection tier II) |
| Delving 5 | Warden's Shroud set, made from Crypt Warden tokens |

## Stage 4: The red lands

**Goal:** leave the safe ground and take something from a danger region.

- Aim for Combat 18, Mining 14 and SkyRPG Level 9 (the chapter's last quest asks for them).
- **Danger regions** are the red zones on your map: the Blightlands, the Ashen Wastes, Frostgrave, the Mire, the Haunted Moors and the Shattered Highlands. Monsters there are 12 to 20 levels higher and drop region essences for region gear. `/region` tells you what is nearby.
- Clear **Spider Hollow** (under dark forests and pale gardens, levels 19 to 26) or the **Drowned Temple** (oceans, levels 26 to 33).
- Slayer tier II: 5,000 coins and slayer level 2.

| Skill level | Unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| Mining 18 | {{item:skyrpg_items:skyiron_pickaxe}} (Tier IV) and the Skyforged set |
| Combat 18 | {{item:skyrpg_items:stormedge}} (Thunderclap) and the Stormplate set |
| Fishing 18 | Stormcaller Rod |
| Farming 18 | Sunreaper Scythe and the Sunward set |
| Foraging 18 | Mistpine Splitter and the Mistwarden set |
| Delving 12 | Arachne's Carapace set, from Spider Hollow tokens |
| Combat 14 to 20 | Region gear: Mirewoven (14), Blightforged (16), Rimeguard, Cinderplate and the Rimecleaver (20), made from region essences |

## Stage 5: The Deep

**Goal:** go underground and reach the Dwarven Halls.

The caves get stranger with depth, in four layers: the **Glimmer Caverns** (y 0 to 40, +4 levels), the **Fungal Depths** (y -20 to 0, +7), the **Dwarven Halls** layer (y -40 to -20, +10) and the **Magma Rifts** (y -64 to -40, +14). The dungeon called **The Dwarven Halls** (levels 32 to 41) sits under mountains, hills and forests, and its boss is the Iron Thane. Reach Delving 5 before the quest "Delver's Creed".

| Skill level | Unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| Mining 26 | {{item:skyrpg_items:duskbore_pick}} (Tier V, Echo Sense) and the Deepwarden set |
| Combat 26 | {{item:skyrpg_items:riftblade}} (Riftstep) and the Riftweave set (15% dodge) |
| Fishing 26 | Abyssal Rod and the Abyssal set |
| Farming 26 | Frostbite Scythe (also needs Frostberry collection tier III), the Frostgrove set, Emberpepper seeds |
| Foraging 26 | Willowcleaver and the Willowshade set |
| Delving 20 | Runeforged and Tide Regalia sets and the Tidecaller Blade |

## Stage 6: Fire below

**Goal:** enter the Nether, find Hellforged ore and burn down the Infernal Citadel.

- Combat 28 and SkyRPG Level 20 are the chapter's final check.
- Nether mobs start at level 20. Make a portal and find the Ember Forest, the Bone Sea and the Obsidian Spires.
- Mine Hellforged ore with a Tier V pickaxe. Blast it into Hellforged Ingots and craft Hellforged armour (Delving 30) and the Hellforged Pickaxe (Mining 40).
- Fish for Magma Fish in lava (Fishing 20 for the quest, the Magmaline Rod at Fishing 40).
- Clear the **Infernal Citadel** (levels 44 to 52). Its boss is the Magma Tyrant.

| Skill level | Unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| Mining 35 | {{item:skyrpg_items:emberheart_pickaxe}} (Tier VI, Molten Core) and the Emberheart set |
| Combat 35 | {{item:skyrpg_items:cinderfang}} (burning hits, life steal) and the Cinderguard set |
| Farming 35 | Cinder Scythe, the Cinderfield set, Moonmelon seeds |
| Foraging 35 | Emberbrand Axe and the Emberbark set |
| Fishing 35 | Leviathan Rod |

## Stage 7: Ancient ruins

**Goal:** two big dungeons and the Slayer veteran.

- Clear the **Ruined Citadel** (rare, mountains and peaks, levels 54 to 58). Its boss is the Fallen Knight.
- Find the sealed stair in the Magma Rifts that leads to the **Catacombs** (very rare, y -60 to -40, levels 59 to 62). Collect three Ossuary Keys from the Bone Wardens to reach the Bonelord.
- Finish a tier IV slayer quest (60,000 coins, slayer level 6).
- Gather 64 Ender Pearls for Odessa.

## Stage 8: The void

**Goal:** the End, and the final boss.

- Enter the End. Mobs start at level 40.
- Explore the outer islands for the **Void Gardens**, **Shattered Isles** and **Astral Fields**. Astral Crystals drop Astral Shards.
- Clear the **Astral Observatory** (levels 60 to 65). Its champion is the Astral Sentinel and its boss is the Star Warden.
- Defeat the **Ender Wyrm**, a level 100 flying serpent with about 2,000 effective health, and drops of Wyrm Scales and a Void Heart.

| Skill level | Unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| Mining 45 | {{item:skyrpg_items:lumen_breaker}} (Tier VII, double ore drops) and the Lumenward set |
| Combat 45 | {{item:skyrpg_items:starfall_blade}} (Starfall) and the Starforged set (crits heal you) |
| Foraging 45 | Starwood Greataxe and the Starwood set |
| Farming 45 | Moonlit Reaper, the Moonharvest set, the Starbloom Planter |
| Fishing 40 | Magmaline Rod and the Leviathan set |

> [!NOTE]
> At the moment the Ender Wyrm is summoned by an administrator, or from a spawn egg. A player-built altar is part of the plan but is not in the game yet. Ask your host if you want a Wyrm fight.

The chapter, and the Chronicle for now, ends when you reach SkyRPG Level 35 and tell Hollis.

## Tips for the whole climb

- Keep **every** skill moving. Skill levels give stats, and each level adds 5 SkyRPG XP.
- Enchant everything you can. See [Enchanting](enchanting).
- Wear an accessory in each of the four slots. See [Accessories](accessories).
- Get a pet for the skill you are levelling. See [Pets](pets).
- Never throw away rare materials. Prism Fragments, Heartstones and Dread Fangs have uses at the Bench and sell for a lot.
- Do not rush the Nether. Hit stage 5 gear first.
