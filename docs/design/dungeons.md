# World dungeons and waystones (Adventure Plan v2, phase 1)

Dungeons are real structures in the survival world, generated from the seed by vanilla's jigsaw generator. You find
one, walk in, fight through it, and it's still there tomorrow. Cleared dungeons reset slowly (bosses, mob packs,
chests and puzzle doors come back after a few in-game days), so they stay worth visiting.

Everything lives in the `world` module (mod id `skyrpg_world`). The templates and worldgen JSON are generated by
Python scripts in `tools/builds/dungeons/` using `tools/builds/dungeon_kit.py`; the runtime (markers, bosses, puzzles,
waystones) is Java in `world/src/main/java/com/skyrpg/world/`.

**The chain:** each boss can drop a treasure map (vanilla explorer map) to the next dungeon: Crypt to Hollow
(`#skyrpg_world:on_hollow_maps`), Hollow to Temple (`#skyrpg_world:on_temple_maps`). Crypt maps
(`#skyrpg_world:on_crypt_maps`) turn up in Wayshrine chests and village chests.

**Vault keys:** every dungeon has a locked vault room (a `key_door`) with its best chest. The key drops from the
dungeon's elite mob packs (`"elite": true` mobs drop their dungeon's key at 35%) and from the boss (100%), so you
can open the vault before or after the boss.

**Boss tokens** (always drop from the boss, for future crafting): `skyrpg_world:warden_sigil`,
`skyrpg_world:arachne_eye`, `skyrpg_world:tide_pearl`.

**Dungeon gear** (rare boss drops; real items in the items mod, `skyrpg_items:`):

| Dungeon | Gear |
| --- | --- |
| Crypt | `cryptbreaker` (sword, epic), Warden's Shroud set `wardens_shroud_{helmet,chestplate,leggings,boots}` (epic, set bonus) |
| Hollow | `arachne_fang` (sword, epic, poisons), `widowsilk_cloak` (cloak, epic) |
| Temple | `tidecaller_blade` (sword, legendary), `tidemother_pendant` (amulet, legendary) |

**First clear:** the first time a player helps kill a dungeon boss they get its `first_clear` reward (coins and skill
XP) and a "FIRST CLEAR" banner. Later kills only give the loot table.

**Discovery:** walking near a dungeon's entrance marker discovers it: a banner, a sound, and a marker on the map and
minimap (and later in the Explorer's Journal).

## Waystones

`skyrpg_world:waystone` is a block. Right-click it to attune (once per player), then right-click again to open the
waystone menu: every waystone you've attuned plus Skyhaven (the hub). Travelling costs XP levels: 1, plus 1 per
1,000 blocks, up to 5 (free to the hub); a different dimension counts as 3,000 blocks.

### Moving, building and renaming (survival play)

Players can take waystones up, move them, craft new ones and rename them. Code: `Waystones`, `WaystoneBlock`, `Explorer`.

- **Identity.** A waystone has an `id` (UUID string), kept in the server's `waystones` saved data next to its dimension,
  position, name, `owner` (UUID of the placer, "" for generated ones), `placed` and `fixed` flags. A player's attunement
  stores that id (`Explorer.Attuned.id`), so it follows the waystone when it moves. Data from before ids derives the id
  from `dimension|pos` (`legacyId`), which the saved entries get too, so old attunements still match.
- **Breaking.** The block has hardness 8 and needs a pickaxe (`mineable/pickaxe`, `requiresCorrectToolForDrops`): about 2 s with
  iron, 3 s with stone. Bare hands are refused with an action-bar line. Its blast resistance stays huge (explosions leave it
  standing), pistons can't push it, and it is in `wither_immune` / `dragon_immune`.
- **One removal hook.** `WaystoneBlock.affectNeighborsAfterRemoval` calls `Waystones.removed` whenever the block disappears by any
  route (pickaxe, command, other mods). The entry becomes `placed = false` and a Waystone item drops with the id and the name
  (`custom_data.skyrpg_waystone`, `custom_name`, `max_stack_size` 1). The item entity never despawns and can't be damaged. Nothing
  is lost and nothing is copied: the entry only ever has one block or one item. A creative player breaking one deletes it for good
  (`Waystones.forget`).
- **Placing.** `setPlacedBy` -> `Waystones.placed`: an item with an id whose waystone is not standing re-places that waystone
  (the new position, the item's name if it was renamed in an anvil, everyone stays attuned, the placer is attuned); an item
  with no id, or an id that is already standing (a creative copy), makes a brand new waystone owned by the placer with
  an automatic name. A stale entry at the same position (the block went without a trace) is dropped.
- **Fixed ones.** `fixed = 1` for the hub's waystone and any waystone standing inside a dungeon's entrance piece (the structure
  piece of its `dungeon` marker, or within 24 blocks of one for a /place'd dungeon). Swinging at one gives "This waystone
  anchors the dungeon: it can't be taken away." They anchor the dungeon's discovery, map marker and name, and the hub, so they
  stay. The flag is worked out the first time anything touches a waystone and saved (so a wayshrine built near a dungeon
  can still be moved). Wayshrine and village waystones are free.
- **Attunement while carried.** The attuned list keeps the entry (`placed = false`), so nobody loses it; the menu, the map and
  travel skip carried waystones, and the quest objective that counts attuned waystones (`Explorer.state().waystones().size()`)
  keeps working. A player's list is refreshed on join, when they open a menu, and for everyone online when a waystone is
  placed, taken up or renamed.
- **Crafting.** `skyrpg_world/skyrpg/recipes/waystone.json`, Artisan Bench, General tab, Mining 8 (the T2 tier): 8 Stone Bricks,
  16 Glimmer Crystals, 4 Iron Ingots, 1 Ender Pearl. Glimmer is the first custom material and the pearl needs a fight
  or a trade, so a first player-made network arrives around the same time as the first Delver gear, not at hour one.
- **Renaming.** 1 to 24 characters, no control characters or section signs (`Waystones.cleanName`). The placer renames their
  own; a generated one (wayshrine, village, dungeon) can be renamed by anyone attuned; the hub's can't be. Three ways: a
  Rename button in the waystone screen (`WaystoneRenameScreen`, payload `WorldPayloads.Rename`, checked again on the server against
  the waystone the menu is open at, range and permission), an anvil on the carried item, or a named name tag used on the stone
  (consumed). Creative players may rename any.
- **Costs and rules** (XP price, warm-up, combat tag) are unchanged; the price is worked out from the waystone's current position.

They're found in three places:
- **Wayshrines:** a small ruined shrine with a waystone, common across the overworld.
- **Villages:** the first time a village's bell chunk loads, a waystone is placed next to the bell.
- **Dungeons:** every dungeon's entrance piece has one (a `minecraft:air` slot is fine; place the block itself,
  `skyrpg_world:waystone`, in the template).

## Templates and markers

Templates are built with `dungeon_kit.Piece` (see its docstring). Everything that does something at runtime is a
**marker**: an invisible `minecraft:marker` entity saved in the template with tag `skyrpg_dungeon` and a `data`
compound whose `skyrpg` field is its kind. Put markers with `piece.marker(x, y, z, kind, **fields)`.

**Groups** link puzzle parts (levers, plates, mob packs, bosses) to the doors they open. A group name only matters
within one template piece, so a puzzle's parts and its door must be in the same piece, and the same room can be
used twice in one dungeon without the two copies interfering.

| Kind | Fields | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `dungeon` | `dungeon` (dungeon id) | Put one in each dungeon's entrance piece. Players within 20 blocks discover the dungeon. |
| `boss` | `boss` (boss id), `radius` (default 12), `group` (optional) | Spawns the boss at the marker when a player comes within `radius`. Respawns after the reset time. Killing it solves `group`. |
| `spawner` | `mobs` (list, below), `radius` (default 10), `spread` (default 3), `group` (optional) | Spawns the pack once when a player comes within `radius`; again after a reset. When every mob from every spawner of a group is dead, the group is solved ("clear the room to open the gate"). |
| `chest` | `loot` (loot table id) | Put by `piece.chest()` / `piece.barrel()`. Refills the container on reset if it's empty. |
| `door` | `group`, `message` (optional) | Stands in one block of a door (iron bars, a wall, a portcullis...). The door is every block of the same type connected to it (up to 64). When the group is solved the door vanishes with a sound and particles; it closes again on reset. |
| `lever` | `group`, `want` (true/false) | Stands in a lever's block. The group is solved when every lever in it is in its wanted position. Give players a hint (a sign, a text display, a mural). |
| `plate` | `group` | Stands in a pressure plate's block. Stepping on it lights it up for good (until reset); the group is solved when every plate in it has been lit. |
| `key_door` | `key` (item id), `message` (optional) | Like `door`, but it opens when a player right-clicks any of its blocks holding the key (one key is used up). |
| `secret` | `name` | A hidden spot. The first time each player gets within 2.5 blocks: "Secret found: <name>" and a small reward. |

Mob entries (spawner `mobs` and boss `summon` moves):

```json
{"type": "minecraft:skeleton", "count": 3, "level": 12, "elite": false, "name": "Crypt Archer",
 "equipment": {"mainhand": "minecraft:bow", "head": "minecraft:chainmail_helmet"}}
```

`type` can be any entity type, vanilla or ours (`skyrpg_combat:glimmer_crawler`, `tide_lurker`, `gravecaller`,
`hollow_wisp`, `bog_hag`, `frost_revenant`, `ember_imp`, `grove_sentinel`). `level` sets the mob's SkyRPG level
(health and damage scale with it, as for world mobs). Elites are tougher, glow, and drop their dungeon's key (35%).
Dungeon mobs never despawn.

Other things a template can hold directly: loot chests (vanilla loot tables), signs (`piece.sign`), and any entity via
`piece.entity()` (a `minecraft:text_display` for floating hints, armour stands, item frames).

## Bosses

`data/skyrpg_world/skyrpg/dungeon_bosses/<id>.json`:

```json
{
  "name": "The Crypt Warden",
  "entity": "minecraft:wither_skeleton",
  "dungeon": "skyrpg_world:forgotten_crypt",
  "level": 18,
  "health": 3000,
  "damage": 14,
  "speed": 0.28,
  "scale": 1.5,
  "knockback_resistance": 0.8,
  "bar_color": "purple",
  "equipment": {"mainhand": "minecraft:netherite_sword", "head": "minecraft:wither_skeleton_skull"},
  "intro": "Who disturbs the Warden's rest?",
  "death": "The Warden crumbles to dust.",
  "max_minions": 6,
  "loot": "skyrpg_world:bosses/crypt_warden",
  "first_clear": {"coins": 5000, "xp": {"skyrpg_skills:combat": 1500}},
  "reset_ticks": 72000,
  "moves": [
    {"type": "slam", "every": 220, "radius": 5, "damage": 12, "warn": "The Warden raises his blade!"},
    {"type": "summon", "every": 400, "mobs": [{"type": "minecraft:skeleton", "count": 3, "level": 14}]},
    {"type": "enrage", "below": 0.4, "speed": 0.3, "damage": 0.3, "warn": "The Warden's eyes burn!"}
  ]
}
```

`health` is the HP players see (big numbers are fine; it's done with SkyRPG toughness). `damage` is the base
melee damage. `reset_ticks` defaults to 72,000 (three in-game days).

Every move has `type`, `every` (ticks between uses, default 200), `delay` (ticks before the first use, default
`every / 2`), `below` (only when the boss is under this fraction of its health, default 1), `warn` (action-bar
warning shown to nearby players when it starts), and optional `particle` and `sound` overrides. The types:

| Type | Fields | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `slam` | `radius` 5, `damage` 10, `knockup` 0.8, `windup` 20 | A ring of particles marks the area, then damage and a launch for everyone inside. |
| `summon` | `mobs` (mob entries), `max` | Minions appear around the boss (up to `max_minions` alive). |
| `leap` | `damage` 10, `radius` 3, `range` 16 | Jumps at a player and hits everyone near the landing spot. |
| `volley` | `projectile` (`arrow`, `fireball`, `web`, `trident`, `snowball`), `count` 5, `damage` 6, `spread` 0.15 | Fires at the target. `web` shots leave a cobweb where they land (cleared after a few seconds). |
| `pull` | `radius` 12, `strength` 1.0 | Drags every player in range towards the boss. |
| `shockwave` | `radius` 8, `damage` 8, `push` 1.5 | Knocks everyone in range away. |
| `blink` | none | Teleports behind its target. |
| `webs` | `radius` 4, `count` 8, `duration` 100 | Temporary cobwebs around each nearby player. |
| `molten` | `radius` 4, `count` 10, `delay` 25, `duration` 100, `damage` 10 | The floor around each nearby player glows, then turns to magma for `duration` ticks; standing on it burns for `damage` every half second. Only plain solid blocks melt, and they come back. |
| `cloud` | `effect`, `amplifier` 0, `duration` 100, `radius` 3.5, `on` (`target` or `self`) | A lingering effect cloud. |
| `enrage` | `speed` 0.3, `damage` 0.3 | Once: faster and stronger (use with `below`). |
| `heal` | `percent` 0.05 | Heals a share of its max health. |
| `ward` | `duration` 160, `reduction` 0.9 | Takes `reduction` less damage for a while (shown with particles and on the boss bar). |

## Building and testing

- `python tools/builds/dungeons/<name>.py` regenerates one dungeon (templates, pools, structure, loot, bosses).
- In game (op): `/place structure skyrpg_world:forgotten_crypt` builds one where you stand; `/locate structure

  `/dungeon info` lists its markers.


## v2: bigger dungeons, real rewards (2026-10-12)

Playtest verdict on v1: too small, rewards too weak. v2 changes:

**Size.** Every dungeon has **two floors** joined by a descending stairwell piece, and a jigsaw `size` of 14-18 with
`max_distance` 116. Floor 1: entrance, 3-4 spine halls, a **champion arena**, the stairwell. Floor 2: 3-4 spine halls,
then the boss. Side branches off every spine hall lead to 12-20 more rooms. Grand halls are 19-29 blocks wide; boss
arenas are about 31x15x31.

**Encounters.** Mob packs have 4-10 mobs, and many come in `waves`. Each floor has traps, and floor 1 has a champion
(a mini-boss with a bar).

**Rewards.**
- **Every chest is per player.** Each player rolls their own loot from every dungeon chest (Lootr-style), plus coins
  and Delving XP. Both refill when the dungeon resets.
- **Bosses pay every kill:** `coins` and `delving_xp` to each participant, plus the loot table, plus a first-clear bonus.
- **The boss leaves a Reward Chest** (per player) rolled from `reward_loot`. It rolls 1 + bonus times, where the bonus
  comes from what the party did this run: packs cleared, secrets found, champions slain.
- **Delving** is a new skill (`skyrpg_skills:delving`). XP comes from dungeon chests, secrets, champions, bosses and
  discoveries.
- **Dungeon gear is craftable** at the Artisan Bench from boss tokens (no RNG wall). Boss tokens drop 2-4 per kill.
- **Enchant tomes** drop from dungeon bosses and champions (`tome_drops`, matched by the boss's tag
  `skyrpg_boss=<boss id>`).

### New and changed marker fields

| Kind | Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chest` | `tier` | `common`, `rare`, `secret`, `vault`, `reward` or `wayshrine`. Coins and Delving XP scale with tier and dungeon level. `piece.chest(..., tier=...)` sets it. |
| `spawner` | `waves` | A list of mob lists. Each wave spawns when the one before is dead (or use `mobs` for one wave). |
| `trap` (new) | `type`, `radius` (4), `every` (40 ticks), `damage` (4) | `arrows`: shoots at the nearest player in range with line of sight (put the marker in a wall slot or behind a grate). `spikes`: hurts players standing within the radius (marks it with crit particles). `flames`: a fire burst that ignites players in range. `poison`: a short poison cloud. Traps work while a player is in range. |

### New boss fields

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `tier` | `boss` (default) or `champion`. A champion is a mini-boss: smaller rewards, no reward chest. Its first kill still counts for the journal. |
| `coins` | `[min, max]` coins to every participant on every kill. |
| `delving_xp` | Delving XP to every participant. |
| `reward_loot` | Loot table for the Reward Chest a boss leaves behind. |
| `health_per_player` | Extra health per extra player in the room when it wakes (default 0.6, so 2 players means +60%). |
| `phases` | `[{"below": 0.66, "message": "The Warden calls the dead!"}]`, announced once each with a burst of effects. Moves can use `below` to unlock with them. |

## The Dwarven Halls and the Abandoned Mine Camp (the Deep, 2026-10-13)

`python tools/builds/dungeons/dwarven_halls.py` and `mine_camp.py` (design: `docs/design/the_deep.md`). The halls are a v2
dungeon built like the Drowned Temple (built rooms in a deepslate, polished blackstone and oxidised copper palette, dwarven
runestones for glowing runes), with the Spider Hollow's surface entrance.

**Layout** (jigsaw size 16, max_distance 116, rare: spacing 40, separation 14, 8 chunks from Spider Hollows):
- **Surface:** the *entrance* piece (25 x 15 x 25, the start piece) holds the ruined **Dwarven Gate** (a carved deepslate
  doorway in a hillside, braziers, a collapsed statue, a waystone and the `dungeon` marker) and the top of a long square-spiral
  stairwell. It projects to `WORLD_SURFACE_WG` with `start_height` 0: a start piece's local y=1 sits at the first free height, so
  its y=0 layer is the top ground block (a ground-level plaza, and beard_thin works where the terrain is). Under it the
  *stairwell* piece (25 x 76 x 25) is stacked on the same footprint and joined by a vertical jigsaw (`down_north` /
  `up_north`, both `aligned`); it holds the rest of the 76-block spiral with landings (packs, chests, arrow traps) and exits
  through an east door at the bottom. (One 90-block piece would adapt to terrain 76 blocks down: a template's ground level is its
  local y=1.)
- **Floor 1, Upper Halls** (Lv 32-36): three spine halls (19 deep; great hall, barracks, rail station, forge hall), the
  **Forgeguard's arena** (champion, bar plug until it falls), then the *descent* (a switchback stairwell, 20 blocks down, leaves by
  its east wall: a right turn). 9 side rooms (Duskquartz vault, library with a hidden alcove, collapsed tunnel, armory, mess
  hall, forge room, cart depot, rune shrine, plate-puzzle vault) and 4 leaves (closet, miner's nook with a cache, guard post,
  ore pocket).
- **Floor 2, The Deep Forge** (Lv 36-41): three spine halls (forge halls, Duskquartz galleries, great halls, rail station,
  barracks), then **the Deep Forge** (31 x 15 x 31, the Iron Thane, a key-door vault and a hoard nook that opens when he dies).
  8 side rooms (deep vault, forge works, lava cistern, king's tomb, foundry, ore gallery, rail junction, rune archive with a
  lever door). Side rooms only open on the side facing the entrance; the far side only gets leaves.
- **Reach rules** (they bit us before): spines are straight and never contain corner pieces; axis A (floor 1) reaches 108 and
  axis B (floor 2) 100 from the entrance centre; the 90 degree turn is in the descent. The script prints the reach table.

**Boss and champion** (`skyrpg/dungeon_bosses/`): `dwarven_champion` "The Forgeguard" (`skyrpg_combat:dwarven_construct`,
champion, scale 1.7, Lv 38, 6,500 HP, drops `thane_key`: slam, shockwave, summon, ward below 50%) and `iron_thane` "The Iron Thane"
(`skyrpg_combat:iron_thane`, Lv 42, 24,000 HP; phases at 66% and 33%). Phase 1: slam, leap, shockwave, ward. Phase 2 adds
Forgeguard and Lost Miner summons, a pull and a heal. Phase 3 adds slag volleys (fireballs), wither clouds under the target
(standing in for a molten floor) and a faster slam, then enrage. Trophy `thanes_rune` (1 guaranteed, a second 25% of the time).
Tomes: `skyrpg/tome_drops/iron_thane.json` and `dwarven_champion.json`.

**Loot:** `chests/dwarven_{common,rare,secret,vault,cart}`, `bosses/{dwarven_champion,iron_thane}`, `rewards/iron_thane`:
Duskquartz shards, Emberite, Skyiron, Glimmer, Dwarven Cogs, Rift Embers, Glimmer Geodes, enchanted books. Ore walls
(`skyrpg_mining:deepslate_duskquartz_ore`) can be mined.

**The Abandoned Mine Camp** (`skyrpg_world:mine_camp`, common: spacing 12, separation 4, `underground_structures`, no heightmap
projection, `start_height` uniform 4..30): one 27 x 11 x 27 template with its own cave room (a dome with a stone shell, so it
never floats in a cave or sits as a bare box in rock), a tent, a campfire, a rail line with a chest minecart, a crate pile,
lanterns, ore veins, a per-player loot chest and a loot barrel, and two packs of Lost Miners (spawner markers work without a
dungeon). The chest has a 15% treasure map (`#skyrpg_world:on_dwarven_maps`) to the Dwarven Halls. Its rail tunnels open into
whatever cave is there or dead-end in rock.

**Offline checks** (run on every build of the halls): jigsaw validation, every block id exists in the game's blockstates, every
doorway and marker is reachable (a walk test, with `door` and `key_door` markers opened), pack sizes 4-10, loot item ids, the
reach table and a Monte-Carlo piece count per floor. `--show <piece> <y>` prints a top-down slice of a piece.

Test: `/place structure skyrpg_world:dwarven_halls` (stand on the ground; the gate is built on you and the halls run under
it, 100+ blocks each way), `/place structure skyrpg_world:mine_camp` (it lands at a random height of 4..30 under where you stand and carves its own room),
`/locate structure skyrpg_world:dwarven_halls`, `/dungeon info`, `/dungeon spawnboss skyrpg_world:iron_thane`.

## The Infernal Citadel (the Nether, 2026-10-13)

`python tools/builds/dungeons/infernal_citadel.py` (design: `docs/design/nether.md`). A v2 dungeon built like the Dwarven Halls, in
the Nether: a blackstone fortress (polished blackstone bricks, nether bricks, gilded blackstone, magma, crimson and warped
accents, black and red banners, soul-fire braziers, chains) on stilts over the lava sea or built into a cliff. Lava falls sit
behind iron bars (lava can't flow into bars, and every other side of each fall is stone).

**Placement:** `skyrpg_world:infernal_citadel`, biomes `#skyrpg_world:has_structure/infernal_citadel` = `nether_wastes`, `basalt_deltas`,
`soul_sand_valley` plus `skyrpg_world:ember_forest`, `bone_sea` and `obsidian_spires` (those three are `"required": false`, so the
tag still loads without them). Rare: `random_spread` spacing 44, separation 16, salt 26101318, kept 8 chunks from the vanilla fortress
and bastion set (`minecraft:nether_complexes`). `step` `underground_structures`, `start_height` absolute 62 (the start piece's local
y=1 sits at y=62), no heightmap projection and `terrain_adaptation` `none`. Every piece has its floor at local y=4 and pilings
below it, so floor 1 is around y=65 and floor 2 around y=45, above the lava sea (y=31).

**Layout** (jigsaw size 16, max_distance 116; every doorway 3x3 at local y=5; spine pools never contain corner pieces):
- **Gatehouse** (start piece, 37 x 32 x 29): a forecourt on stilts with a waystone and the `dungeon` marker, a portcullis gate between two
  banner towers, and the gate hall behind it (a causeway between two lava moats, columns, lava falls, arrow slits). The spine leaves
  by its north wall.
- **Floor 1, The Burning Bastion** (Lv 44-48), axis A: three spine stages of 17-deep halls (muster halls, barracks, war forges, lava
  bridges, a prison block, the chapel of flame), the **Knight's Hall** (champion arena, door plug of bars), then the *descent* (a
  switchback stair carved in basalt and blackstone, 20 down, leaving by its east wall: the 90 degree turn). 8 side rooms (armoury vault
  with a plate door, cell block, flame shrine, war room with a hidden strongbox, mess hall, guard post, lava overlook, forge annex)
  and 4 leaves (closet, smuggler's nook with a cache, guard post, vent).
- **Floor 2, The Molten Throne** (Lv 48-52), axis B, 20 lower: three spine stages (lava bridges, magma foundries, obsidian
  colonnades, imp warrens, ember chapel), then **the Molten Throne** (35 x 23 x 35: a plain solid arena floor, a magma-heart lava fall behind the
  stepped throne, lava falls down both walls, a key-door vault (`citadel_key`) and a hoard nook that opens when the Tyrant dies). 9 side rooms (armoury vault, imp den,
  magma cistern, obsidian crypt with a cache, war room, flame shrine, forge annex, soul well, lava overlook); side rooms only open on
  the side facing the entrance, the far side only gets leaves.
- **Expected pieces** (Monte-Carlo of the jigsaw tree, collisions ignored): about 1 gatehouse, 16 on floor 1 (3 spine halls, champion arena,
  descent and about 11 side rooms and leaves) and 13 on floor 2 (3 spine halls, the throne and about 9 side rooms and leaves).
- **Reach** (the script prints the table): axis A reaches 106 (107.5 with floor 2's widest piece), axis B 90, both under the 110 limit.

**Champion and boss** (`skyrpg/dungeon_bosses/`):
- `infernal_knight` "The Infernal Knight": `minecraft:wither_skeleton`, champion, scale 2.2, Lv 50, 14,000 HP, netherite sword and armour from
  `equipment`. Moves: slam, leap, fireball volley, a ward below 50%, summon Citadel Guards. Drops `citadel_key`.
- `magma_tyrant` "The Magma Tyrant": `skyrpg_combat:magma_tyrant`, scale 1.0, Lv 52, 36,000 HP, damage 44. Phase 1: magma slam, fireball
  volleys, leap. Phase 2 (below 66%): summons magma cubes and Ember Imps, a pull, a heal (below 50%). Phase 3 (below 33%): the
  `molten` floor, a shockwave, a faster slam, enrage. Trophy `tyrants_core` (1 guaranteed, a second 25% of the time), a Reward
  Chest (`rewards/magma_tyrant`), first-clear rewards, tomes in `skyrpg/tome_drops/`.

**Loot:** `chests/citadel_{common,rare,secret,vault}`, `bosses/{infernal_knight,magma_tyrant}`, `rewards/magma_tyrant`: gold, blaze rods,
magma cream, netherite scrap (rare), Emberite, Rift Embers, Dread Crystals, Hellforged Scrap and Ingots (`skyrpg_mining:hellforged_*`),
enchanted books, Wolf Pup and Mole eggs, rarely a Golden Pet Egg.

Test: `/locate structure skyrpg_world:infernal_citadel` (in the Nether), `/place structure skyrpg_world:infernal_citadel` (stand in open air
around y=60: the gatehouse is placed at y=62), `/dungeon spawnboss skyrpg_world:magma_tyrant`.

## The Astral Observatory (the End, 2026-10-13)

trim, glass roofs and windows, star-map murals (blue concrete with white and sea lantern stars), cartography tables and lecterns, bookshelves, brass
telescopes on purpur tripods, end rods and sea lanterns on chains, purple and light blue banners.

## The Ruined Citadel and the Catacombs (the end game, 2026-10-13)

Two more v2 dungeons, designed in `docs/design/end_game.md`. Both are Python scripts on `dungeon_kit.py` plus the shared helpers
in `tools/builds/dungeons/endgame_kit.py` (Room, rock-lined descents, loot helpers and every offline check, including a
rotation-exact Monte-Carlo of the jigsaw tree that prints the **reach table**). Run `python tools/builds/dungeons/ruined_citadel.py` or
`catacombs.py` (add `--show <piece path> <y>` for a slice). Every check runs on each build: jigsaw validation, block and item ids,
a walk test (every doorway and marker reachable, `door` and `key_door` markers opened), pack sizes, spine rules, reach <= 110.

### The Ruined Citadel (`skyrpg_world:ruined_citadel`, Lv 54-58)

**Placement:** biomes `#skyrpg_world:has_structure/ruined_citadel` = `frozen_peaks`, `jagged_peaks`, `stony_peaks`, `snowy_slopes`, `grove`,
`windswept_hills`, `windswept_gravelly_hills`. Rare: `random_spread` spacing 56, separation 22, salt 26111205, kept 10 chunks from the Dwarven
Halls. `step` `underground_structures`, start piece projected to `WORLD_SURFACE_WG`, `start_height` 0, `terrain_adaptation` `beard_thin`.

**Layout** (size 16, max_distance 116; doorways 3x3 at y=1; stacked vertically so it stays inside the reach limit):
- **Gatehouse** (start piece 25 x 16 x 25): two broken towers and a gate arch built into the slope, a plaza with banners on poles, a fallen statue, a
  waystone and the `dungeon` marker; a tunnel into the **stairwell** (25 x 20 x 25, a vertical jigsaw below it) with two landings (packs, chests, an arrow trap).
- **Floor 1, the Outer Ward** (Lv 54-56): two spine stages of 19-deep halls (sunken courtyards, barracks, stables with skeleton horses, mess halls), 5
  side rooms (armoury behind three plates, kitchen, storeroom with a cache, guardroom, cellar well) and 3 leaves, the **Knight-Captain's Hall** (bar plug), then
  descent 1 (16 down, a 90 degree turn, carved in rock).
- **Floor 2, the Keep** (Lv 56-57): two spine stages (libraries, great halls, chapels, a statue gallery), 6 side rooms (scriptorium, treasury behind three levers,
  solar, cells, shrine, armoury) and 3 leaves; side rooms only on the side that faces the entrance. Descent 2 (16 down).
- **Floor 3, the Throne Room** (Lv 57-58): the **Hall of Banners** (a portcullis shut until three levers are in the order raised-lowered-raised, a pack waiting
  on each side) and the **Throne Room** (31 x 21 x 31, plain blackstone floor for the `molten` move; a `key_door` vault for the `citadel_crest_key` with two vault
  chests, and a hoard nook behind a door that opens when the Fallen Knight dies).
- Mobs: armoured zombies (Citadel Knights, iron then diamond), armoured skeletons (archers, Skeleton Knights with diamond swords), vindicators and
  pillagers as castle guards. (No mounted knights: a spawner can't seat a rider, so the stables only hold idle skeleton horses.)

**Champion and boss** (`skyrpg/dungeon_bosses/`):
- `knight_captain` "The Knight-Captain": `minecraft:zombie`, champion, scale 1.8, Lv 57, 16,000 HP, diamond sword and armour. Slam, leap, arrow volley,
  a ward below 50%, summons knights. Drops `citadel_crest_key`.
- `fallen_knight` "The Fallen Knight": `minecraft:wither_skeleton`, scale 2.6, Lv 58, 48,000 HP, damage 54, netherite gear and a black banner in the offhand.
  Phase 1: slam, leap, shadow-fire volley. Phase 2 (below 66%): summons Fallen Guards and Skeleton Knights, blink, a wither `cloud`, heal and ward below 50%.
  Phase 3 (below 33%): pull, the `molten` floor, shockwave, a faster slam, enrage. Trophy `fallen_crest` (1 guaranteed, a second 25%), a Reward Chest.

### The Catacombs (`skyrpg_world:catacombs`, Lv 59-62)

**Placement:** biomes `#minecraft:is_overworld` (in the `has_structure/catacombs` tag). Very rare: spacing 64, separation 26, salt 26111206. `step`
`underground_structures`, no heightmap projection, `terrain_adaptation` `none`, `start_height` absolute -58 (the start piece's local y=1 sits at y=-58, so the
labyrinth floor is y=-59 and the whole structure lies in y -59..-36, the Magma Rifts layer). There is no surface part: players find it with `/locate`, digging
into the **sealed stair**.

**Layout** (size 16; every piece's floor is local y=0):
- **Entrance** (start piece 25 x 24 x 25): solid rock around a square-spiral stair of 18 blocks; the top is a small sealed bone chamber at about y=-41 (a sign, candles,
  skulls, a chest, the `dungeon` marker, a cracked wall to dig through), two landings with packs, an arrow trap, the exit south.
- **Spine** (each stage 21 deep): `s1` -> `s2` -> `tomb/gate` -> `tomb/crypt`. Stages choose between **mazes** (6 x 5 cells, loops, dead-end chests and
  packs, traps, a different seed each) and halls: a crypt hall, an ossuary hall, a **lever hall** and a **plate hall** (the latter two shut the spine with a grate until
  the puzzle is solved). Every spine piece has a west and an east door at its middle row.
- **Bone Wardens:** `s1` west, `s1` east and `s2` west lead to the `wardens` pool, whose only piece is a Bone Warden's chamber (a champion; each drops exactly one
  `ossuary_key`). `s2` east leads to the side rooms (ossuary, bone pit, sarcophagi, trap gallery, shrine, lever vault, plate vault, charnel) and corridors. Warden
  chambers and side rooms continue into **lanes**: crossroads, tees and straights cut from solid stone, ending in leaves (urns, a cache, a grave).
  Elites also drop Ossuary Keys (35%), and a rare chest has a 5% spare key, so a collision that loses a warden can't lock the tomb.
- **The inner tomb:** `key_door` uses up one key per click, so it can't count to three; `tomb/gate` is a hall of **three bone doors in a row**, each a `key_door`
  that takes one Ossuary Key. Behind them, the **Bonelord's Crypt** (31 x 18 x 31, plain floor for `molten`, bone arches, a throne, a hoard alcove behind a door that
  opens when he dies).

**Bosses:**
- `bone_warden` "A Bone Warden" (x3): `skyrpg_combat:bone_giant`, champion, scale 1.3, Lv 60, 15,000 HP. Slam, leap, wither cloud, ward, summons Bone Soldiers. Drops an `ossuary_key`.
- `bonelord` "The Bonelord": `skyrpg_combat:bone_giant`, scale 2.0, Lv 62, 60,000 HP, damage 70, a golden helmet for the crown. Phase 1: slam, rib volley (arrows),
  leap, wither `cloud`. Phase 2 (below 66%): summons Tomb Wraiths and Crypt Archers, a pull, heal and ward below 50%. Phase 3 (below 33%): the `molten` floor,
  shockwave, a faster slam, enrage. Trophy `bonelord_phylactery` (1 guaranteed, a second 25%), a Reward Chest.

**Loot** (both): `chests/{ruined_citadel,catacombs}_{common,rare,secret,vault}`, `bosses/*`, `rewards/{fallen_knight,bonelord}`: the best materials so far
(Hellforged Ingots, Duskquartz Shards, Lumenite Crystals and Attuned Lumenite, Prism Fragments and Prisms, Heartstones, Dread Crystals, Marrow Shards in the
Catacombs), enchanted books, valor seals, elite trophies, Nightmare Cores, Wolf Pup and Mole eggs, rarely a Golden Pet Egg; tome drops in
`skyrpg/tome_drops/{fallen_knight,knight_captain,bonelord,bone_warden}.json`. Every chest is per-player with the usual coins and Delving XP by tier.

**New items** (`skyrpg/items/*.json`, to register in `WorldContent`): `citadel_crest_key`, `fallen_crest`, `ossuary_key`, `bonelord_phylactery`.
