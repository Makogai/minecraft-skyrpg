# Danger regions (Adventure Plan v2, phase 2)

Big patches of the overworld where the land itself is corrupted and the monsters are far stronger. They show **red on
the map and minimap**, with the region's name at its heart. Going in is how you get the best materials; staying out
is how you stay alive.

Everything lives in the `world` module: `DangerRegions` (placement, levels, spawns, drops, titles, sync),
`DangerRegionFeature` (the land), `HeartShrine` (the shrine at the centre), `RegionBosses` (the hearts' bosses and Heart
Sigils), `RegionShape` (the blob, shared with the client) and the client's `RegionMap` and `Atmosphere` (fog). Data is
generated by `tools/builds/regions.py` and `tools/builds/wild.py`.

## Where they are

- The overworld is cut into 768-block cells. Each cell has a 24% chance to hold one region, decided by the world seed.
  That gives roughly one region every 1,600 blocks.
- Placement: the region's centre lands in the middle half of its cell, with a radius of 150-230. The edge wobbles with
  three sine waves, so every region is a different blob.
- The **type depends on the biome at the centre**. A cell whose biome no type wants (oceans, dripstone caves and the like) has
  no region. Mountains do: stony, jagged and frozen peaks and the windswept hills belong to the Shattered Highlands.
- Nothing within 450 blocks of world spawn.
- The server can say which region covers any spot, loaded or not, from world generation threads too. The client
  never needs the seed: the server sends the regions around the player.

## Hearts and fog (The Wild)

- **The heart:** a shrine at every region's centre, built with the land (new chunks only): a round platform of the type's own
  blocks, a ruined low wall, four pillars with lights, and an altar with the heart block. Each type has a boss (Blight Lord,
  Ash Colossus, Frost Revenant King, Bog Mother, Lantern Wraith, Storm Griffin; table in `the_wild.md`). Wake it with a Heart
  Sigil (32 of the type's essence and 4 Blood Shards, Artisan Bench "region"), used within 24 blocks of the altar, or wait for
  a Blood Moon: each heart wakes once per moon when a player comes within 64 blocks. Several regions can have a boss up at
  once.
- **Blood Moon:** inside a region the moon's horde is the region's own monsters, twice as often with twice the `max_mobs`; a
  title names the boss and a waypoint points at the heart.
- **Fog:** each type has a `fog` colour and a `fog_density` (0 to 1). Inside, the client blends the fog towards the colour
  and pulls it in by the density, fading over the last 30 blocks of the edge. Moors grey (0.55), Mire green (0.4), Ashen Wastes
  ash grey (0.35), Blightlands dark red and Frostgrave white (0.3), Highlands pale blue (0.25).

## What happens there

- **Land:** chunks generated inside a region are corrupted by `DangerRegionFeature`. Every block from 40 above the
  ground to 4 below goes through the type's palette, so the trees change too. The edge frays over 12 blocks, and the
  type's decorations are scattered. Chunks that existed before the mod keep their look, but the danger still applies.
- **Monsters:** every monster spawning inside gets `level_bonus` extra levels, through core's `MobLevelling` bonus
  hook. Natural spawns count, and so do the region's own.
- **Region spawns:** every 3 seconds, near each survival player inside, the region spawns a group from its `mobs`
  list 18-36 blocks away, up to `max_mobs` nearby. They aren't persistent.
- **Drops:** monsters killed by a player inside roll the region's `drops`.
- **Arrival:** entering a region shows a red title with its danger and level bonus. Ambient particles drift around
  players inside (crimson spores, ash, snowflakes, spores).
- **Map:** the map gets a red wash, stronger along the border, and a marker at the centre that shows the danger
  on hover.
- **Gear:** each region has a weapon and an accessory crafted from its essence and a Dread Crystal at the Artisan
  Bench (category "region").
- `/region` tells anyone which regions are near; `/region list` (ops) looks 3,000 blocks out; `/region nearest <type>` (ops)
  finds the closest of a type within 30,000 blocks.

## Region type format

`data/<ns>/skyrpg/danger_regions/<id>.json`, written by `tools/builds/regions.py` (its docstring lists every field):
`name`, `color`, `biomes`, `weight`, `level_bonus`, `danger`, `description`, `palette` (block id or #tag to block id;
properties carry over), `decorations` (scatter / spike / patch / headstone / grave / islet / ore), `mobs` (dungeon mob
entries plus `weight` and `count`; also `tags`, `speed`, `fireproof`, `rise`), `max_mobs`, `drops`, `particle`,
`night_particle`, and for the hearts `boss` (a `dungeon_bosses` id), `sigil` (item id), `fog`, `fog_density` and `shrine`
(`floor`, `accent`, `wall`, `pillar`, `light`, `heart` blocks).
